# wild mine

New mode, separate from the classic chamber. P1 = platform, P2 = active ball.
No co-op/versus split, no win condition: players use it as they want.

## players

- P1 platform: existing machine; piston tilt ±90° from horizontal (classic ±10°).
- P2 ball: new player kind (not a platform), player-controlled.

## Ball

- Ball: 10% loss per bounce; left/right motor; jump adds energy.
- Ball rolling keeps impulse and moment of inertia; a jump preserves both.
  Smooth cave curves roll; a sharp curvature change is a wall-like hit. Compute
  damage from the contact force.
- Ball damages every rock surface, not only the ceiling underside.
- Ball energy source: ball battery (open: platform supply? atomic?).

## Map

- Infinite seeded rock, 512 cells in y (~64 m at 0.125 m/cell).
- No global floor or side walls: cave surfaces are the floor. Platform needs a
  cave span ≥ 3.6 m.
- Start cave: 25% of the initial view height and width.
- Vertical scale is a log function of the active ball’s y (current 0.25× far
  edge is the reference); reuse the classic cell size and view scale.
	Camera average smooth follow player.

## Cave representation

- The current one-heightmap-per-column model cannot represent cave side walls,
  overhangs, or debris on ledges. Decide: 2D solid occupancy, signed distance
  field, or per-cave heightmaps. It must support arbitrary boundaries,
  collision, debris resting, and row streaming.
- Debris can rest on any solid surface, not only a global floor; use the same
  surface query as collision.
- Read `gas-caves.1-plan.md` first: void vs mined-out rock, stable roof,
  occupancy/collision.

## Debris

- Debris is indestructible and remains debris as in whole game.
- Damped impact: debris absorbs most of a hit; rock below takes weak damage,
  spread over 4× the normal damage radius.
- Setting (available in all game modes): debris Y compression 1..8, scaling the existing debris heightmap
  (`floorCellHeight = cell/4` today; 4 = current 4:1 tile). Wild mine default
  1.5; applies to collision and rendering, and the energy ledger stays exact.

## Economy

- Auto-cart: call a cart to the platform to sell cargo (wild mine has no selling
  walls); it buys the load and returns. Reuses the market/ledger. if cargo full - call cart automatically.
