# 0.3.6 — Water gun & fluid system (design draft)

## Relationship to the sandblaster and cave plans

The sandblaster (`0.3.6-Sandblaster-plan.md`, `js/sandblaster.js`) establishes
the per-owner tool pattern in this codebase: a `profiles` Map keyed by owner
id, fixed preallocated typed-array projectile pools, hold-to-fire and
`autoFire` standing orders, shared `SteamCeiling.chip` weakening, and exact
energy and material conservation. Note that the sandblaster feeds on hopper
sand and stone with no wall-bought magazine; the water gun follows the same
magazine-free discipline with its own supply rule below.

Water must also stay compatible with the upcoming 2D cave plans
(`gas-caves.1-plan.md` and `0.4.0-wild-mine-draft.md`):

- Classic chamber currently models rock as a ceiling heightmap (`firstRow`)
  above a 1D rubble floor (`floorPiles`).
- `gas-caves.1` introduces true 2D solid occupancy (`void`, `intact`,
  `damaged`, `removed`) with interior ceilings, side walls, and arches.
- `0.4.0-wild-mine` removes the global floor and side walls altogether in
  favor of infinite seeded 2D caves (`512` cells tall at `0.125 m/cell`),
  overhangs, ledges, ±90° platform tilt, and debris resting on any solid
  surface.

A 1D floor-heightmap water layer cannot pool on upper cave ledges, cascade
over overhangs, or equalize under submerged arches. Water should therefore be
designed from the start as a **2D free-surface fluid** over a shared 2D
solid-occupancy / surface-normal interface so one simulation serves both the
classic chamber and caves without a rewrite.

## Core gameplay decisions

- **Unlimited tank (initial version).** The water gun has an unlimited water
  supply in v1 — no wall purchases, no finite onboard water capacity, and no
  ammo magazine. Firing draws platform reserve energy to pressurize and
  project the water stream; the gun freely floods water into the chamber or
  cave. (A finite tank, water recycling loop, or tank-capacity upgrades remain
  optional later balance layers and are intentionally deferred.)
- **Under-platform suction pump.** The platform retrieves water back simply by
  sucking directly **underneath its hull** (the intake span under the chassis
  and wheelbase, reaching down into pooled water below the belly). Pumping
  draws water out of the chamber/cave and collects any dissolved or suspended
  mineral slurry carried by that water into the platform's hopper.
  - When a platform's hopper is full, suction either pauses slurry intake
    (leaving dissolved sediment in the remaining water) or pauses the pump
    entirely; never silently discard mineral mass or value.
- **Aimed nozzle, manual first.** Unlike the straight-up sandblaster, the
  nozzle aims toward the cursor (clamped to valid nozzle arcs above the deck)
  and inherits the platform's velocity at launch. Hold-to-fire projects a
  continuous stream; release stops. Pointer-capture handling avoids setting
  travel targets on fire clicks. Bot operation is a follow-up.
- **Per-owner state.** Every platform owns its own water gun profile (aim,
  fire rate, auto-fire standing order, pump toggle, counters) in a `profiles`
  Map keyed by owner id, matching `js/sandblaster.js` and `js/rockets.js`.
- **Low-damage dissolution & slurry transport.** The water jet weakens and
  dissolves impacted rock at a lower per-hit rate than the sandblaster (or
  soaks a slightly wider surface zone via `SteamCeiling.chip` / a dedicated
  dissolution rule). Dissolved rock mass and its exact mineral value enter the
  water as suspended slurry, flow downhill and across basins with the fluid,
  and are recovered into cargo when sucked up under a platform (or settle onto
  floor/ledge rubble when water drains or stagnates).

## Fluid architecture: particle vs. cell vs. hybrid

An interactive standalone prototype (`prototypes/water-lab.html`,
`prototypes/water-lab.js`) compares three real-time 2D architectures at exact
game scale (`24 × 15 m`, `192 × 120` rock at `0.125 m/cell`, `96 × 60` fluid
grid at `0.25 m/cell`, `3.6 m` wheeled platform, unlimited tank, under-platform
suction, rock dissolution, and four scenes: Classic Chamber, Caves & Ledges,
Wild Mine U-Tube, and Dam Breach):

| Architecture | Visible surface & smooth flow | Unlimited tank & 2D caves | Conservation, streaming & measured cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hybrid: Ballistic Jet/Cascade + 2D Pressure/Wave Cells** *(recommended)* | Crisp aimed jet stream; `C⁰`-continuous sub-cell Marching Squares surface with luminous meniscus; horizontal staggered wave momentum (`edgeU`) for fast spreading & surges; overhang-to-droplet re-emission for parabolic waterfalls. | **Unlimited flooded volume** at constant fixed memory (`5,760` cells at `0.25 m`); handles overhangs, ledges, and submerged U-tube siphons natively with zero particle cap. | Exact per-flux water & slurry conservation (`drift ≈ 0`); row streaming is a flat typed-array shift; trivial save/load. **0.40–1.12 ms/step**, **0.8–1.7 ms draw**. |
| **Particles: 2D SPH / DDR** *(Clavet double-density relaxation)* | Unified jet, splash, slosh and pool; organic cohesion and spray rendered through a density-field Marching Squares surface. | Fixed particle cap (`~1,800` pts ≈ `30 m²`) clashes with unlimited flooding; calm pools stay slightly bumpy/compressible; large caves spread particles thin. | Exact per-particle slurry; requires a sleeping-cell layer to scale across 512-row Wild Mine caves. **0.62–1.82 ms/step**. |
| **Grid + Particle: 2D FLIP / PIC** *(staggered MAC grid)* | Best dynamic wave, breaking-waterfall, and platform bow-wake realism; incompressible flat pools with zero bounce (airborne jet particles skip MAC projection to preserve arc speed). | Handles arbitrary 2D caves via MAC solid mask, but still particle-capped (`~2,400` pts ≈ `37.5 m²`) under unlimited flooding. | Exact per-particle slurry; heaviest state to stream across moving cave rows or serialize. **0.53–1.61 ms/step**. |

### Recommended model: Hybrid (Ballistic Jet/Cascade + 2D Smooth-Surface Cells)

The hybrid architecture best satisfies all four constraints of this game —
unlimited-tank flooding, smooth visible free surface, arbitrary 2D caves with
row streaming, and exact mineral/energy accounting:

1. **Airborne phase (fixed typed-array droplet pool):**
   - Pressurized nozzle droplets fly ballistically under gravity (inheriting
     platform velocity), dissolve rock on high-speed impact, and pick up exact
     dissolved mineral mass & value.
   - **Overhang-to-droplet re-emission:** cell water spilling off a rock ledge
     into open air (two empty cells beneath) transfers its exact volume,
     horizontal momentum, and slurry into a cascade droplet so waterfalls arc
     parabolically onto lower ledges instead of forming floating grid bars.
   - On hitting rock or a pool surface, a droplet deposits its exact volume and
     slurry into the open fluid cell and injects horizontal momentum into the
     local cell edges.
2. **Pooled & flowing phase (2D finite-volume cell grid):**
   - Fluid grid at `0.25 m/cell` (`96 × 60` in the classic chamber, `2 × 2`
     rock cells per fluid cell; or `0.125 m/cell` if tighter cave crevices need
     it — measure against cave widths).
   - Multi-pass vertical gravity fall, analytical hydrostatic compression
     (`maxComp ≈ 0.025–0.03`) with upward pressure relief for U-tube arches,
     and staggered horizontal wave momentum (`edgeU`) on supported cells only
     (preventing mid-air diffusion).
   - Every cell flux moves water volume `Δw` and exact proportional slurry
     `Δs = s · (Δw / w)` (snapping `Δs = s` when draining empty so zero
     slurry is ever stranded in a dry cell).
   - **Under-platform suction:** drains wet cells inside the chassis intake
     zone (`x ∈ [plat.x − halfSpan, plat.x + halfSpan]`, reaching below the
     belly onto the ground beneath), creating a natural local surface drawdown
     that pulls surrounding water inward from both sides and transfers exact
     slurry mass & value into the platform hopper.
3. **Visible smooth surface rendering (Canvas 2D, zero hot-path allocations):**
   - Build a `C⁰`-continuous signed sub-cell height field at grid nodes
     (`(cols+1) × (rows+1)`), lightly smoothed horizontally across neighboring
     open surface cells so calm pools render glassy flat with zero row-crossing
     popping.
   - Average open cells only when populating corners adjacent to solid rock,
     and draw solid rock/rubble after water: water meets curved cave walls,
     overhangs, and sloped rubble **100% flush with zero air gap**.
   - Emit ONE unified Marching Squares path (scanline-merged full-cell runs
     plus interpolated boundary polygons) and fill once with a vertical depth
     gradient — avoiding semi-transparent tile overlap seams and expensive
     `ctx.clip()` masks.
   - Overlay suspended slurry turbidity inside submerged water so mineral-rich
     runoff reads visually, and stroke only true **air-water** contour segments
     (skipping rock-contact edges) with a soft glow plus bright meniscus crest.

*Fallback / alternative:* if playtesting favors maximum splash/wave drama over
unlimited flooded volume, 2D FLIP (also runnable in `prototypes/water-lab.html`)
shares the exact same Marching Squares surface renderer and 2D cave solid mask;
hybridizing FLIP near the active camera with cell storage for distant cave
pools is a well-defined upgrade path if ever needed.

## Shared terrain & cave interface

To avoid coupling `js/water.js` to classic heightmaps:

- Water reads terrain through a thin 2D solid query (`isSolid(fc, fr)` /
  local rock SDF & surface normal for ballistic impact and meniscus wall
  masking), rebuilt only on dirty regions when rock is mined, dissolved,
  collapsed, or graded.
- In the classic chamber, solid occupancy is derived directly from intact
  ceiling rock (`strength > 0`) plus floor rubble (`y < floorAt(x)`) and
  chamber end walls.
- In `gas-caves.1` and `0.4.0-wild-mine`, the exact same query reads 2D cell
  occupancy (`intact | damaged | rubble` = solid; `void | removed` = open).
- Top-row replenishment (`js/ceiling.js`) and Wild Mine row streaming shift
  the water and slurry grids by the same row delta (evicting or booking any
  fluid scrolled out of active bounds cleanly).

## Accounting & energy ledger

- **Material and value conservation (exact):** rock dissolved by the water jet
  transfers its exact cell mass, mineral mix, and quoted value into suspended
  slurry. Slurry travels strictly with water flux, settles onto floor/ledge
  rubble if water is left behind (optional sedimentation), or enters the
  collecting platform's hopper when sucked under the hull. Total dissolved
  mass/value equals `suspended + settled + hopper` at every step.
- **Energy ledger with an unlimited tank (exact):** water comes from an
  unlimited supply and does not alter `platformMass` or `cargoHeight`. Each
  nozzle launch draws `shotEnergy` from `world.battery` (and optional suction
  pump power while actively drawing water) to pressurize and project the jet;
  terrain-prescribed vertical launch speed books to `world.terrainWork` like
  the sandblaster. At rock impact, strength removed books to
  `world.fractureEnergy` and the remaining launch energy books to `world.heat`.
  Ambient pool flow and waterfall gravity dissipation after impact remain
  outside the dry rig's mechanical ledger (no phantom energy created by
  flooding water from an unlimited tank).
- **Economy tuning:** determine jet energy draw, dissolution rate, and pump
  suction power with a dedicated yield probe
  (`experiments/water-yield-probe.cjs`, mirroring
  `experiments/sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs`) before locking shop prices and
  equipment gates.

## Scope for implementation milestone

- `js/water.js`: per-owner profiles, fixed ballistic jet/cascade pool, 2D
  hydrostatic + wave-momentum fluid & slurry grid, dirty-region solid mask
  adapter, aimed fire (unlimited tank), under-platform suction pump, unified
  Marching Squares smooth-surface renderer.
- Integration: `js/physics.js` step/reset/nozzle hooks, `js/ceiling.js`
  dissolution & cargo-load/spill hooks, `js/equipment.js` shop item, page
  panel (Fire / Aim, Auto-fire, Pump under-rig toggle, status), canvas draw
  layer ordering (water before rock/rubble flush pass, jets & pump intake
  overlay after platform), help topic, README.
- Tests & probes: `experiments/water.test.js` (aim clamping, unlimited tank
  flooding, under-platform suction retrieval, full-hopper handling, exact
  water & slurry conservation, U-tube & overhang flow, energy ledger closure,
  row-shift preservation, pool bounds, determinism), interface wiring tests,
  and `experiments/water-yield-probe.cjs`.
- Acceptance: visible smooth free surface with no cell-row popping or wall
  gaps; smooth flow across both classic uneven rubble and 2D cave ledges;
  unlimited-tank flooding balanced by under-platform retrieval; zero slurry
  or energy ledger drift; bounded hot-path cost with zero per-frame
  allocations.
