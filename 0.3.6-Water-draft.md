
## Water gun (after sand gun)

- Sand gun (not implemented; prerequisite): platform tool, fires small stones;
  small damage; ammo/energy from storage; stones fall back as debris.
- Water gun: platform tool (owner TBD); hold to fire water droplets; low damage;
  aim follows cursor. Press-and-hold fires, release stops; a short click still
  sets autopilot X and must not move the destination.
- Water moves debris by random dissolve/erosion/sedimentation; the platform pumps
  water + dissolved debris from under itself. Water model TBD; keep the
  mass/value ledger exact.
