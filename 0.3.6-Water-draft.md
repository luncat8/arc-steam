# 0.3.6 — Water gun (draft, after the sandblaster)

## Status after the sandblaster review

The sand gun this draft was written against is no longer a prerequisite on the
drawing board: the sandblaster is implemented and wired per
`0.3.6-Sandblaster-plan.md` (`js/sandblaster.js`, Impact-gated 520 cr shop
item, per-owner magazine and wall auto-buy, F/Blast hold-to-fire, shared
`SteamCeiling.chip` weakening, exact energy ledger). The water gun below is
the next tool in the same family and should reuse that machinery rather than
re-derive it.

## Decisions carried over from the sandblaster

- **Per-owner tool, same profile shape.** A `profiles` Map keyed by owner id
  (charges, auto-buy, visit count, counters) in the water module, mirroring
  `js/sandblaster.js` and `js/rockets.js`. Owner is no longer TBD: every
  platform owns its own water gun, its own purse debit and its own standing
  auto-buy order, exactly like the sandblaster.
- **Manual this milestone.** Hold to fire, release stops; bot operation is a
  follow-up, like the sandblaster's.
- **Reuse the droplet flight machinery.** The sandblaster's ballistic grain
  step (climb, impact, fall, catch/loss, the exact energy booking at each
  resolution) is the model for the droplets. The water gun differs in two
  ways: the droplets are aimed (below) and their effect on the rock is
  dissolution rather than chipping.
- **Wall purchases and auto-buy.** Water (or water + abrasive mix) is bought
  at a SELL wall for the owner's own purse, with the same per-visit auto-buy
  cap pattern. The magazine shape and the ledger entries follow the
  sandblaster's.
- **Exact ledger.** The dissolve/erosion/sedimentation model must keep the
  mass/value ledger exact the way the sandblaster's catch and loss do: every
  droplet's energy books at launch, impact and resolution, and every gram of
  dissolved material books to the platform that pumps it.

## Open design points

- **Aim follows the cursor.** The nozzle throws droplets along the direction
  from the nozzle to the cursor, clamped to the chamber, instead of straight
  up. A press-and-hold fires continuously along the held aim; release stops.
  A short click on the fire button still sets autopilot X and must not move
  the travel destination — the sandblaster's pointer-capture Blast handling
  (pointerdown fires, pointerup stops, no destination side effects) is the
  pattern to keep.
- **Low damage, dissolution model.** "Low damage" needs a concrete rule:
  either the shared `SteamCeiling.chip` weakening at a smaller radius/energy
  (a droplet carries far less than a grain), or a new dissolution rule that
  thins rock over a wider area. Decide against the probe: the sandblaster's
  yield probe (`experiments/sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs`) is the template for
  measuring what a droplet stream brings down before fixing the numbers.
- **Water moves debris by random dissolve/erosion/sedimentation.** The
  droplets thin the rock and the loosened material settles as debris; the
  platform then pumps water plus dissolved debris from under itself. The pump
  is the water gun's collector moment: it gathers the slurry into the
  platform's cargo (value booked to that platform), the way the sandblaster's
  catch returns the charge to the owner. Whether the pump needs its own
  geometry (a wider scoop than the sandblaster's collector span) or reuses
  the collector catch is an implementation choice, pending the dissolution
  model.
- **Water model TBD.** Whether water is a consumable (bought at the wall,
  spent by firing, caught again like sand) or an onboard tank refilled at the
  wall changes the magazine shape. The sandblaster's caught-charge return is
  the precedent for a consumable that cycles; a tank is simpler but loses the
  material-conservation story.

## Scope

- `js/water.js` (or an extension of the sandblaster module): per-owner
  profiles, fixed owner-tagged droplet pool, aimed fire, the droplet step
  (flight, dissolve, fall, pump catch, loss), draw.
- Page: the water panel (Fire, Buy, auto-buy, status), the fire key, the
  nozzle and droplet drawing, the help topic, the README — mirroring the
  sandblaster's wiring.
- Tests: cost, dissolution accumulation, pump recovery and loss, the energy
  and mass/value ledger, pool bounds; a yield probe for the economy.
- Acceptance: material and value conserved except for pump losses; damage
  accumulates through the chosen rule; the ledger stays balanced across
  firing, dissolution and pumping.
