# 0.3.2 — Top rock growth and live market trend review

## Review of minerals.1

- `collapseChunkMaxNow()` in `js/ceiling.js` read only `mineralClusterGrowth[0..2]`
  instead of all six entries of `mineralTable`, so raising Fluorite, Silver or
  Gold growth above Iron/Copper/Crystal did not scale `collapseChunkMax`.
- `#market-drawer` showed lifetime sales and the transaction ledger without the
  per-mineral live quotes, drift against base price or short-term quote slope
  described in `README.md` and `js/help.js`.
- Tier-III equipment and late progression cost calibration against the realised
  six-mineral income spread was deferred into `0.3.7-plan.md` so upcoming
  replenishment, rocket and sandblaster mechanics are included in one pass.

## Shipped

- Market review fixes (`js/ceiling.js`, `index.html`, `styles.css`,
  `js/main.js`, `js/main_readouts.js`, `js/help.js`):
  - Fixed `collapseChunkMaxNow()` to scan `0 .. mineralCount - 1`.
  - Preallocated `mineralTrends = new Float64Array(mineralCount)` on
    `SteamCeiling.state`, updated causally in `updateMarket()` as the
    per-minute relative slope over the preceding second.
  - Added `#market-rows` (`Mineral`, `cr/cell`, `vs Base`, `Trend`, `Cargo`,
    `Sold`) in `#market-drawer` plus directional arrows on `#inventory-rows`,
    guarded by `setText` / `setTone` so paused frames perform zero DOM writes.
- Top rock growth (`js/ceiling.js`, `js/physics.js`, `js/main_draw.js`,
  `js/main_input.js`, `js/main_readouts.js`):
  - Preallocated typed arrays up to `maxRows = 120` and decoupled continuous
    downward shift (`totalShift`, `queuedShift`, `growthRate = 5` rows/s) from
    empty-bottom-row compaction (`baseRow`, `pruneBottomRows()`).
  - Triggered `SteamCeiling.queueGrowth(1)` on upward platform rebounds
    (reflector piston and full-collector / hull deck bounces) and elastic /
    half-energy ground rebounds; `launch`, collector capture, side walls and
    rock contacts do not queue rows.
  - Shared `populateRow(row, geologicalRow, ...)` between `reset()`,
    `generateTestRock()` and `appendTopRow()` so incoming strata extend the
    seeded 3-channel Y-compressed mineral seams seamlessly and revalue at
    current market quotes.
  - Capped rock reach at 70% of chamber height (`10.5 m`, `84` rows) so at
    least 30% (`minBottom = 4.5 m`) stays open below: `pruneBottomRows()` only
    compacts completely mined rows (`strength <= 0`) without ever destroying
    intact lower rock, and `queueGrowth` / `stepGrowth` stop adding new lines
    whenever `lowestIntactY() <= 4.5 m` until lower rock is mined clear.
  - Replaced row-count-dependent discrete quadratic cell heights with a
    chamber-anchored `C^1` monotone cubic perspective over `[11.375, 15.0]` m
    (unit scale below `11.375 m`, `0.25x` at `15.0 m`, 6-step Newton inverse in
    `rockWorldY`), eliminating visual jumps as rows enter or compact.
  - Tracked `selectedGeoLayer` across `baseRow` compactions so rock hover
    highlights and `mineral-cell` stay attached to the physical cell.
  - Booked incoming top-row potential energy and prescribed downward shift in
    `exportedEnergy` so `SteamPhysics.totalEnergy(world)` remains invariant.
