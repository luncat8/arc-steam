# 0.3.1 — Delayed ceiling collapse

## Goal

Separate impact damage from material detachment. A hit first weakens ceiling
pixels; pixels fall later, in local groups, with probability driven by damage.

## Decisions

- An impact increases damage in a radius with falloff; it does not detach
  pixels immediately.
- The ceiling is divided into local collapse regions.
- Each region is checked periodically, with probability depending on its
  damage level.
- On a successful check, only a bounded number of eligible pixels detach.
- Eligibility is limited to pixels whose damage lies in a configured range.
- Highly damaged regions collapse more often, but the process stays gradual.
- Check frequency and detached volume are capped to avoid load spikes on large
  damaged areas.

## Suggested implementation

- Add damage and collapse-region state to `js/ceiling.js`.
- Add a scheduler that processes a limited number of regions per frame.
- Store collapse probability and detached-pixel limits in config.
- Reuse the existing debris creation path for detached pixels.
- Add tests for probability scaling, eligibility ranges and load caps.

## Acceptance

- A single impact does not detach pixels immediately: it spends at most half of a cell's remaining strength and leaves the ring around it standing.
- Collapse probability rises with damage: a region rolls with a probability that rises with the mean looseness of the rock it has actually been hit in.
- Only eligible, damaged pixels detach: only exposed cells whose damage is high enough, and only in clumps of 1..4 cells.
- Large damaged areas do not cause frame spikes: one pass over the field per 0.25 s tick, a clump never larger than `collapseChunkMax`, and a cell is never detached twice.
- The inspector marks a cell loose when its damage is high enough to let it go.
