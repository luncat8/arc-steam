# Wild Mine grader — Slice 5 worklog

## Review and implementation

- Reviewed the bore's signed grade and direction convention, its wheel-anchored tunnel floors, and the existing rubble/energy ledgers. The previous Wild blade only traversed single-cell steps and always levelled and shaved, so it could not dress larger chamber drops or preserve fill for ramps.
- Added Wild-only platform-pitch, bore-grade and smooth blade modes. Pitch and bore modes transport loose material toward the chosen world-space slope and retain the existing shave and settlement. Smooth mode redistributes without shaving, so the player can build fill by making repeated passes. Selection persists across run resets; Classic remains unchanged.
- Added connected-floor traversal across arbitrary vertical steps, rejecting floating shelves, obstructed air and unrelated galleries. A buried lip now shares a continuous rendered and physical crest; roofs and available material bound the result. Mixed cells carry their own value during every transfer, and a cumulative shaved-value counter preserves the material audit after settlement.
- Kept the existing 45 N grader resistance and energy booking. Landed rubble was already outside the mechanical ledger, so no second rubble-potential account is introduced. The slope-following modes still shave and pay the normal blade share; smooth never claims a payment for transported material.
- Added the Wild controls and help text, plus physics, topology, streaming, material, energy and interface tests.
