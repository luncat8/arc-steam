# 0.4.0 Wild Mine fuel — worklog

## Review of the previous slice

The rocket fabricator worked, but its own invariant was not true: a fabricated
rocket took 2 kJ of useful reserve energy and one cargo cell out of the world
without booking either. A moving fab cycle drifted `physics.totalEnergy` by
about 2.0 kJ per rocket, plus the consumed cargo's potential and platform
kinetic share. The module's cargo loop also carried an indentation break.

Fixed by adding `world.exportedProduct`, a positive ledger term for energy that
leaves the platform inside a product:

- `fabCycle` now records `delivered + removedCargoMass * g * cargoHeight +
  ½ removedCargoMass * vx²` there; the rocket already carries its own charge
  outside the ledger by design, so this is the export side of that rule.
- `physics.createWorld` initialises it and `totalEnergy` adds it.
- `experiments/rocket-fab.test.js` gained a moving-cycle ledger test.

## Slice 2 — cargo fuel

### Decisions

- Wild Mine has no pad and no refuelling wall. The installed engine burns cargo
  and holds `world.battery` at `physics.config.battery` (the engine-dependent
  `reserveNow()`).
- Three fuel families over the existing mineral table, no new ore and no second
  market:
  - solid (coal, electric): stone, sand, quartz;
  - liquid (oil): gas, fizzy halite, fluorite, vitriol, halite;
  - fissile (nuclear): iron, copper, corundum, silver, gold, pure gold.
  - hand has no grade and never burns.
- Refill rate is `capacity / engine.reload`, the same seconds-to-full a pad
  refill uses. Tools that outdraw it run the reserve buffer down.
- Each burn takes the cheapest loaded cell per joule in its own family, using
  the live quote, so ore survives while cheaper fuel is present.
- Chemical energy enters the reserve as input energy; `physics.spend` still
  applies engine efficiency, so combustion waste heat appears exactly where the
  charge-pad path already books it.
- Cargo potential that was already in `ceiling.energy` is reconciled against
  `world.importedEnergy`; a moving platform's kinetic share for the removed mass
  is booked to heat. The ledger returned 0 drift at rest and while driving.
- The reserve card, cargo card and status strip report engine family, fuel
  cells, chemical kJ and refill kW, or the exact no-fuel reason. A `Wild fuel`
  help topic and its reserve-card mark were added.

(6/6), then the full suite: 35 files, every test green. `node
experiments/fuel-probe.cjs` recorded the opening-bed table: solid 0.70 kJ
