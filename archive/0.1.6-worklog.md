# 0.1.6 — Rock-shape reflection keeps the ball impulse; measured trips and first equipment panel

## Decisions and plan

- Playtest report: rock-shape reflection sometimes caused unexpected very fast ball motion; the ball should keep impulse. Measurement over ~10⁷ traced contacts and full gameplay runs showed the reflection law itself never amplifies speed (`|v'|² = |v|² - (1-e²)(v·n)²` for any unit normal), so the report was chased as two contact-resolution defects instead of a restitution change.
- Defect 1 — stacked impulses: a ball touching two rock surfaces at once (heightmap spikes, square corners) received a second zero-time reflection impulse in the same step with up to 164° normal rotation, flipping the velocity into a sudden fast zip. Fix: at most one rock impulse per step; later zero-time contacts only separate the ball, so it keeps the contact impulse instead of stacking flips.
- Defect 2 — lost impulse (fly-through): contacts where the ball already overlapped the surface were silently skipped (the sweep ignored `dist0 < radius - 1e-4`, corners ignored deep overlap). The smooth heightmap cuts up to ~0.67 m into square cells at crater edges, so switching Rock Shape with the ball near the surface left it inside the solid — measured pre-fix: the ball stayed inside the rock for 480/480 frames and drifted through it at full speed. Fix: any overlap within the contact span resolves at `s = 0` with separation to exactly radius, and a centre already inside the solid (either shape) is resolved immediately: dropped to the surface with the standard reflection impulse. Escaping (non-approaching) contacts are left untouched, so the ball keeps its impulse and exits naturally.
- Exact contact placement: the trace returns the separated contact centre and the ball is placed there, removing the chord/parabola snap drift; the snap's potential change is booked to heat so the energy ledger stays exact. Separation is position-only: the impulse law (90% normal-energy retention) and drive pulse are unchanged, and measured gameplay is bit-identical to pre-fix in normal runs.
- Continued by the plan with measured mine–collect–sell trips (smooth default and square option, 8 seeds each, one 12 kJ battery shift or 240 s): smooth yields ~166 cells/min and ~247 cr/min, square yields ~348 cells/min and ~503 cr/min, with ~6 cr realized per cell and ~650–1400 cr per battery shift. Mineral prices [3, 7, 11] cr/cell are kept: the realized average sits mid-range with a healthy 3.7× spread between ores. First-tier equipment costs are set against the measured shift income (250/400/350 cr ≈ half to one shift each).
- First equipment upgrade panel: three purchasable items with explicit deltas on top of base tuning (installed equipment is separate from tuning data): Rebound pad (+3% normal-energy retention, +6 kg), Drive coil (+12 J pulse, +12 kg), Lightweight chassis (−12 kg dry mass). Purchases debit funds through the economy ledger (`type: 'buy'` entries), refuse duplicates and insufficient funds, and install immediately into the running simulation. Mass changes shift platform kinetic energy, so the difference is booked to heat at install and the energy ledger stays exact.

## Implementation

- `js/ceiling.js`: `traceSmooth`/`traceSquare` inside-solid rescue (dropped onto the surface, outward normal, only inside the rock layer); segment/face/corner overlap contacts resolve at `s = 0` with push-out to exactly radius; approach checks kept for sweep contacts so non-approaching touches cannot spin on zero-time events; `trace()` fully resets the shared hit record; `purchase(label, amount, time)` debits funds and appends a frozen `buy` ledger entry.
- `js/physics.js`: type-5 contacts place the ball at the exact separated contact position (snap ΔPE booked to heat) and apply damage + `passiveImpact` at most once per step (`rockImpulse` gate); effective retention/pulse/dry mass read installed-equipment deltas on top of base config; `dryMass()` exported; world creation resets installed equipment.
- `js/equipment.js` (new): frozen item table with cost/mass/retention/pulse deltas, install/uninstall-free state set, funds-gated `install(id, world)` that books mass-change kinetic energy to heat, `reset()`.
- `js/main.js` / `index.html` / `styles.css`: EQUIPMENT UPGRADES panel (per-item cost, mass and effect preview, Buy/Installed state, funds gating), purchase status line, `buy` entries in the market ledger renderer, platform-mass readout from `dryMass()`.
- `experiments/ceiling.test.js`: overlapping contacts resolve at the surface; deep inside-solid centres are dropped below the surface in both shapes.
- `experiments/physics.test.js`: rock reflection never grows ball speed beyond gravity on jagged craters and never freezes the ball (both shapes); Rock Shape switch cannot tunnel the ball through either solid in either direction and cannot amplify speed; energy balance holds.
- `experiments/equipment.test.js` (new): purchase requires funds, debits once, refuses duplicates, and appends a frozen `buy` entry; the pad/coil/chassis change bounce retention, pulse size and acceleration in-simulation; world reset clears installed equipment; mass changes keep the energy ledger exact.
- `experiments/rock-impulse-check.cjs`: dense trace scan (≈9.3 × 10⁶ contacts, both shapes, craters/overlaps), per-step impulse counting through `damage()` calls (no stacked impulses), and shape-switch tunnel probes. `experiments/mine-trips.cjs`: the mine–collect–sell trip policy used for economy calibration. Results in `experiments/logs/0.1.6-mine-trips.txt`.
- `README.md`: documented the impulse-preserving rock reflection guarantee and the equipment panel.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: 30/30 pass (24 previous + 3 reflection regressions + 3 equipment tests).
- `node experiments/rock-impulse-check.cjs`: dense scan 9,296,028 traces with 0 amplifications and 0 non-unit normals; 371 rock impulses in aggressive gameplay with 0 stacked-impulse steps and 0 unexplained speed gain; both shape-switch directions never place the ball inside the solid and never amplify speed.
- Pre-fix reproduction (separate processes, old `js/`): ball inside square rock for 480/480 frames after a `smooth → square` switch with the ball at the crater-edge surface; the same probe now shows 0 inside frames and gravity-only speeds. A false inside-rock report for any ball below the layer (found by the trip script freezing square mode) is fixed and covered by the freeze assertions.
- Drive-game comparison old vs new (3 seeds): identical max speeds (19.49/18.76/18.75 m/s), grounding times, pushes and bounces — normal play is unchanged.
- Economy calibration runs (see log): smooth 247 cr/min, square 503 cr/min under the scripted policy; first-tier prices (250/400/350 cr) verified fundable within one to two battery shifts; `experiments/equipment.test.js` proves purchases, effects and ledger exactness.
- `node --check` on all application and experiment scripts, and `git diff --check`: pass. Browser smoke not run (no browser executable in this environment).
