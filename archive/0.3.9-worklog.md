# Perspective physics and the debris ceiling

## Previous-task review

Two settings from `0.3.4` and `0.3.7` were reported as "not doing anything".
Both reports were right, and for different reasons.

`PERSPECTIVE` was built as a view rule and the code kept that promise too
literally: `main_draw.js` passed `config.radius` as the ellipse's horizontal
radius and multiplied only the vertical one, so a ball in the warp was drawn as
a squashed ellipse while the simulation kept a 0.18 m circle. A picture that
disagrees with the body is not a perspective; it is a second, wrong shape.
It was also nearly unreachable: a 240 s probe with the whole autopilot enabled
peaked at 10.94 m, and the warp starts at 11.375 m, so most players never saw
the band at all.

`DEBRIS` was built as "the height of a tile, never the material", and piles are
stored in metres, so switching 4 → 1 changed only the tile the *next* grain
would be drawn with. Existing piles kept their metres, the ground kept its
height, and the only visible effect was a different tessellation of the same
pile. The 0.7 m per-column cap was a second, unrelated "max level".

## Implementation

- `js/ceiling.js`
  - `warpIntegral(y)` factors the antiderivative the warp already used, so
    `rockDrawnY` and the new `perspectiveScale` are the same curve.
  - `perspectiveScale(y)` returns the chamber's local scale at that height —
    `rockScale(y)`, 1× up to 11.375 m, a mild stretch just inside the warp,
    0.25× at the roof — or 1 everywhere under `Constant size`.
  - `setDepthScale` / `state.depthScale` hold the setting beside the law.
  - `setFloorCompression` re-lays every column by `oldSize / newSize` and books
    what the roof cannot hold in `lostCells` / `lostValue`.
  - `pileLimit` is `top` (15 m); the 0.7 m deck cap is gone.
  - `drawFloor` charts a column in at most 64 rows of proportionally taller
    tiles, so a pile at the roof costs no more than a shallow one.
  - `updateHeightmap` treats an exhausted column as the roof and does the
    row-to-metre multiply itself.
- `js/physics.js`
  - `ballRadiusAt(y)` is the drawn radius, `placementRadius` seats a ball on a
    surface, `setBallPerspective` is the one writer of the setting.
  - `advanceBall` solves a step with the smallest body it spans; the wall
    rebound snaps onto the wall; the rock trace, floor, roof, deck and hull all
    use that body.
  - `planeContactTime` refines the contact with the radius at the contact
    height, so a face and the body that met it agree.
  - Held seat, release height, scoop, depenetration and `focusBallOf` all read
    the ball's own radius at the platform's height.
- `js/prediction.js`, `js/autopilot.js`, `js/players.js`: the forecast marches,
  deck seats and strike apexes use the same body.
- `js/main_draw.js`: `context.arc` at `config.radius * perspectiveScale(y)`,
  the same number the physics uses; the damage ring keeps its own overlay.
- `js/main_input.js`: both selects call the real setters, drop the autopilot
  plan and refresh; `resetRun` reapplies both as chamber rules.
- `js/help.js`, `README.md`: both topics, both bar rows and the test summary
  rewritten. `archive/0.3.4-plan.md` and `archive/0.3.7-plan.md` carry a
  superseded note.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: 300 / 300.
- `experiments/depth-scale.test.js` (new, 6 cases): the law is the cells' own
  (1× below the warp, a quarter at the roof, the same number at 12.6/13.4/14.4);
  `Depth scale` resizes the body `ballRadiusAt` returns; a ball rolled at the
  right wall from 14.6 m rebounds exactly on `width - itsRadius`, while
  `Constant size` rebounds on the classic wall; a deck face at 14.3 m is met
  one own-radius above it instead of one classic radius; below the warp both
  settings fly an identical ball to 1e-12; the ballistic computer's drawn
  flight stops on the small body's wall.
- `experiments/debris-compression.test.js`: re-lay, roof cap, roof overflow
  booked as lost, 64-row budget for a deep pile, tile ratio and layer count,
  harvest parity, New game and test rock keeping the choice.
- `experiments/interface.test.js`: round ball at the physics radius, a high ball
  drawn small, `Constant size` restoring 0.18 m in both layers, New game
  keeping the law; DEBRIS switching 0.10 m to 0.40 m with `floorAt` following.
- A page-level probe through `experiments/dom.cjs`: `DEBRIS 1` turns a 0.10 m
  pile into 0.40 m and the ride height follows; `Constant size` gives a 0.18 m
  roof ball and `Depth scale` a 0.045 m one; New game keeps both settings and
  clears the floor.
- 300 s autopilot balance probe: max pile 0.563 m against 1 205 lost cells, hull
  never above 6 m — the roof cap is not a trap in normal play.
- Bench: 60 simulated seconds 479 ms against 478 ms on the base commit.

## Pitfalls found

- `createWorld()` calls `ceiling.reset(1)`: clear the rock *after* the world
  exists, or the reset restores it.
- `firstRow` is only refreshed by `updateFirstRow`, so every trace refreshes it;
  a heightmap built from an exhausted column must be handled as the roof.
- The face solver had an unused-parameter end (`halfExtent` passed but a stale
  `angle` referenced); splitting `faceContactTime` out made the fixed-point
  refinement obvious and removed the trap.
- A radius that varies with height makes "the ball's radius" ambiguous at every
  call site: the rule is the smallest body the step spans, and the body at the
  contact height for a face.

## Browser check

`experiments/browser-smoke.cjs` gained the two settings: switching `DEBRIS`
4 → 1 re-lays a 0.10 m pile to 0.40 m (cell height 0.03125 → 0.125) and the
floor band's pixels change, and `PERSPECTIVE` moves `ballRadiusAt(top)` from
0.045 m under `Depth scale` to the classic 0.18 m under `Constant size` while
the floor keeps 0.18 m. The full smoke run passes on this commit, including the
known-slow touch page (its cross-page hover timings are the only reason it
needed a `toDataURL` readback instead of a locator screenshot).
