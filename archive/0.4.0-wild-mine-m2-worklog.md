# 0.4.0 Wild Mine — M1 fixes and milestone M2 (mode shell)

## 1. M1 review & fixes (`js/cave.js`)

Reviewing `js/cave.js` and `0.4.0-wild-mine-plan.md` ahead of M2 surfaced five
concrete issues, all fixed and covered by three new tests in
`experiments/cave.test.js`:

- **`stream` world-metre indexing & `stored` restore.** `stream(minX, maxX)` was
  passing world metres directly into `chunkFor(column)` (which expects column
  indices), evicting active chunks at `x / cell`, and dropping previously
  edited chunks from `stored` when re-entering instead of restoring them.
  Fixed via `columnAt`, `existingChunk`, `restoreStored`, and `syncHaloFromNeighbors`.
- **4-neighbor free-face rule & border protection.** `weaken` now checks all four
  cardinal neighbours (`±1` column, `±1` row) rather than only the cell below,
  allowing horizontal cave wall and ceiling mining while holding strictly
  interior rock and `row = 0` / `row = rows - 1` border rows at `heldCellShare`.
- **`traceCircle` initial overlap & immediate exit.** When a circle begins
  penetrating rock (`sdf < r`), `traceCircle` pushes out along the gradient
  normal first and clears zero-length step hits.
- **`chunkLimit = 12` default.** Keeps a 192 m live cache window (eliminating
  cache thrashing across wide queries) while preserving explicit small-limit
  eviction tests (`chunkLimit: 4`).
- **Renderer hooks.** Exposed `pad`, `stride`, `chunk.editStamp`,
  `elementsAt(column, row, out)`, and `mixAt(column, row, out, elementsOut)` so
  a tile cache only recomputes dirty rows in a single geology pass.

## 2. M2 — the mode shell

### Domain (`js/wild-mine.js`, `js/physics.js`, `js/players.js`)

- **`js/wild-mine.js` (`SteamWildMine`)**:
  - Mode toggle (`setMode('classic' | 'wild')`, `getMode()`, `isWild()`).
  - **D7 vertical scale**: hyperbolic reciprocal law `s(u) = 1 / (1 + 3u)` with
    `scaleSpan = 16 m`, `minScale = 0.25`, anchored at `ball.y`, with exact
    closed-form `drawnY` and inverse `worldY`.
  - **D8 camera**: critically damped follow at `cameraRate = 2.5 /s` targeting
    the midpoint of `world.platform` and the active ball (or `world.platform`
    alone when no ball is active), streaming chunks ahead of motion.
  - **Platform hull on cave SDF**: `groundAt(x, refY)` uses `cave.floorBelow`
    from `refY` (`platform.y + 1`) so the chassis rides ledges, shelves, and
    cliff drops without snagging on roof rock above; `hullClear`,
    `constrainPlatform`, and `protectHull` sample wheel bases and hull corners
    against `cave.sdfAt` and push out or clamp `x`/`vx` when hitting cave walls.
  - **Start pose**: places P1 on the start shelf (`y = 30.875 m`), holds P2 on
    the collector deck (`ball.state === 'held'`), and records `world.initialEnergy`.
- **`js/physics.js` & `js/players.js`**:
  - Added `wildMode()`, `pistonMaxAngleNow()` (`±90°` in Wild Mine, `±10°` in
    Classic), `refY` plumbing across `platformBaseAt`, `platformPitchAt`,
    `updatePlatformTerrain`, and `movePlatform`, plus Wild Mine guards on
    `ballRadiusAt`, `atSellWall`, `onChargePad`, and `canLaunch` (ball launch stays
    locked until M3).
  - `players.init(world, 0)` boots Wild Mine as single-rig (`players.update` skips
    classic rival bots); `setPistonAngleOf` reads `physics.pistonMaxAngleNow()`.

### Renderer & UI (`js/main_wild.js`, `index.html`, `styles.css`, `js/main*.js`)

- **`js/main_wild.js`** (`wild*`-prefixed top-level bindings to satisfy `dom.cjs`
  lexical collision checks):
  - **Row-cached RGBA tile renderer**: per-chunk offscreen `<canvas>` tiles (LRU
    limit 12) filled lazily per row in `Uint32Array` via `putImageData` and
    drawn row-by-row through D7 `wildDrawnRowY[r..r+1]` with
    `imageSmoothingEnabled = false`. Invalidates only on `chunk.editStamp`,
    view-mode change, or price quote drift (`viewMode === 'price'`). Falls back
    to run-length `fillRect` under headless `dom.cjs` mock canvases.
  - Supports all 6 Classic view modes (`minerals`, `elements`, `price`,
    `initial-strength`, `strength`, `damage`), D7 4 m reference grid, D7-scaled
    `scale(1, pScale)` platform draw, held ball draw, travel target marker, and
    top-left corner HUD readout (`X`, `Y`, `SCALE 1.00× @ P2`).
  - **Input & readouts**: `wildWorldYOf`, `wildInspectRockAtPointer` (reusing the
    chamber's `#mineral-tooltip`), `wildPointerDown` (travel target on open air,
    rock selection on solid cells, piston drag), `wildModeNotes`, and
    `wildStatusReadout`.
- **`index.html` & `js/main*.js` & `styles.css`**:
  - Added `#chamber-title` and `#game-mode` (`classic` / `wild`) in the top bar.
  - Hidden rival rows in `js/main_players.js` backed by `.player-row[hidden] { display: none; }`
    in `styles.css` (caught via Chromium visual inspection: `.player-row { display: flex; }`
    previously overrode `[hidden]`).
  - `js/main_input.js` wires `#game-mode` `change` -> `resetRun`, restores Classic
    `resize()` when switching back from Wild Mine, scales piston wheel/drag rate
    in Wild Mine, and lifts slider clamping to `±90°`.
