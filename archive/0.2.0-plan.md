# 0.2.0 — Click-first chamber, contextual warnings, and the centre charge pad

Source: the 0.1.8 open decision and the `draft.txt` notes on shortcuts and energy.

## Decisions

- The mineral inspector has no coordinate sliders. A cell is chosen by clicking the rock it is in, which is how the player already thinks about what to mine; the sliders forced two numbers (column 96 / layer 1) that mean nothing while playing. The inspector reports the selected cell as `Column c / 192 · Layer r / 16` plus strength, mineral mix, price and elements.
- Chamber clicks are split into two zones, both drawn on the canvas so the rule is visible:
  - **Rock** (`y` from the exposed surface up to the ceiling, or any intact square cell in square mode, inside `0…24 m`) selects an inspector cell. The smooth surface is tested against the same linear column profile the renderer draws, so a click in a crater selects the exposed cell under the pointer while a click above the surface does nothing.
  - **Low band** (`y ≤ 1.7 m`, the height of the target marker already drawn for travel commands) sets the horizontal travel target.
  - Open air between the two zones does neither: inspecting rock can never make the platform drive.
- The centre charge pad is the next energy resupply model, replacing credit-only refuelling as the primary path:
  - A 4 m strip of ground centred at `x = 12`, rendered as a hatched blue plate labelled `CHARGE PAD · 3 kW` (green and `CHARGING` while active).
  - The installed engine draws from it while the platform stands still on the strip (`|vx| < 0.1 m/s`): 3 kW, so a full 12 kJ reserve takes exactly 4 s.
  - Charging is a ground type, not a consumable or a purchase: no credits, no fuel mass. The cost of energy is therefore position and downtime — the platform must return to the middle of the chamber and stop, which is the decision the draft asks for ("charge station at center — just type of ground that charge/refuel according to engine").
  - Imported energy is booked in `world.importedEnergy` (and `world.chargedEnergy`) exactly like a bought recharge, so the energy ledger stays exact: charging adds to the balance as an import, and `totalEnergy` subtracts it.
- The market recharge stays as an **emergency resupply** at 25 cr/kJ. It is the rescue for a platform stranded far from the centre with an empty reserve; the pad is the free but positional route.
- Telemetry warnings highlight the card that needs attention instead of adding another readout: the card gains a coloured left bar, an amber/red value and a tag drawn from `data-warning` in CSS (`LOW`, `EMPTY`, `FULL`, `HOT`, `CUTOFF`). Thresholds derive from simulation values, not new tuning: reserve below 10% is `LOW`, under 0.5 J is `EMPTY`; cargo at the 100-cell limit is `FULL`; any thermal derate is `HOT` and zero drive output is `CUTOFF`. The note under each value says what to do (park on the pad, sell at a wall, cool down).

## Suggested implementation

- `js/physics.js`: `chargePadWidth` / `chargeSeconds` / `chargePower` / `chargeMaxSpeed` in config (power derived from the 12 kJ reserve), `chargePadMin()`, `chargePadMax()`, `onChargePad(world)`, `charging(world)`, `charge(world, dt)` called from `step`, and `world.chargedEnergy` for accounting.
- `js/main.js`: inspector state is a cell index; `rockCellIndex(x, y)` resolves a click against the smooth or square surface, `selectCell(index)` is the single writer; `setWarning(id, state)` writes only on change; the reserve, cargo and thermal notes become state-aware; the canvas draws the pad plate and label.
- `index.html` / `styles.css`: sliders removed, `#mineral-cell` added, telemetry cards get ids and notes, charge-pad legend swatch and footer note, warning styling.

## Acceptance

- Clicking visible rock selects the cell under the pointer and leaves `world.target` untouched; clicking the low band sets the target and leaves the selection untouched; clicking open air changes neither.
- No column or layer sliders remain in the markup or the interface wiring.
- A platform parked on the centre strip fills the reserve from empty in 4 s; a moving platform and a platform elsewhere on the ground charge nothing; the import keeps the energy balance exact and adds no ledger credit entries.
- The telemetry strip shows `LOW` / `EMPTY` on the reserve, `FULL` on cargo, `HOT` / `CUTOFF` on temperature at the stated thresholds, and clears each tag when the condition ends.
