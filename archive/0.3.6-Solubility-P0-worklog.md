# 0.3.6 Solubility — P0 implementation worklog

Task: implement the P0 correctness fixes from
`0.3.6-Solubility-implementation-review.md`, add channel-level regression tests,
and correct the contradictory statements in `0.3.6-Solubility-plan.md`. The live
game port (plan §8.2) was **not** in scope.

## Review of the previous task

The read-only review found four load-bearing prototype defects: Mode B vented
Fluorite gas, gas-rock residue kept the expensive parent quote instead of the
stone quote, skeleton collapse used a static `remStrength` and never dropped
Halite-cemented Copper clasts, and Mode B was neither the published formula nor
the bound-B channel law. The review probe printed the old numbers; its verdicts
remain a pre-P0 snapshot, now marked as such at the top of the report.

## Changes

1. **Bound-B Mode B (`prototypes/solubility-lab.js`).** Replaced the ad-hoc
   normalisation and hard `reactTotal >= 0.22` cutoff with an exclusive
   `boundB / freeB` split. Hydrate-bound `B` becomes blue brine; crystal-bound
   `B` becomes `55%` Fluorite green brine + `45%` unleachable residue + `0` gas;
   free `B` becomes `85%` vented gas + `15%` stone residue. `out.boundB`,
   `out.freeB` and `out.crystal` expose the partition for tests. `crystalBrine`
   is scaled with the free-gas-rock factor and normalisation so it can never
   exceed the halite channel. Both `table` and `formula` now give Fluorite
   `55/45/0`; the Fluorite column yields `8.774` / `9.088` green brine, `0` gas
   and `14` standing exhausted cells.
2. **Gas-rock stone residue and exact value ledger.** Table chemistry now prices
   gas-rock residue at `MINERALS[def.residueMineral].price` and books the
   destroyed `price - stoneQuote` into `CHEM_GAS_VAL`. Submerged gas grains
   re-materialise as Stone with the stone quote and add the difference to
   `lostGasValue`. A `1.0` gas grain now ends as `0.15` Stone worth `0.435 cr`
   and `0.85` vented gas worth `149.565 cr`; mass/value drift stays at float
   epsilon. Cumulative dissolution slices are clamped to the rock cell's
   remaining mass/value, and detached per-mineral residue grains are scaled to
   the cell's exact remaining `rockMass` / `rockValue` (last component takes the
   remainder). This removed a `~3.2e-6` value drift that appeared after dynamic
   collapse started detaching mixed cells.
3. **Dynamic skeleton collapse.** `underminedSkeleton()` detects soluble cement
   around *different* insoluble clasts; pure Fluorite concentrate stays coherent.
   `exposeRockCell()` now compares
   `max(0, remStrength * (1 - dissolveProg) - accumulated jet damage)` against
   `DEBRIS_LIMIT`, so exhausted inclusion seams also let go under continued wash.
   The 60/40 karst seam at `x = 17.6` now dissolves `110` cells, leaves `0`
   exhausted skeletons and drops `110` Copper debris grains in `6 s`.
4. **Calibrated geology boxes.** `MINERALS[0..12]` now carry the plan §4.2
   calibrated `(R, G, B)` ranges and the geology scene compiles them at host
   weight `0.0883`. A direct 13-row cache measurement of the rounded boxes gives
   `copper + vitriol = 7.80%`; the `autoAdjust` probe log pins `7.83%`.
5. **Regression tests (`experiments/solubility-lab.test.js`).** Added four
   tests: all eight Mode B octant centers conserve channel mass and keep
   bound-B out of the gas channel; five representative centers match Mode A
   stoichiometry; both settings pass the Fluorite column check; gas residue is
   stone-quoted with destroyed value booked; dynamic karst collapse releases
   Copper clasts while static 75/25 strength stays `1.20 J`; and an overshot
   saturated parcel stays saturated through proportional pumping but recovers
   `sat > 0.85` after fresh water is poured. Suite grew from `9` to `13` tests.
6. **Plan corrections (`0.3.6-Solubility-plan.md`).** §1 item 7 now states the
   shared bound-B / gas-loss / residue-quote / dynamic-collapse rules. §4.2 line
   120 now says the lab uses the calibrated boxes at `0.0883`, not the §4.1
   prototype boxes. §5 replaces the scalar `Vitriol 1.00 / Gas ~0.61` claim with
   the implemented bound-B law and measured centers (`Vitriol 0.907`, Gas
   `0.85`, Fluorite `0.55`). §6.2 replaces static `remStrength` with
   `effStrength`, explicitly resolves the 75/25 example, and documents exact
   residue-value scaling. §8.2 item 4 lists the new lab test coverage.
7. **Review artifacts.** `0.3.6-Solubility-implementation-review.md` gained a
   pre-P0 status note; `archive/0.3.6-Solubility-review-worklog.md` points here;
   `experiments/solubility-review-probe.cjs` now prints corrected bound-B
   channels, stone-quoted residue, dynamic collapse, the added Fizzy center, and
   the `0.0883` geology measurement. Two reusable pitfalls (exact residue-value
   scaling and exclusive bound/free classification) were recorded in
   `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.

No files under `js/`, `index.html`, or the shipped live-game UI were changed.
`js/water.js`, the 13-row `js/minerals.js`, `basePricesInto()`-derived quotes and
`autoAdjust` integration remain later milestones.
