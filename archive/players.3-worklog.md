# players.3 — the competitor plays the ball like the rig

- Deleted the leftover sweep policy. `botDirection`, the wall turnaround in `stepRival`, the `steerWanted` "nothing to play → full throttle to the wall" branch and the `landing < 2.4 s || ball.y < 4` gate are gone. The competitor no longer paces the floor.
- Deleted the ownership blind spot behind it: `ownBall` only considered a ball whose `owner === rival.id`, so in `1 shared` and `2 shared` a competitor saw nothing to do. Measured before the change: a shared ball lying on the floor at x = 10 was driven over (rival x = 9.96) with `ballTarget` still `NaN`, then the platform went to the wall and back on a ~33 s cycle.
- `playBall(rival)` replaces it: every ball `mayPush` allows is playable, a ball on the floor first (it can be recovered at once), then the flight whose landing is soonest, never a ball held in a collector. It keeps the ball it is already playing unless another lands more than `ballCommit = 0.6 s` sooner, so two shared balls do not make it dither between landing points.
- `stepBallPolicy` turns that into one mark: the landing point at deck height (`rival.y + deck + radius`, with `ballLandingTime`), a floor ball's own x, or the nearer SELL wall when the hopper is full. It also sets `rival.playing` and `rival.markTime`; `rival.ballTarget` is gone.
- The pulse is now the platform's bounce drive, not the owner's: `rival.pulse` stays armed while *any* ball that deck may bounce would still fall short of `ceiling − radius`, and `bounceOnDeck` applies it to any ball the deck may push — the ownership rule already said a competitor may push a shared ball, so gating the pulse to owned balls contradicted it.
- `steerWanted` follows one mark either way: a bot's policy mark at the speed `physics.arrivalSpeed` allows, a travel click at full speed until its braking distance. `powerOf` uses `rival.commanded` (set from the wanted speed) instead of "any bot is pushing", so a competitor standing on its mark no longer outranks a driven rig for the ride-over rule.
- Moved the arrival rule out of the autopilot into `physics.arrivalSpeed(reach, time, acceleration)` (`approachSpeed 2.5`, `rollingArrival 0.5` now config), because both bots brake into their mark with it and `js/players.js` must not depend on the autopilot module. `commandTravel` calls it; the rig's behaviour is unchanged.
- Ledger: two holes appeared the moment a competitor touched a *shared* ball, which is in the rig's ledger. `scoop` lifted and pulsed the ball with no booking at all, and `bounceOnDeck` booked its restitution *before* applying the deck pulse, so every pulse leaked exactly 42 J. Both now book the difference as imported energy — the competitor's lift and pulse come from outside the rig — and `scoop` also books the potential-energy change from the floor to the deck.
- `applyBalls` clears `rival.playing` when the arrangement is rebuilt, so a remembered ball can never be a dead object from the previous set.
- Tests: `experiments/balls.test.js` (13) gained "a competitor plays a shared ball on the floor instead of walking past it" (the reported bug, with the ledger check), "a deck pulse lifts a shared ball, not only one it owns", "a competitor keeps a shared ball in play on its own", and replaced the gate test with "a competitor plans for the flight it may push, from any height, and for nothing else". `experiments/players.test.js` (14) replaced the two sweep tests with "the competitor bot holds station when there is nothing it may play", "a full competitor hopper makes its own selling trip and then resumes the ball", and rewrote both manual-override tests against the ball policy. The interface `parkRival` comment now says the rival chases the ball rather than sweeping.
