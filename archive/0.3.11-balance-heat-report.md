### 1. Water gun draw split & review findings

Across the 6 seeds × 7 berths (`n = 42`, 30 s parked, T1 stock electric `26.0 kJ`) before touching constants:

- **Jet was `81.5%` and pump `18.5%` of total draw** (`33.82 kJ` vs `7.66 kJ` overall; `18.91 kJ` vs `4.33 kJ` across the 36 off-pad berths; `123.25 kJ` vs `27.64 kJ` on the 6 centre-pad samples) — inverting `0.3.10-Water-worklog.md`'s guess because `135 J × 46/s = 6.21 kW` outweighed `420 J/unit × 2.6 u/s = 1.09 kW`. Turning the pump off added only ~15 droplets off-pad while leaving `22.8 cr` mean stranded in floor brine; on flooded fresh water the pump still drew the full `1.09 kW` (`32.76 kJ / 30 s`) with zero solute.
- **Concrete bugs fixed along the way:**
  - `water.js` `stepPump` pumped without checking `hasTool('rig')`, ran past `0 J` by fabricating unbacked `importedEnergy` (`+6.75 kJ` on an off-pad flooded test), and added solute mass to a moving hull without conserving momentum (`catchGrain` also read `machineMass` after `loadWetCargo`, double-counting grain mass).
  - `water.js` `step` rebuilt all 5,760 solid-mask parcels and copied fluid arrays at 240 Hz even in completely dry runs; a bit-exact `chamberWet()` check now skips wet sweeps when dry.
  - `sandblaster.js` and `water.js` bypassed `engine.efficiency` (letting 20%-efficient `coal` spend its large raw pack with zero motor loss) and kept firing through the `135 °C` thermal cutoff.
  - `players.js` now clears water `botFire` on BOT off, supports `botPaused` on manual uncheck, shares thermal hysteresis (`80 °C` pause / `65 °C` resume), counts active water+pump power in charge-trip headroom, and no longer overwrites the player's `Pump` toggle every tick.

### 2. Tool inventory & unified resource rules (`0.3.8-plan.md`)

All reserve-powered tools now route through `SteamPhysics.spend` / `reserveCost` (`cost = useful / η`, motor waste `cost - useful → heat`) and respect the `135 °C` cutoff:

| Tool | Role & resource coupling | Rebalanced draw |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Movement** | `dKE / η`, `480 → 1920 N`, `12 → 24 m/s`; derates `85 → 135 °C`; `60 N / 0.8 m/s` empty-pack hand crank | Unchanged |
| **Reflector piston** (+ pad, coil, ram, strike balls) | `42 → 84 J` useful pulse `/ η`; balls trade rebound retention for fracture/radius | Unchanged |
| **Brake + flywheel** | Mechanical brake works at any heat/charge; flywheel returns `49.5%` of brake KE (`+14 kg`) | Unchanged |
| **Ramp / Ballistic computer I–II / Commercial grader** | `0 J` reserve, `0 W` heat (ride-over geometry, economy intercepts lowering brake heat, 50% external sweep) | Unchanged |
| **Road grader** | `45 N · \|v\| / η` rolling work into heat (`+10 kg`, rig only) for 50% of shaved rubble | Unchanged |
| **Rocket rack** | `0 J` reserve, `0 W` heat; bounded by `14 cr`/round, 6-round rack, and wall reloads | Unchanged |
| **Sandblaster** | `135 J` useful/grain × `20/s` + `20 g` hopper sand/stone; stops on full hopper, empty feed, or `135 °C` cutoff | `2.70 kW` useful (`3.38 kW` electric, `4.91 kW` oil/nuclear, `13.50 kW` coal) |
| **Water gun + suction pump** | Jet `36 J` useful/drop × `46/s` + pump `120 J` useful/u × `2.6 u/s`; capped by battery, full hopper, and `135 °C` cutoff | `1.97 kW` useful (`2.46 kW` electric — `2.07 + 0.39 kW`, `3.58 kW` oil/nuclear, `9.84 kW` coal) |

**Cooling ladder** (`js/physics.js`, `js/research.js`):
- Bare hull: `1.1 → 3.60 W/K` (`~269 W` at `85 °C`)
- **Heat transfer** (`thermodynamics`): `+0.5 → +1.40 W/K` (`5.00 W/K` live)
- Radiator fins: `72 → 140 W/(m²·K)` — **Vent grille** `26.00 W/K` (`+4 kg`), **Radiator array** `54.00 W/K` (`+8 kg`), **Heavy radiator bank** `103.00 W/K` (`+16 kg`)

### 3. Measured balance across stages & engines

Full tables are in `experiments/logs/0.3.11-water-energy-split.txt` and `experiments/logs/0.3.11-tool-stage-balance.txt`:

- **Stage 1 — Starter (T1, bare `3.6 W/K`):**
  - Stock electric smart movement + piston stays at `74 °C` (`Tmax 81 °C`, `0%` derating, `100%` output). Aggressive chase (`92 °C`) and bare road grader (`92 °C`) enter mild derating until **Heat transfer** (`55 °C`, `0%` hot). Bare `coal` overheats (`108 °C`, `40%` hot) until a **Vent grille** (`55 °C`); `hand` binds on storage/force (`56%` pad time).
  - Weak-engine off-pad sand/water bursts stop in **`0.05–0.17 s`** (`hand`, reserve), **`1.6–3.6 s`** (`coal`, `135 °C` cutoff), **`3.2–6.9 s`** (`oil`, reserve), **`3.9–8.3 s`** (`electric`, reserve), and **`4.2–10.1 s`** (`nuclear`, bare-hull cutoff). BOT refuses nozzles on `hand` and T1 `coal`.
  - Paid emergency fuel remains unprofitable for both nozzles in direct parity (`water-yield-probe`: mean `13.1` cells, median `-476.9 cr`; `sandblaster-yield-probe`: mean `14.0` cells, median `-481.8 cr`).
- **Stage 2 — Midgame (T2 + Vent / Radiator array):**
  - **Vent grille** carries continuous T2 movement + piston + grader at `45–54 °C` (`100%` output) on `electric`/`oil`/`nuclear` and tames `coal` (`63 °C`).
  - **Radiator array** sustains one continuous nozzle + dry tools with distinct limits per engine: `electric` runs cool and binds on pad reloads (`12–16%` pad, `11–16%` cool pause), `oil` cuts pad time (`7–10%`) with moderate thermal pacing (`33–50%`), `nuclear` balances large reserve (`187 kJ`) against moderate heat (`37–51%`), and `coal` stays heat-bound (`79–87%` cool pause).
- **Stage 3 — Endgame (T3 full chain + Heavy radiator bank, all tools active):**
  - Runs movement, rammed piston, spiked ball, flywheel, road grader, rockets, sandblaster, water gun and brine pump together with **`0%` thermal cutoff** on `electric` (`50 °C` BOT / `72 °C` unthrottled auto, `98–100%` drive output), `oil` (`55 °C` / `82 °C`, fastest `3 s` reload), and `nuclear` (`63 °C` / `78 °C`, largest `333 kJ` pack, `4%` BOT pad time). `coal` remains heat-limited when firing both nozzles continuously (`72%` BOT pause, `82%` unthrottled cutoff).
  - Parked 30 s T3 electric + `bank` water jet runs uninterrupted off a single pack (`1,440` droplets, `37%` of reserve, `Tmax 45 °C`, median `25` cells / `68.6 cr` cargo on free pad power).
  - Every measured run closed `totalEnergy` to `≤ 1.6e-7 J`.
