## What now works

**Cargo is the fuel.** No pad, no refuel wall, no second commodity. `js/fuel.js` maps the existing mineral table onto three engine families:

| Grade | Engines | Minerals | Chemical yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| solid | coal, electric | stone, sand, quartz | 3.0 / 2.0 / 2.5 kJ/kg |
| liquid | oil | gas rock, fizzy halite, fluorite, vitriol, halite | 40 / 20 / 14 / 10 / 5 kJ/kg |
| fissile | nuclear | iron, copper, corundum, silver, gold, pure gold | 20–100 kJ/kg |

`hand` has no grade and never burns.

**Burn rules**
- The engine tops the reserve up at `capacity / engine.reload` — the same seconds-to-full a Classic pad refill takes.
- Each burn takes the cheapest loaded cell per joule in its own family first, using the live quote, so ore survives while cheaper fuel is present.
- Chemical energy enters the reserve as input energy; `physics.spend` still applies efficiency, so waste heat lands exactly where the pad-fed path books it.
- The ledger reconciles cargo potential against the imported share and a moving platform's kinetic share into heat. Drift measured at ~1e-10 while driving.

**UI/help:** reserve card names the engine, grade, fuel cells/kJ and refill kW; cargo card shows the family or the exact no-fuel reason; a new `Wild fuel` help topic and reserve-card mark explain it.
