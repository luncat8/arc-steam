# balls.1 — shared, two-shared and one-per-platform balls

- A ball is now `{ owner, x, y, vx, vy, state }` in `world.balls`. `owner: null` is a shared ball; `owner: 'rig'` or `'rival-1'` is a ball only that platform may push. `physics.mayPush(ball, id)` is the whole ownership rule, and `isRigBall` decides the ledger: rig-pushable balls are in `totalEnergy`, a competitor's own ball is outside it exactly like the commercial grader's blade.
- `world.ball` became a live getter over the list (`defineProperty` in `setBallMode`) returning `focusBall`: the held ball first, else the rig-pushable ball whose landing is soonest. Prediction, the autopilot, the launch, the readouts and every existing test therefore keep reading one ball and stayed correct without a shim.
- `advanceBall(world, ball, dt)` replaced the single-ball step: wall, ceiling, floor, rock, the rig's collector and piston (only for rig-pushable balls) and every competitor deck (only for balls that competitor may push). Balls never collide with each other; `heldBall`/`heldBallMass` let the rig hold exactly one, and the collector bounces a second ball instead of swallowing it.
- New arrangements via `setBallMode(world, mode, anchors)`: `shared1` (one shared ball at 12.8 m, 5 m, vx +1.2·scale), `shared2` (adds a shared ball at 7 m, 7 m, vx −1.2·scale) and `each` (a rig ball plus one per competitor at `min(width − r, rival.x + 1.4)`, 6 m, vx −1.2·scale). An unknown mode falls back to `shared1`, and every switch re-seats the balls and re-bases `world.initialEnergy`, so the ledger never chases a teleported ball.
- Competitor deck contact is `bounceOnDeck`: passive `sqrt(retention)` restitution, the deck's tangent carried at 35%, and — only for a ball that competitor owns and only while its `pulse` is on — a pulse sized as `v_n = sqrt(v_n² + 2·pulse/m)`. Ledger entries are booked only for rig-pushable gains and losses. `scoop` lifts a grounded owned ball onto the deck with one pulse (the competitor's version of the rig's catch, without a collector).
- `js/players.js` owns the arrangement (`ballMode`, `applyBalls`, `setBallMode(mode)`), the rival's ball policy (`stepBallPolicy`, `ownBall`, `ballTarget`, `pulse`) and rival pose bookkeeping. A rival chases the landing point when the ball lands within 2.4 s or is below 4 m, brakes by `sqrt(2·brakeAccel·|d|)` and sweeps the floor while the ball is high; `pulse` stays on while the apex would fall short of `ceiling − 0.5`. Manual input still outranks the policy. `createRival` now seeds `previousX/Y/Angle` from its settled pose — an unset previous pose solved deck contacts against a phantom body at the origin.
- `js/autopilot.js` records the ball it planned for (`seenBall`) and drops the plan when `world.ball` changes, so `2 shared` and `1 per platform` hand it a new target instead of carrying an intercept across balls.
- Interface: a `BALLS` select in the system bar after `ROCK SHAPE` (`1 shared` selected, `2 shared`, `1 per platform`), its own `?` mark and `balls` help topic (index 1, right after `keys`), a draw loop over `world.balls` (owner colour and a `#6f5a44` rim for an owned ball, cream when shared, pale while flying), `#ball-energy` appending `· n balls` when more than one is the rig's, and a change handler that sets the mode, resets the autopilot, clears held input and refreshes the panel. New game restores the select from the current mode.
- Bug found by the bot smoke runs, and fixed: `bodySurfaceAt` fed a body's whole-step pose delta to the ball contact solve as surface velocity. A competitor climbing a boulder shoulder rose 1.33 m in one step, so its deck reported 319 m/s and 54 rad/s; the ball left at 619 m/s, `world.heat` jumped 62 kJ per contact, the temperature pinned the drive at the 135 °C cutoff and the rig sat still next to its ball — the "autopilot stall". The surface step is now clamped to the body's own limits (`speedNow()` horizontal, `config.platformDropRate` vertical, `config.platformPitchRate` angular), which is exactly what a pose correction is allowed to be: the ball is still shoved by a rising deck, but at deck speed. The same clamp covers the rig's own boulder climbs, which could inject the same energy through the piston solve.
- Tests: `experiments/balls.test.js` (10) — the default arrangement, the two shared balls in the ledger, ownership in `each`, no foreign-ball contact in either direction, the competitor's deck bounce/pulse/scoop, the competitor mining the ceiling for its own purse, the competitor chase and its refusal to chase the rig ball, the focused-ball autopilot, arrangement switching with an exact ledger, and the pose-jump clamp. `experiments/interface.test.js` gained the BALLS-chamber test (26) and lost a positional `help.topics[3]` reference in favour of `help.find('movement')`, because inserting the `balls` topic shifted the index.
