# 0.1.2 — Newtonian contacts and mineral debris

## Implementation

- Fixed stored-ball horizontal momentum: captures exchange momentum with the platform, the held ball contributes to platform effective mass, and release inherits platform velocity rather than resetting horizontal speed.
- Added friction-limited tangential impulse at reflector contacts, exchanging horizontal momentum between the moving deck and ball. Energy dissipated by tangential slip is accounted as heat; vertical restitution remains calibrated to 90% normal-energy retention.
- Added a seeded, smooth-noise mineral ceiling field with normalized three-component composition, strength per cell, and bounded debris storage. Ball impacts apply radial falloff damage funded from ceiling-impact dissipation; failed cells emit fragments. Fragments fall under gravity and can be caught in the collector region; uncaught fragments are recorded as lost. The simulation exposes extracted/caught/lost mass and cargo value counters for the next UI/economy step.
- Added cell damage/debris rendering and direct-file script inclusion. Updated current-scope documentation.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: 13/13 pass, covering deterministic normalized ceiling composition, damage/fracture, energy accounting, release momentum, moving-platform bounce exchange, collision bounds, and replay determinism.
- `node --check js/main.js`, `node --check js/physics.js`, `node --check js/ceiling.js`, and `git diff --check`: pass.
