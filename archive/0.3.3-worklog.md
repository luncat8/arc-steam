# 0.3.3 — Infinite upper boundary

## Decisions

- Track `upperBoundaryY` across all columns as the highest exposed free face
  plus any contiguous collapse-ready rock (`damageField >= collapseLooseFloor`
  in normal rock) above that face.
- Measure required upper headroom from `maxActiveDamageRadius(world) * cell`
  across the rig and active balls (`8.775` cells stock up to `10.175` cells for
  the spiked hammer ball), ensuring at least one full damage radius of rock
  remains below `top = 15 m` whenever lower clearance permits.
- Add a short (`0.35 s`) upward free-flight lookahead in `watchBoundary(world)`:
  when a flying ball will reach a high shaft roof within the lookahead window,
  request `1.5 * radiusMeters` below `top` so new rows begin sliding in before
  impact, while clamping against low intact rock so fast balls under unmined
  rock never false-trigger.
- Share `queuedShift` and `stepGrowth(dt)` with bounce-driven growth (0.3.2):
  `watchBoundary(world)` only tops `queuedShift` up by `neededShift - queuedShift`
  when positive, preventing double-counting and keeping the combined insertion
  rate strictly at or below `5` rows/s.
- Preserve the non-destructive 70% chamber-height cap (`minBottom = 4.5 m`,
  30% open height below): if `lowestIntactY() <= 4.5 m`, neither bounce growth
  nor upper-boundary replenishment destroys lower cells or adds new lines until
  lower rock is mined clear.
