# 0.4.0 Wild Mine — ball friction sleep + M4 streaming

## 1. Review of the P2 ball upgrades

The previous milestone added mid-air spin windup and Coulomb bounce friction.
On a flat open shelf that already worked: motor-off rolling resistance of
`0.35 m/s²` stops a 1 m/s coast in ~3 s, and a spinning-at-rest ball couples
into a roll then sleeps.

The bug the player hit is different. The start-room wall (and any cave face
with `ny <= 0`) stole the floor contact:

- `rollStep` bailed to `airStep` whenever the SDF normal was a wall.
- `resolveContact` returned without friction when `v_n >= 0` (sitting still).
- `step` only re-entered `rollStep` while `contact === CONTACT_ROCK`.

After a wall touch the flag dropped to `CONTACT_NONE`, `ω` froze in air, and a
ball sitting on land against the face spun forever with the engine off.

## 2. What was implemented

### Floor-supported roll (`js/wild-ball.js`)

- If the SDF reports a wall/overhang but `floorBelow` still supports the
  centre, resolve the wall hit then keep Coulomb friction and rolling
  resistance on the floor tangent.
- A nearly-still ball (`|vy| <= leaveSpeed` and a floor gap inside
  `contactSlop`) re-enters `rollStep` even when the contact flag was cleared,
  so leftover spin after a bounce is damped on landing.
- Rolling resistance now also runs at zero speed (so a coupled `ω` with
  `v_t = 0` snaps to rest). Gravity load for the friction cone is
  `max(0, ny)`.
- Motor-off sleep: once both centre speed and rim speed fall under
  `restSpeed = 0.04 m/s` on a supporting face, `v` and `ω` snap to 0 and the
  leftover kinetic is booked as heat.

### M4 streaming under motion (`js/wild-mine.js`)

Stream the union of the camera, platform and ball (still 1 fresh chunk per
frame) so a ball that sprints ahead of the follow still has rock. Far-field
scale and the generation budget were already in M1/M2.

## 3. Validation

- `node --test experiments/wild-mine.test.js` → 19 / 19 pass, including
  `M3 friction: motor-off spin on land and against a wall dies instead of
  freewheeling` and `M4 streaming: a 12 m/s window stays in budget, evicts
  clean chunks, and restores edits`.
- `experiments/cave.test.js`, `wild-mine-interface.test.js`,
  `physics.test.js`, `balls.test.js` → 80 / 80 pass.
- Probe: roll into the start-room wall at 2 m/s. Before: `ω` froze at 5.2 rad/s
  against the face. After: bounce, land, rolling resistance, sleep at t ≈ 6 s
  with `vx = 0`, `ω = 0`, ledger drift `~6e-11 J`.
