## Water Solver Optimization (`js/water.js`)

Added wet-row bounds (`rowWet`, `rowSweep`, `wetRowMin`, `wetRowMax`,
`hasParcels`, `colPile`) with zero per-step allocations:
- `scanWetRows()` keeps the dry-chamber fast path intact (`~7.5–12.8 µs/step`)
  and bounds active wet rows (`wetRowMin..wetRowMax`) when parcels exist.
- `stepFluid()` copies and sweeps only `rowSweep` rows (`rowWet ± 2`) instead
  of all 60 × 96 parcels and all 14 mineral channels per parcel.
- `rebuildSolidMask()`, `dissolveContacts()`, `measureMetrics()`, and `draw()`
  skip empty rows and cache column rubble heights in `colPile`.

**2,400-step parity & speedup verification**:
- `floor puddle`: bit-exact parity (`water 99.9599`, `brine 0.0000`, `cells 0`,
  `pumped 0.0000`); `1072.99 ms` (`447.08 µs/step`) → **`264.91 ms` (`110.38 µs/step`, 4.05× faster)**.
- `active gun+pump`: bit-exact parity (`water 3.2385`, `brine 0.7937`, `cells 9`,
  `pumped 22.2615`); `1253.57 ms` (`522.32 µs/step`) → **`588.40–1019.72 ms` (`245.17–424.88 µs/step`, 1.23–2.13× faster)**.

## Optional Solute-Aware Pump Mode (`js/water.js`, UI, `experiments/water.test.js`)

Implemented `settings.pumpMode` (`'always' | 'solute'`), `setPumpMode(mode)`,
and optional UI toggle `#water-pump-solute` (`Idle pump on shallow fresh water`)
in `index.html`, `js/main.js`, `js/main_input.js`, and `js/main_readouts.js`.
When `'solute'` is selected, `stepPump(dt)` leaves shallow (`<= 0.45` units/col)
and nearly fresh (`loadTotal / max(held, 0.05) < 0.002`) floor water unpumped
while lifting brine-bearing parcels and deep flood excess.

**6-seed A/B evaluation (`pumpMode`: `'always'` vs. `'solute'`)**:
- **Parked 30 s (`x=12`, T1 electric)**: `'solute'` reduced pumped water from
  `35.6 u` (`5.34 kJ`) to `22.1 u` (`3.32 kJ`, `-38%`) and raised `pumpCr` from
  `8.5 cr` to `9.9 cr` (`+16%`) because saved reserve went into jet shots.
- **Active 120 s BOT (`T2 electric`, 6 seeds)**: `'always'` outperformed
  `'solute'` (`grossCr 1255.9` vs `827.9` at `0.002` / `676.9` at `0.02`;
  `pumpCr 471.0` vs `333.2` / `351.5`), because moving rigs scatter dilute
  brine across wide pools (`wLeft 123.1 u`, `brineLeft 16.7 kg`) and skip it on
  the first pass. On heat-limited `T2 coal`, `'solute'` cut pump energy slightly
  at high flood risk when cooling pauses stacked water.
- **Decision**: keep **`'always'` as default**, expose `'solute'` as an opt-in
  setting/checkbox (`#water-pump-solute`) for players who want stationary rinse
  power savings, and pin it with a dedicated unit test in `experiments/water.test.js`.
