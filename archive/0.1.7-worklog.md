# 0.1.7 — Live state API, response tuning, and thermal controls

## Decisions

- Replaced per-read `SteamCeiling.state()` snapshots and nested counter functions with one stable `SteamCeiling.state` live view. Scalar counters use property getters; commands remain methods. This removes per-read object/closure allocation, especially from the render loop, and makes reads distinct from actions. Applied the same property-based state pattern to cached installed-equipment deltas.
- Slowed the ball's free-flight cycle by 1.5× without reducing its energy budget or bounce height: effective ball inertia is 2.25 kg and its gravity is 9.81 / 2.25 m/s², so its speed at a given energy/height is two-thirds and flight time is 1.5×. Debris remains at 9.81 m/s².
- Increased platform force from 180 N to 270 N. The 7 m/s cap stays unchanged; greater force improves acceleration and reversals rather than increasing top speed.
- Promoted the previous heat counter to stored thermal energy above 20 °C. A 300 J/K reservoir cools by convection and Stefan–Boltzmann radiation; exported cooling is tracked in the total-energy balance. Powered movement and bounce pulses derate linearly from 85 °C to a 135 °C cutoff, with full braking retained.
- Added a 650 cr radiator-array purchase (+8 kg, 0.35 m² exposed area), whose area increases both convective and radiative cooling. Added live temperature, stored heat, cooled-energy, and thermal-output feedback to the HUD.

## Implementation

- `js/ceiling.js`: stable frozen `state` property with live scalar getters and existing typed-array buffers; removed query-like state/funds/cargo/energy methods from the public API. Rock traces accept the ball's gravity so swept contacts match the tuned ballistic trajectory.
- `js/equipment.js`: cached state deltas, radiator item, and inelastic coupling of added mass to a moving platform with the kinetic-energy difference booked as heat.
- `js/physics.js`: separate ball-specific mass/gravity tuning, stronger platform force, thermal capacity/cooling/derating, cooling-energy accounting, and thermal checks for powered launches and bounces.
- `js/main.js`, `index.html`, `styles.css`: property-based state reads, equipment list access, temperature and cooling telemetry, and thermal status/launch affordance.
- Updated numerical, equipment, collision diagnostic, and browser-smoke tests, plus the standalone plan and README.
- Added measured run output at `experiments/logs/0.1.7-mine-trips.txt`.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: 35/35 pass, including live-state identity, 1.5× ball flight tuning, 1.5× platform force, exact thermal export accounting, overheating/derating, and radiator effectiveness.
- `node experiments/rock-impulse-check.cjs`: 7,239,822 traces, zero impulse amplifications/non-unit normals, zero stacked impulses, and no shape-switch tunneling.
- `experiments/mine-trips.cjs` (8 seeds per rock shape, 240 s cap): max ball speed averaged 13.6 m/s in smooth and 13.4 m/s in square mode. Average sale value was 275 cr/min smooth and 177 cr/min square under the scripted policy. Thermal load became visible: mean per-run peak temperature was 93.7 °C in smooth mode and 116.2 °C in square mode; mean per-run minimum drive output was 81% and 38%, respectively. These are calibration measurements, not a final economic balance claim.
- `node --check` on application and experiment scripts; `git diff --check`: pass.
- Browser smoke is updated but could not run because Playwright is not installed in this environment.
