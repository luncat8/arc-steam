Key findings from the review:

1. **The jet, not the pump, dominated draw (`81.5%` vs `18.5%`).** At `135 J ×
   46/s = 6.21 kW` (`6.48 kW` at 240 Hz step rounding) versus `420 J/u ×
   2.6 u/s = 1.09 kW`, `0.3.10-Water-worklog.md` had the share inverted. Still,
   both were oversized: even the pump alone drew ~1.1 kW on barren floodwater,
   and combined `~7.6 kW` exceeded even a tier-3 heavy radiator bank (`3.72 kW`
   at `85 °C`, `6.68 kW` at `134 °C`), cooking parked pad rigs to `458 °C` and
   upgraded T3 rigs to `150–192 °C`.
2. **Pre-existing water and nozzle defects found:**
   - `water.js` `stepPump` never checked `hasTool('rig')` (a rig with no water
     gun still pumped and billed floor water) and never capped lifted volume by
     `world.battery`, fabricating `+6.75 kJ` of free `importedEnergy` once the
     reserve hit `0 J`.
   - `water.js` `stepPump` added pumped solute mass to a moving platform without
     conserving horizontal momentum, leaking `+0.5 · dm · vx²` into
     `totalEnergy` on every moving pump step (tens to hundreds of joules over a
     shift), and `catchGrain` read `machineMass` after `loadWetCargo`,
     double-counting grain mass in the merge.
   - `water.js` `step` rebuilt the 5,760-parcel solid mask and swept all fluid
     buffers at 240 Hz even in completely dry chambers (~25× slowdown).
   - `sandblaster.js` and `water.js` deducted raw `shotEnergy` instead of
     routing through `engine.efficiency` (letting 20%-efficient `coal`'s bloated
     raw fuel pack run nozzles as cheaply as electric with zero motor waste
     heat) and kept firing right through the `135 °C` thermal cutoff.
   - `players.js` lacked `water.clearBotFire` on BOT shutdown, lacked
     `botPaused` and thermal gating on the rig water gun, omitted water+pump
     draw from the charge-trip safety lead, and forced `gun.setPump(true)` every
     tick (overwriting a player's unchecked Pump toggle).
3. **Pre-change thermal curve failed at both ends:** bare stock `1.1 W/K`
   (`106 W` at `85 °C`) overheated even a gentle starter smart electric shift
   (`111 °C`, 48% output) and a hand crank (`95 °C` from fallen-debris heat
   alone), while `72 W/(m²·K)` radiators could not carry sustained multi-tool
   operation on T3 + `bank`.

## 2. Changes implemented

- **Unified engine-efficiency & thermal-cutoff law (`js/physics.js`,
  `js/sandblaster.js`, `js/water.js`):**
  - Exported `physics.reserveCost(useful, owner)` and `physics.spend(world,
    useful)` (with exact battery clamping). Every reserve-powered tool —
    movement, piston, road grader, sandblaster, water gun and brine pump — now
    debits `useful / engine.efficiency`, books motor waste into `world.heat`,
    and pauses at the `135 °C` thermal cutoff (braking, chemical rockets and the
    empty-reserve crew hand crank remain available).
  - Kept `sandblaster` at `135 J` useful/grain × `20/s` (`2.70 kW` useful;
    `168.75 J` / `3.38 kW` on stock 80% electric, `4.91 kW` on oil/nuclear,
    `13.50 kW` on coal).
  - Rebalanced `water` to `shotEnergy = 36 J` useful/droplet × `46/s` (`1.66 kW`
    useful; `45 J` / `2.07 kW` stock electric) and `pumpJoulesPerUnit = 120 J`
    useful/unit × `2.6 u/s` (`0.31 kW` useful; `150 J/u` / `0.39 kW` stock
    electric, `2.46 kW` combined, keeping the ~`82 / 18%` jet/pump split).
  - Added `streamPower(owner)` on both `SteamSandblaster` and `SteamWater`
    (including pump draw on `'rig'` when `pumpOn` is set).
- **Water ledger, pump guards & dry fast path (`js/water.js`):**
  - Gated `stepPump` on `hasTool('rig')`, `thermalFactor(world) > 0`, and
    affordable reserve volume (zero unbacked `importedEnergy`).
  - Added inelastic momentum conservation + heat booking in `stepPump` when
    lifting floor brine into a moving rig, and moved `machineMass` before
    `loadWetCargo` in `catchGrain`.
  - Added bit-exact `chamberWet()` guard in `water.step` so dry runs skip
    5,760-parcel mask rebuilds and fluid copies.
  - Added `botFire` / `botManaged` / `botPaused` to `water.js` matching
    `sandblaster.js`.
- **Cooling ladder (`js/physics.js`, `js/research.js`):**
  - Raised bare `convectiveConductance` `1.1 → 3.6 W/K` (`269 W` at `85 °C`),
    `thermodynamics.coolingBonus` `0.5 → 1.4 W/K` (`5.00 W/K` live), and
    `radiatorConvectionCoefficient` `72 → 140 W/(m²·K)` (`vent` `26.00 W/K`,
    `radiator` `54.00 W/K`, `bank` `103.00 W/K`, keeping the `4 / 8 / 16 kg`
    mass trade-offs).
- **BOT policy, readouts, docs & plan (`js/players.js`, `js/main_readouts.js`,
  `js/equipment.js`, `js/help.js`, `README.md`, `0.3.8-plan.md`):**
  - Wired `clearRigNozzles` + rival water cleanup on BOT off, shared thermal
    hysteresis (`80 °C` pause / `65 °C` resume), combined active sand+water
    charge-trip safety lead, and stopped overwriting `pumpOn`.
  - Updated sandblast and water HUD status lines for engine-efficiency reserve
    cost, thermal cutoff, and BOT pause/underpowered/cooling states.
  - Synced shop, help and README descriptions, and expanded `0.3.8-plan.md` into
    a standalone tool/engine/storage/thermal progression specification.

## 3. Validation & measured results

- **Water split & yield (`experiments/water-energy-split.cjs`,
  `experiments/logs/0.3.11-water-energy-split.txt`, `experiments/water-yield-probe.cjs`):**
  - T1 stock electric off-pad (`n = 36`): `401` drops (`8.34 s` burst to 20%
    reserve floor), `18.05 kJ` jet + `4.43 kJ` pump (`80.3 / 19.7%`), `0.00 kJ`
    unbacked import, cells `4` med / `10.7` mean, cargo `6.3` med / `35.6 cr`
    mean, `Tmax = 89 °C`, ledger drift `≤ 3.1e-9 J`.
  - T1 on-pad (`n = 6`): cleanly stops at `135 °C` thermal cutoff (`Tmax 136 °C`,
    `49%` cutoff vs `458 °C` previously). No-tool pump draws `0.00 kJ`.
  - Standard `water-yield-probe.cjs` (`n = 42`): cells `8` med / `13.1` mean (up
    from `4 / 8.3`), paid-fuel profit `-476.9 cr` med / `-585.3 cr` mean — in
    direct parity with `sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs` (`12` med / `14.0` mean
    cells, `-481.8 cr` med / `-544.6 cr` mean paid-fuel profit).
  - Upgraded T3 electric + `bank` off-pad (`n = 12`): runs the full 30 s off a
    single pack (`1,440` drops, `76.05 kJ` of `207.9 kJ`), cells `25` med /
    `22.4` mean, cargo `68.6` med / `78.9 cr` mean on free pad energy,
    `Tmax = 45 °C`, `0%` cutoff, drift `4.7e-10 J`.
- **Stage & engine diversity (`experiments/tool-stage-probe.cjs`,
  `experiments/logs/0.3.11-tool-stage-balance.txt`):**
  - **S1 bare:** electric smart move+piston stays at `74 °C` (`Tmax 81 °C`, `0%`
    hot, `100%` output); aggressive chase (`92 °C`) and bare grader (`92 °C`)
    dip into mild derate until `thermodynamics` (`55 °C`, `0%` hot); bare coal
    chase hits `108 °C` (`40%` hot) until `vent` (`55 °C`, `0%` hot); hand
    spends `56%` on pad (`0.3 kJ` storage limit). Off-pad T1 nozzle bursts stop
    in `0.05–0.17 s` (hand, reserve), `1.55–3.63 s` (coal, cutoff), `3.20–6.88 s`
    (oil, reserve), `3.90–8.34 s` (electric, reserve), and `4.19–10.09 s`
    (nuclear, bare-hull cutoff).
  - **S2 midgame:** `T2 + vent + grader` runs `45–54 °C` (`100%` output) on
    electric/oil/nuclear (`63 °C` on coal). `T2 + radiator + grader + 1 nozzle`
    runs continuously with distinct limits: electric (`1,235` sand / `3,483`
    water, `11–16%` thermal pause, `12–16%` pad), oil (`7–10%` pad, `33–50%`
    thermal pause), nuclear (`10–11%` pad, `37–51%` thermal pause), coal
    (`79–87%` thermal pause).
  - **S3 endgame (`T3 + bank + grader + rockets + sandblaster + water + pump +
    spiked ball`):** full 90 s shifts sustain all tools with `0%` thermal cutoff
    on electric (`50 °C` BOT / `72 °C` unthrottled auto, `98–100%` drive
    output), oil (`55 °C` BOT / `82 °C` auto, `6–11%` pad), and nuclear (`63 °C`
    BOT / `78 °C` auto, `4–10%` pad), while coal still hits thermal limits under
    dual nozzles (`72%` BOT pause, `82%` unthrottled cutoff). Every shift closed
    `totalEnergy` to `≤ 1.6e-7 J`.
- **Automated suite:** `node --check js/*.js` clean and `node --test
  experiments/*.test.js` passed all 434 tests (including new pump, efficiency,
  thermal-cutoff, moving-hull ledger, BOT water management and S1–S3 progression
  regressions).

## 4. Suggested next step

Measure full 240 s progression-shift income across multiple seeds with all ten
minerals, top-row replenishment, rockets, sandblaster and water gun active
(`0.3.8-plan.md`), then verify whether late-tier prices (`nuclear`, `bank`,
`overdrive`, `watergun`, `ballistics2`) and an optional solute-aware pump mode
(skipping or low-idling suction when under-hull parcels carry near-zero solute
and low flood depth) improve mid-to-late-game pacing. Also consider bounding
`water.js`'s active row range during wet steps so multi-tool shifts stay as fast
wet as they now are dry.
