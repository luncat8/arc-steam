# players.2 — same platform, live tails, real player switch

- Rebuilt `js/players.js` around one body for every player. The competitor is no longer a low 3.2 m box: it is the rig's own platform — `physics.config.platformWidth`, the same body bottom and top (0.2 / 0.6), the same wheels, deck and plates — told apart by a dim, warmer colour and by its load heap instead of the collector, piston, fuel bar and thermometer.
- Rig machinery moved out of `main.js`'s inline chassis block into `drawRigMachinery(context)`, handed to `SteamPlayers.draw(context, extras)` as an overlay drawn inside the rig's own body transform. One body pass for every player, sorted by height so the platform riding on top covers the one underneath; the rig no longer wins the draw order by being drawn last.
- Tails are now retractable plates on both ends of every body. A plate answers only a platform on its own side of the body, only while that gap is closing and shorter than `tailReach = 2.6 m`, and its target angle is `clamp((reach − gap) / (reach − full))` with `tailFullGap = 0.6 m`. The angle is rate-limited to 90°/s, so the plate swings down as a platform closes in and folds as it leaves. The collision profile is `bodyTop − (|dx| − half) / tan(θ)`, i.e. the plate line; drawing and wheel contact read the same line, and the plate is clipped where it meets the floor.
- The rig no longer always rides on top. `powerOf(player)` is the player's own force setting while it is commanding movement (direction, travel target, or a bot policy) and zero while coasting or braking. `above(a, b)` compares power, ties keeping the lower index up. `surfaceAt` only returns bodies the asking platform outranks, one way only, so the stronger platform climbs the other's plate and deck while the weaker stays on the floor — no pair can lift itself step by step.
- Competitors now drive the rig's engine: acceleration `config.force / config.platformMass × force`, top speed `config.maxSpeed × force`, wheel bounds `platformMin()`/`platformMax()`; they settle with `platformBaseAt(x, angle, ownId)` so they inherit the same terrain, grader and body query.
- Force setting is per player. `SteamPlayers.setForce` writes the selected player (and mirrors to `world.forceSetting` for the rig); the slider and W/S read `playerApi.force(active)`. `syncForceControl` reads the selected player's value whenever the controls sync, so switching back restores the rig's number.
- Panel now names the platform it drives: `#control-title` reads `PLATFORM / LOW-TIER DRIVE` or `RIVAL 1 / PLATFORM DRIVE`, the new `#player-focus` line reads `DRIVING RIVAL 1 · FORCE 90% · LOAD 0 CELLS · BOT`, and `#competitor-note` states that ball, piston, autopilot and telemetry stay with the rig. `setForce` refreshes that line immediately instead of waiting for the readout.
- Unscoped ground queries (`platformGroundAt(x)` with no id, used by the autopilot and the prediction) resolve to the rig, so a platform never stands on itself; this was the cause of the autopilot's intercept mark drifting by a body height.
- `experiments/interface.test.js` gained a `parkRival()` helper (used by the autopilot test) because a live competitor sweeping the floor now legitimately moves the rig through the ride-over mechanic.
- `experiments/players.test.js` rewritten to 12 tests: one body for every player, deployment as a function of the gap, the rate limit, power ordering in both directions, rig riding over, competitor riding over a parked rig, harvest conservation, sweep-and-sell, manual travel, held keys, rig selling trip, per-player force on switch.
- Validation:
	- `node --check js/*.js` — clean.
	- `node --test experiments/*.test.js` — 129 / 129 passing.
	- `git diff --check` — clean.
	- Offscreen canvas renders (`@napi-rs/canvas` outside the repo) of four gaps: folded plates apart, a half-open plate on approach, the rig climbing, the rig on top. The plate is always the line the wheels meet.
