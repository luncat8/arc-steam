# 0.3.1 — Delayed ceiling collapse

## What changed

- `js/ceiling.js`: impacts no longer spend a cell's whole strength in one blow. `damage()` caps per-cell removal at half of the cell's remaining strength and records the loosened share in a new `damageField` (0..1, exposed via `state`). The hanging-rock cleanup loop can now only fire on cells already spent to zero, so it no longer opens craters at once.
- The ceiling is divided into 12 × 6 collapse regions. A slow clock (0.25 s) examines every region each tick; a region whose mean looseness of *touched* rock passes a floor rolls with a probability that rises with that looseness, and a successful roll detaches a bounded clump (1..4 cells) of its loosest exposed cells.
- Only cells that are exposed (their own column's surface or a neighbour already gone) may detach, so a collapse always opens the face the player can see and never pays out as invisible rubble.
- A fresh gap unloads the rock beside it: the fallen clump's neighbours shed half their looseness, so one battered spot cannot grind the whole band away pass after pass.
- `collapse(seconds)` is exposed on the API so a measurement or test can turn loosened rock into falling rock without running the whole simulation.
- `main.js` inspector: the strength readout marks a cell `loose` when its damage is high enough to let it go.
- `README.md` and `0.1.0-plan.md` updated to describe the delayed collapse instead of instant destruction.

## Decisions

- A blow spends at most half of a cell's remaining strength; the contact cells stay standing and the ring around them is left loose. Rock falls in local groups over the seconds after the hit, and a well-battered pocket collapses more often. The "1–2 cells per hit" the damage curve was tuned to still holds for the cells a blow actually finishes.
- Region looseness is the mean damage of the rock that has actually been hit, not the mean over the whole band, so a well-battered pocket counts as much as a band that is loose everywhere.
- The collapse is bounded: one pass over the field per tick, a clump never larger than `collapseChunkMax`, and a cell is never detached twice.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js` — 191 / 194 pass. The 3 failures (`autopilot.test.js` 5 and 6, `blade` once per stint) fail on the clean baseline too; they are pre-existing and unrelated to the collapse.
- `experiments/collapse-probe.cjs`: a 40 J hit extracts 65 cells immediately and 80 after 60 s of collapse; 40 × 3 J hits extract 314 cells; a 200 J hit extracts 108 immediately and nothing more (its ring is not yet loose enough).
- Real shifts extract 350–440 cells in 180 s, comparable to before.
