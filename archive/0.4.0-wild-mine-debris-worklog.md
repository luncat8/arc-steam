# Wild Mine — ledge soil and fracture

## Diagnosis

- The previous pass had already separated flying grains from settled bins. But painting a rectangle up to each occupied bin’s midpoint left the sloped crest unfilled and its shoulders invisible. Nearby elevations could blend across a gap, and the grader moved height/value without moving the associated cells.
- A blow could take at most half the remaining strength, yet release waited for strength below 0.0001 J. The M3 probe needed fourteen strong drops on one spot. The damage byte could also round small hits away. Soil contacts bypassed fracture entirely.
- Shards could damage the cave, but the parent rocket still used the Classic ceiling to place its burst. Only the ball consumed the list of broken cells. The cargo panel’s extraction and falling counters also read the Classic run.

## Changes

- Keep cells and break-time value per occupied `(absolute column, support row)`. Derive thickness from the live DEBRIS factor. Cache the five-column footprint of each bin’s 1–3–3–1 stencil; use the same two vertices for the filled strip, outline, contact normal, sweeps, rig ground and grain landing. An empty shoulder does not need a material bin.
- Follow a one-cell stair only when rock and air both connect and the lower soil fills to its lip. End the strip at a shaft or disconnected shelf; limit it to the local overhang. Compression and a direct dig or fracture invalidate the local geometry. A mined support immediately re-seats its soil. The blade moves and cuts cells at their actual value density. No ore stones or resting particle bodies.
- Apply gain once to the incoming fracture budget. Focus part of a bare hit on the contact cell, distribute the rest by falloff, and keep the per-cell half-strength cap. Release exposed rock below the shared 0.8 J strength / at least 0.3 looseness thresholds. Hold buried material without healing it. Snapshot free faces before opening any and refresh the SDF only for actual holes. Accumulate fractional looseness without byte rounding.
- A crest hit sends at most one fifth of the normal fracture budget into the backing rock over four times the radius. Other bounce loss still goes to heat. Ball and shard removals use the same post-edit notification: each released cell becomes one catchable grain. The bounded flight pool settles a displaced grain rather than overwriting its mass/value. Deliberate `dig` edits re-seat soil without spawning a second mining yield.
- Reserve one hopper slot per caught grain and transfer its full mineral mix and locked value together. Loading a grain mineral-by-mineral had let the final share fail the capacity check after earlier shares were accepted. In the seed-1 last-slot regression the old path reported a catch but left the hopper at **99.651771 cells**; it now ends at **100**, and the next grain settles intact.
- Use the active cave for rocket fuses, dud landings and projected flight. Show Wild Mine looseness and mining counters from the wild run. The DEBRIS selector reflects the actual 1.5:1 default and remembers the Classic and Wild choices separately.

## Validation

- Seed 1, x = 2.8 m: three stock-speed (3.2 m/s) drops opened the shelf and deposited its first cell. The remaining strength after each strike was 2.49, 1.25, then 0 J. Soft rolls still do no fracture work.
- Real Chromium, `file://`, seed 1: a round fired from the start platform burst at y = 33.5 m. After three simulated seconds its 24 shard contacts had opened **67 cells: 12 caught, 55 in ledge piles, none airborne or lost**. Energy error was below 2e-10 J. Checked the same scene at compression 1, 1.5 and 8; each rendered the soil fill. Browser tools and screenshots were kept outside the repository.
- Regression coverage: stacked and offset shelves, buried steps, cut supports, local roof overflow, negative/chunk-seam columns, compression, grading/harvesting, actual projected fill and outline, diffuse contact from an overlap, a full flight pool, last-slot collection, and the extraction-to-cargo/soil loop. The recycling comparison uses a real flat soil bed so excavating its landing site does not change the comparison geometry.

- `node --test --test-concurrency=2 experiments/*.test.js experiments/*.test.cjs`: **537 passed, 0 failed or skipped**. Chromium `experiments/browser-smoke.cjs` also passed without page errors; the separate Wild launch/render check above exercised the cave-specific path. `git diff --check` was clean.

**Next suggested step:** playtest lateral slumping at tall ledge ends. The crest currently tapers on supporting rock; only mining a support or working the grader transports settled material. Any future spill over an edge should move cells and their value, not just extend the drawn silhouette.
