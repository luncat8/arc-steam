# 0.4.0 Wild Mine — aimed bore (`Hammer buffers`) worklog

## Review of prior slices and fixes

- `js/wild-ball.js` bounce recycling measured the normal incoming speed in the world frame rather than the surface frame. On a deck moving horizontally with a tilted reflector, `recycle()` could harvest or waste fictitious normal energy relative to the deck. Normal contact speed is now measured relative to the moving surface velocity while keeping exact world-frame kinetic conservation.
- `js/wild-ball.js` compressed the piston animation on passive underside/back-face ball contacts and ticked `pistonOffset` on zero-pulse launches from an empty reserve or thermal cutoff. Both now trigger only on actual front-face returns and positive funded pulses.
- `js/physics.js` `createWorld()` now resets `SteamRocketFab` and `SteamWildBore` alongside the other per-run modules so run resets cannot carry leftover tool timers or in-flight cut state.
- `js/equipment.js` (`atShop`) and `js/main_progress.js` allowed research and equipment anywhere in Wild Mine while still checking Classic's left shop wall (`platformMin() + 0.2`, unreachable in Wild Mine where `platformMin()` is `-Infinity`) for buying or swapping engines. Wild Mine now allows engine purchases and swaps whenever the rig is stopped (`|vx| <= 0.1 m/s`) anywhere in the cave; Classic keeps its stopped-at-left-shop rule untouched.

## Slice 4 — Aimed bore (`Hammer buffers`, D16–D20)

- Updated `0.4.0-wild-mine-tools-plan.md` with Slice 4 decisions (`D16–D20`) and moved the sloped/smoother road grader and mining automation into `Later slices`.
- Added `bore` (**Hammer buffers**, `620 cr`, `+14 kg`, `requires: 'impact'`, rig-only) to `js/equipment.js`, sitting in the Impact forging branch (`180 + 320 + 620 = 1,120 cr` full path from a fresh run).
- Implemented `js/wild-bore.js` (`SteamWildBore`):
  - Twin percussion buffer heads mounted on the left and right noses of the Wild Mine platform; inert in Classic and when unfitted.
  - Clears a `1.125 m` passage (`1.5 × 0.75 m` platform clearance, 9 cells high) extending `1.125 m` (9 columns) ahead of the active nose at grades from `-30°` to `+30°` (`maxGrade = π/6`).
  - Uses vertical-column parallelogram bounds (`floorY = anchorY + side * (wx - anchorX) * tan θ`, `roofY = floorY + tunnelHeight / cos θ`) with a locked heading anchor so climbing cuts never flatten rising steps and descending cuts never leave an overhang wedge at the bumper. On descending breaks, an overhead-only collar spanning `platformWheelHalfSpan` (`1.6 m`) trims the convex lip above the live tilted deck while adding zero cells on level, climbing, or steady-slope runs.
  - Draws up to `borePower = 1,800 W` useful work from the rig reserve via `physics.thermalFactor(world)` (respecting thermal derating above `95 °C` and cutoff at `140 °C`), paying `workPerStrength = 14 J` per remaining strength joule (`cost = remainingStrength * 14 / engine.efficiency`). Removed rock strength books to `world.fractureEnergy`, while percussion loss and engine waste book to `world.heat`, keeping `totalEnergy(world)` conserved to floating-point precision.
  - Pulverises cut cells without spawning cargo, floor rubble, or new cave seeps, avoiding both corridor self-choking and a net-positive stone-fuel loop. Exposed existing seeps flow normally, and loose soil resting on opened ledges drops and settles onto the new floor via `SteamWildRubble.dropAbove`.
- Added `cave.boreCells` and exported `cave.solidCell` in `js/cave.js`. Partial cell weakening scales into `chunk.damage` with the same susceptibility curve as `chipCell`, so pre-fractured rock cuts for proportionally less reserve and partial hammer progress persists cleanly across chunk eviction.
- Wired `SteamWildBore` reset and stepping in `js/wild-mine.js`, and taught Wild `groundAt` to skip up to 3 solid cells of low tunnel roof above open air so `platformPitchAt` follows bored slopes instead of snapping to overhead rock.
- Added `#wild-bore-controls` (`Bore V` hold button, `Cursor aim` toggle, `-30°..+30°` `Tunnel grade` slider, `Auto-bore ahead while driving` checkbox, live cell/reserve readout, and `bore` context-help topic) across `index.html`, `styles.css`, `js/help.js`, `js/main.js`, `js/main_input.js`, `js/main_readouts.js`, `js/main_wild.js`, and `js/main_draw.js` (twin bumper heads with animated stroke/glow plus dashed cave clearance guide). Hidden and unbound in Classic.

## Balance and calibration

`node experiments/wild-bore-probe.cjs` reproduces the full benchmark in [experiments/logs/wild-bore-balance.txt](../experiments/logs/wild-bore-balance.txt): 81-cell (`1.125 × 1.125 m`) full-face cuts across three natural strata vs measured piston excavation, all five powertrains on host stone, 14 s auto-bore chamber breakouts across four seeds, and opening-room cart affordability across seven seeds.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Halite (seed 1) | 1.76 | 1.12 | 2.50 · 7.9% | 30.8 | 105.0 / 70.0 | 28 |

| Corundum (seed 20) | 7.16 | 4.51 | 10.14 · 32.3% | 125.2 | 210.0 / 420.0 | 51 |

- At `14 J` useful per strength joule and `1,800 W`, the bore pays strictly by remaining rock strength (`4.07×` more reserve in corundum than halite), beats piston splash on horizontal transit and hard plugs where the piston's per-hit weakening cap stalls, yet costs `1.40×–2.33×` more per host-stone cell than piston mining while yielding zero ore or fuel — keeping piston, ball, and rockets the right tools for harvesting veins.
- Powertrain trade-offs hold on an 81-cell stone cut: electric (`2.65 s`, `5.95 kJ`, `38 °C`), oil (`2.65 s`, `8.66 kJ`, `47 °C`), and nuclear (`2.65 s`, `8.66 kJ`, `47 °C`) run full rate; coal (`2.71 s`, `23.81 kJ`, `97 °C`) begins thermal derating without an upgraded radiator; hand crank (`3.81 s`, `4.91 kJ`, `105 °C`) demands repeated reserves.
- Over 14 s auto-bore runs (`electric` + `radiator` + 30 stone fuel cells), the rig drives smoothly through level, climbing (`+20°`, `+25°`), and descending (`-15°`, `-20°`) tunnels on seeds `1, 2, 5, 20` and breaks into adjacent caverns with active seeps (`4.82 kg` pumped on seed 1 left `-20°`, `6.95 kg` on seed 2 right `0°`, `1.14 kg` on seed 20 left `+25°`).
- Opening-room roofs across seeds `1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20, 24` hold `415–456` cells worth `3,479–12,947 cr` (`830–3,120 cr` per 100-cell cart), funding the entire `1,120 cr` research + Hammer buffers path in `0.36–1.35` cart loads (median `0.94`).
