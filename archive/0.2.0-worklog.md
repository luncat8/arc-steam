# gui.2 / 0.2.0 — Click-first chamber, telemetry warnings, centre charge pad

Sources: `html-layout-draft.md` (click zones), the user's request, and the 0.1.8 open decision on the next energy resupply.

## Review of the previous step

- The 0.1.8 open decision ("which energy era, and how is it refilled") had been carried through gui.1 without an answer. The draft already contained the intended model — a centre charge station that is "just a type of ground that charges according to the engine, 4 s for the whole reserve" — so this milestone builds it rather than inventing a fuel economy.
- The mineral inspector still used two range sliders and displayed "96 / 192", which is not a meaningful coordinate while playing. Clicking rock already selected a cell, so the sliders only duplicated a better control. Removing them also removed the `input` handlers, the value syncing, and `column-label`/`layer-label`.
- A chamber click meant "travel" everywhere, including on rock. That made inspecting a cell also command a drive, which is why the previous smoke test could only prove that both changed together.

## Decisions

- Inspector: clicked cell only. `rockCellIndex(x, y)` hit-tests the drawn smooth profile (linear between the same `heightmap` vertices the renderer uses) or the intact square cell, returns the cell index, and `selectCell(index)` is the single writer for column/layer. The panel reports the location, strength, mineral mix, price and elements.
- Click zones split by height and drawn on the canvas: rock selects, the low band (`y ≤ 1.7 m`, the existing target-marker height) sets travel, open air does neither. The 1.7 m line is visible as the target marker itself; no new control was added.
- Warnings are data attributes on the existing telemetry cards, styled entirely in CSS: reserve `LOW` (< 10%) / `EMPTY` (< 0.5 J), cargo `FULL` (100 cells), thermal `HOT` (any derate) / `CUTOFF` (zero output). `setWarning` writes only on change, so the 10 Hz readout does not churn attributes, and the notes under each value carry the actionable hint.
- Energy resupply is the centre **charge pad**: a 4 m strip of ground at the chamber centre drawn as a hatched plate, 3 kW while the platform stands still on it, filling the 12 kJ reserve in exactly 4 s. No credits, no fuel mass, no purchase — the cost is travel and downtime, which is the position-dependent trade the draft describes.
- The paid recharge stays, relabelled **emergency resupply**: it is the rescue for a platform that cannot reach the pad (typically stranded at a wall with an empty reserve) and would otherwise soft-lock the run. Price stays 25 cr/kJ.
- Charging imports are booked in `world.importedEnergy` like bought joules and additionally counted in `world.chargedEnergy`, so the energy ledger stays exact and the market ledger is untouched (the pad costs no credits).

## Implementation

- `js/physics.js`: `chargePadWidth` (4 m), `chargeSeconds` (4), `chargePower` (reserve / 4), `chargeMaxSpeed` (0.1 m/s), `chargePadMin/Max`, `onChargePad`, `charging`, `charge` called from `step`, `chargedEnergy` counter, exported helpers.
- `js/main.js`: `rockCellIndex`, `selectCell`, `moveClickHeight`, `warningState`, `setWarning`, state-aware reserve/cargo/thermal notes, pad plate and label in `draw`, no slider listeners, pointer handler routes by zone.
- `index.html`: sliders removed, `#mineral-cell`, telemetry ids and notes, charge-pad legend swatch and footer note, emergency-resupply wording.
- `styles.css`: warning card styling (amber `LOW`/`FULL`/`HOT`, red `EMPTY`/`CUTOFF`), pad legend colour, inspector cell line, narrow-screen system-bar wrapping.
- `experiments/physics.test.js`: pad fills 12 kJ in 4 s and books the import; a moving platform and an off-pad platform charge nothing. Existing empty-reserve tests moved off the pad.
- `experiments/economy.test.js`: the empty-reserve stop test runs at `x = 3` so the pad cannot mask it.
- `experiments/interface.test.js`: click-zone test, warnings test, charging test; `fillCargo` and `chamberPoint` helpers mirror the canvas transform.
- `experiments/browser-smoke.cjs`: `chamberClick` helper mirrors the renderer transform; asserts the sliders are gone, a rock click moves only the inspector, a low click commands travel, and the pad note/legend are present.
- `experiments/mine-trips.cjs`: reports charged energy per shift; log in `experiments/logs/0.2.0-mine-trips.txt`.
- `0.1.0-plan.md`, `README.md`, `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`, `0.2.0-plan.md` updated.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: 62/62 pass (physics 22, interface 11, others unchanged).
- Headless Chromium smoke (`playwright-core` + `@sparticuz/chromium`): PASS, no page errors; covers the click zones using world coordinates, the pad note and legend, and the 390 px layout.
- Full-page screenshots at 1440 and 390 px reviewed: no horizontal overflow, inspector fits its column, the canvas lost its previous vertical letterboxing once the extra panel block was removed.
- `experiments/mine-trips.cjs`: the existing policy never deliberately parks on the pad, yet collects 0.88 kJ (smooth) and 1.86 kJ (square) per shift against a 76–82 W draw and 0.6–0.8 paid recharges. The pad is therefore not a free win for a busy policy; it is a free option for a player who deliberately parks.
- `git diff --check` clean; `node --check` on every changed script.
