# 0.2.3 — Tier-II economy intercepts and persistent grader garage

- Tier-II trajectory data now activates the economical, timed movement policy for the regular chase as well as the timed-intercept switch. It begins moving toward a predicted rock contact during the descent and replans against the next predicted contact after each bounce, avoiding the full-throttle rush-and-brake pattern.
- Commercial grader dispatch no longer requires parking at the left shop. Its hire button remains available while the grader is running, with status feedback instead of a parking restriction. The grader remains visible parked in its low right-side garage and through wall turnarounds; draw order keeps it above the garage opening.
- Validation: `node --test experiments/*.test.js` — all 108 tests pass, including tier-II pre-contact/post-bounce movement, open-location grader dispatch, garage rendering, and interface availability.
