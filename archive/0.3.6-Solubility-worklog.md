# 0.3.6 Solubility worklog — prototype upgrade to the updated plan

## Review of the previous prototype

The previous `prototypes/solubility-lab.*` already had the Hybrid fluid grid,
exact two-channel (Halite/Vitriol) accounting, gas loss, and placer panning,
but diverged from the updated `0.3.6-Solubility-plan.md` in load-bearing ways:

- No saturation cap: stagnant brine dissolved at full rate forever, so the
  pump-away-to-keep-cutting loop did not exist.
- Only two solute channels with invented prices (Halite 1.80, Fizzy priced as
  65% Halite + 35% at the gas price), so Fizzy vented 35% and its lost-gas
  value dwarfed its brine by construction instead of by stoichiometry.
- Fluorite dropped Quartz debris (its 45% residue lost its identity), and
  cell detachment used a 22% reactivity cutoff instead of skeleton strength.
- Gold liberation gave Pure Gold 92% of lump value, not the market-exact
  97.35% / 2.65% split; loose soluble grains never dissolved.

## Done

- `prototypes/solubility-lab.js`:
  - Saturation: `satCap` state (default 60 g/wu, lab slider) with
    `saturationFactor = max(0, 1 - totalLoad / (vol * satCap))` multiplying
    every dissolution rate (jet impact, passive contact, submerged grains).
    Fresh jet parcels cut, saturated parcels stall until pumped.
  - Per-mineral brine bookkeeping: fluid cells, cascade/jet droplets, and
    the under-platform hopper all carry `loads[m]` / `loadValues[m]` in
    preallocated `Float64Array(… × 14)` buffers with per-cell totals for
    hot-path skipping. Mineral definitions now carry the plan's reference
    quotes (vitriol 22.04, halite 2.52, fizzy 3.65, fluorite 12.23, gas
    150.00 cap, gold 147.35, quartz 5.21, pure_gold 573.80), `sol` class,
    `rho`, `dye`, `ventF`/`brine`/`residue` fractions; water tinting blends
    the load-weighted `dye` of every dissolved mineral.
  - Stoichiometry: Fizzy Halite is `R2G2B2` (66.7% brine + 33.3% gas lost);
    Fluorite-Salt gives 55% green brine + 45% Fluorite concentrate residue
    with zero gas lost. Dissolution is incremental: soluble slices enter the
    contacting parcel as they leach, so strong-skeleton cells release brine
    without detaching.
  - Structural collapse: detachment requires the remaining insoluble
    skeleton `strengthInto`-style sum `< debrisLimit (0.8 J)`, with the
    probabilistic roll `(prog - 0.50) * 6 * dt` past 55% progress and
    guaranteed completion at 100%. Pure Fluorite (0.45 × 3.0 = 1.35 J)
    leaches to an exhausted standing skeleton instead of collapsing.
  - Submerged soluble grains dissolve at 3× rate × saturation, following the
    same brine/gas splits; Fluorite grains stop exactly at the 45% floor
    (Gas grains leave Stone), insoluble grains never dissolve, and tiny
    fully-soluble remainders finish cleanly as brine.
  - Gold placer liberation: 25% Pure Gold mass carrying exactly 97.35% of
    lump value (573.80/g equivalent) + 75% Quartz at 2.65%, exact by
    construction (`goldVal = origVal * 0.9735`, Quartz takes the remainder).
  - Composition Law hydrate smoothstep recalibrated to 0.15–0.42; both
    chemistry modes share saturation, collapse, grain dissolution, and the
    double-precision ledger (drifts ≲ 1e-12 over full scenes, 0 in the
    controlled grain tests). Cell Inspector shows skeleton vs `debrisLimit`,
    live brine saturation %, and per-mineral loads.
- `prototypes/solubility-lab.html`: `satCap` slider (1–200 g/wu).
- `experiments/solubility-geology-probe.cjs` (new): mirrors
  `js/geology.js autoAdjust` for the 13-mineral table with narrowed Blue
  Vitriol; calibrated host weight 0.0883 with `copper + vitriol = 7.83%`,
  exactly the baseline copper share. Exact calibrated boxes recorded in
  `0.3.6-Solubility-plan.md` §4.2; log in
  `experiments/logs/0.3.6-solubility-geology.txt`.
- `experiments/solubility-lab.test.js`: 9 tests — the original six updated
  (Fizzy now asserts 33.3%/66.7% mass stoichiometry, Fluorite asserts the
  standing 1.35 J skeleton) plus new saturation-stall, submerged
  soluble-grain / Fluorite 45%-floor / insoluble-grain, `debrisLimit`
  skeleton and reference-quote, and 97.35%/2.65% Gold liberation coverage.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/solubility-lab.test.js` — 9/9 pass; mass/value
  drift < 1e-9 in scene tests and < 1e-11 in controlled grain tests.
- `node experiments/solubility-geology-probe.cjs` — converges on target
  shares for all 13 minerals (gas 0.92% vs 0.96% target; gas is fixed by
  design in calibration).
- Visual: the sandbox has no system Chromium libraries, so the page was
  rendered headlessly via jsdom + `@napi-rs/canvas` (outside the repo)
  driving the real `file://` page: app boots, satCap slider binds, demo
  sweep dissolves columns with gas bubbles at Fizzy, HUD drift reads
  0.0000, screenshot inspected. A true Chromium pass should be repeated
  where browser libraries exist.
- `deferred`: `solubility.2-plan.md` (evaporation crusts, settling basins,
  beaker mode, strip chart, time warp) intentionally left untouched.
