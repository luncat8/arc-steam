## Slice — wrong fuel, then the service cart

### Fuel tables rebuilt minimal (owner correction)

The three-family tables from Slice 2 (solid: stone/sand/quartz; liquid: gas,
fizzy, fluorite, vitriol, halite; fissile: iron/copper/corundum/silver/gold)
were rejected as nonsense. Replaced with the smallest honest sets, unchanged
mechanics:

| Grade | Engines | Minerals (kJ/kg) |
|---|---|---|
| coal (solid) | coal, electric | stone 3.0, sand 2.0 |
| liquid | oil | fizzy halite 20.0, vitriol 10.0 |
| nuclear (fissile) | nuclear | gas rock 40.0 |

`gradeLists` / `gradeNames`, `fuel.read` reasons and help text updated to
match. No mineral was invented; the cave simply does not hold a literal
"coal", so the coal engine burns the coal-like solids. **Judgment call to
confirm:** if a different solid (e.g. quartz) was intended for coal, it is a
one-line table change. experiments/fuel.test.js retargeted to the new tables
(10/10); experiments/wild-water.test.js oil case now loads fizzy instead of
gas (10/10).

### Service cart (replaces the D10 driving simulation)

Owner spec: when the hopper fills — or once a minute while it is not empty —
a service cart appears from a background (z-axis storyline, not cave
geometry) tunnel near the start; click it to sell; a checkbox autosells on
arrival; the cart then disappears; the sale keeps the last 20% of fuel-grade
cargo; grader preinstalled.

Implemented as `js/wild-cart.js` + a screen-space cut-in in
`js/main_wild.js#wildDrawCart` (portal + cart, z-axis zoom in, beacon blink,
storyline caption). No waypoints, no collision, no physics — the cart is a
rendered arrival and a transaction.

- Triggers: hopper full, or `world.time - lastDepart >= 60` with cargo
  aboard (lastDepart seeds at run start, so the first scheduled call lands a
  minute in; a full hopper calls sooner).
- Click-to-sell: `wildPointerDown` hit-tests the cart before any rock/floor
  handling; the click books the sale and never reaches the cave.
- Autosell: `#cart-autosell` checkbox → `SteamWildCart.setAutosell`; sells at
  the arriving→docked transition if ticked. `#cart-status` announces
  en-route/docked/sold in the panel.
- Sale: `ceiling.sellCargoKeeping(keepMass, …)` — an absolute per-mineral
  reserve, pro rata by mass against the locked cargoValue, one frozen `sell`
  ledger entry, cargo cells/value decremented, `exportedEnergy` booked like
  `sellCargo`. The cart keeps 20% of each fuel-grade mineral (stone, sand,
  fizzy, vitriol, gas) **of the load at that call** — the reservation shrinks
  with repeated calls instead of ratcheting to zero in one pass, and autosell
  can never sell the engine's fuel outright.
- Docked dwell: 30 s waiting for a click (unsold cart leaves empty-handed);
  after a sale it lingers 2.2 s, then slides out and hides for the minute.
- Portal is drawn at the spawn shelf projected to the screen, clamped into
  view whenever the rig has driven beyond the start; always visible and
  clickable. It is a storyline, so this is deliberate, not a fallback.

### Page wiring fixed in passing

`js/wild-rubble.js` and `js/wild-cart.js` were never referenced by index.html
— in the browser `window.SteamWildRubble` did not exist and every wild rubble
behaviour silently no-opped. Added both script tags (rubble before
wild-mine.js, cart before it too) plus `cart-*` ids in main.js and
`createLinearGradient`/`measureText` in the dom.cjs shim (the cart cut-in
encountered both missing stubs). The headless interface path now exercises
the cart end to end.
