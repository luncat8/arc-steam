# 0.1.4 — Inspector/ledger merge, softer rock, left-wall pocket and wedge

## Decisions and plan

- Merged the best of the mineral-readout prototype into the 2D rock simulation instead of keeping either branch: per-cell inspection, per-mineral cargo accounting, and a sale ledger now sit on top of 2D exposed-face rock, mass-bearing cargo, and automatic wall sales. Unlimited anywhere storage/sales were not brought back; the 100-cell mass-bearing cargo with location-based wall markets stays as the better rule.
- Softened rock per playtest: impact damage removes double strength over a 1.5× wider radius (4.5 cells) with linear falloff to zero at the edge. Booked fracture energy is strength removed divided by two, so the energy ledger stays exact while fewer hits break each cell.
- Fixed the asymmetric-platform dead zone at the left wall with fixed simulation geometry: a pocket the collector docks into (platform minimum 0.54 m, reflector covering x = 0) plus an unbreakable wedge above the pocket that funnels wall-side falls toward the reflector. Docking trades catching for bouncing: the hidden collector cannot catch until undocked.

## Implementation

- `js/ceiling.js`: 2× damage multiplier with 4.5-cell zero-edge falloff; pool-exhaustion fix (failed cells count as extracted before the loss); per-mineral cargo/sold masses; frozen wall-sale ledger entries with time, cells, mass, mix, and credits; lost-value tracking; fragment bounce off the wedge slope with dissipated heat.
- `js/physics.js`: left pocket travel limit with wall sales at the new minimum; swept ball collision against the wedge slope (approaching-only, no zero-time stick) and base (from below), plus penetration resolve; held-ball clamp clear of the wedge; sale timestamps through the step.
- `index.html` / `js/main.js` / `styles.css`: 2D mineral inspector (column/layer sliders plus click-the-rock selection with canvas outline, strength meter, Fe/Cu/Cr and R/G/B readouts, extracted/falling/caught/lost counts) and a cargo/market panel (per-mineral table, sale ledger with latest eight, status line). Canvas view widened to show the pocket; pocket, walls, and wedge rendered; SELL marks lifted clear of the wedge.
- Tests and smoke: updated the left-wall sale for the pocket minimum (both walls now covered); new coverage for damage scale/radius/falloff, pool accounting, per-mineral conservation and frozen ledger entries, cell conservation across settling, fragment and ball wedge bounces, base blocking, pocket docking/reflection, and trapped-bounce escape. Browser smoke now exercises the inspector sliders and rock-click selection.
