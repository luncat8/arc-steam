# 0.3.6 worklog — rockets.3 review fixes and the sandblaster

## Context

`archive/rockets.2-plan.md` / `archive/rockets.2-worklog.md` and
`archive/rockets.3-worklog.md` describe the per-owner rocket rack and the rival
seam trips. This milestone first reviews that work for genuinely better
approaches, then implements the sandblaster planned in
`0.3.6-Sandblaster-plan.md`.

## Rockets.3 review — improvements applied

- `js/players.js`: the rig's and each rival's rocket trip state was three
  near-identical inline blocks. They are now one shared state machine
  (`rigRocketTrip` for the rig, a per-rival `rocketTrip` object) driven by
  `stepRocketTripState` / `endRocketTripState` / `startRocketTripState`, with
  the fire mark, the 5 s catch window and the cooldown wait in one place. The
  rival trip now also turns on the owner's standing auto-buy order when the
  rack goes in, so a rival replenishes its own rounds at its own wall instead
  of running dry after one trip.
- `js/rockets.js`: the cooldown remainder was simplified to
  `Math.max(0, cooldown - dt)` during the review. That broke five tests — a
  `5.256e-16` remainder blocked the next fire and stranded stock at 3. The
  original snap (`cooldown - dt > dt * 0.5 ? cooldown - dt : 0`) is
  load-bearing and was restored.
- Validation: `node --test experiments/rockets.test.js experiments/players.test.js`
  — 62/62; `experiments/interface.test.js` — 50/50.

## Sandblaster — implementation

- `js/sandblaster.js` (new): the nozzle simulation. Per-owner profiles in a
  `profiles` Map keyed by owner id (charges, auto-buy, visit count, counters),
  mirroring `js/rockets.js`. Fixed typed-array grain pools
  (`maxGrains = 5 owners × 40`). Grain states FREE/FLYING/FALLING.
  - Fire spends one charge and the per-grain reserve draw (13.5 J), which buys
    the grain's lift and speed exactly like the piston's pulse, so the grain
    carries exactly what was spent. The rig draws the mine's battery; a rival's
    nozzle is machinery outside the rig's ledger and books the draw as imported
    work, the same way its piston pulse does.
  - Grains climb ballistically and impact through the shared
    `SteamCeiling.chip(x, surfaceAt(x), energy, 1)` rule: one-cell radius, at
    most half a cell's strength per blow, so a seam needs several hits and one
    grain never brings a cell down by itself. The share the rock took books to
    `world.fractureEnergy`, the rest of the impact to `world.heat`.
  - The grain falls back with a small spread. A collector catch returns the
    charge to the grain's owner and exchanges momentum into the platform's
    velocity; grains that reach the floor or leave the chamber are lost. The
    impact, catch and loss book the grain's whole remaining energy, so the
    mine's total energy closes exactly at any platform height (verified in the
    tests).
  - Sand is bought at a SELL wall (3 cr a charge, capacity 60), with a
    per-owner auto-buy order capped at 10 charges per wall visit. `fill` is
    the test-mode grant. `draw` renders the streaks and grains through the
    ceiling's perspective helpers.
- `js/physics.js`: `collectorX(world, owner)` (machine lookup plus the
  collector-fraction offset, shared with the rocket rack's `launcherX`);
  `createWorld` resets the sandblaster, `step` runs it after the rockets.
- `js/equipment.js`: the Impact-gated sandblaster shop item (520 cr, +7 kg).
- Page wiring: `index.html` (Blast / Buy / auto-buy / status panel, script
  tag), `js/main.js` (ui list), `js/main_input.js` (F in `isGameKey`,
  hold-to-fire in the frame loop, Blast pointer capture, Buy and the auto-buy
  change handler), `js/main_readouts.js` (`sandblastReadout`: charges, price,
  SELL-wall/funds/reserve disable reasons), `js/main_draw.js` (nozzle glyph
  and `SteamSandblaster.draw`), `js/help.js` (sandblaster topic, F in the key
  list), `README.md` (controls and mechanics).

## Notes

- The impact, catch and loss bookings were written and then corrected in three
  passes: the first version booked only kinetic energy (the platform-height
  residual leaked), then the impact snapped the grain to the face without
  booking the overshoot, then the impact's fall-spread was booked at the max
  spread instead of the grain's actual random spread. The final form books
  the grain's whole remaining energy at each resolution, and the tests pin the
  ledger closed.
- `experiments/commune.test.js` and `experiments/interface.test.js` now load
  `js/sandblaster.js` alongside `js/rockets.js`, since the readout references
  the module unconditionally.

# 0.3.6 fix — the sandblaster feeds on the hopper

Source: the `## Sandblaster` note in `draft.txt`.

## Review of the previous step

- The draft's design was missed: the nozzle had a bought 60-charge magazine,
  a Buy button and a wall auto-buy order. Debris returns and keeps its mass,
  so it needs no ammunition.
- A grain caught by a rival booked the rival body's kinetic-energy change to
  the rig's heat, although a rival body is outside the rig's ledger. The
  parked-rig tests never saw it. A catch by a rival now books only the
  grain's own energy.
- The interface test pressed `F` on a rival without releasing it, so the
  rest of that test ran with the stream held.
- README claimed test mode filled the magazine; the page never did.

## Done

- `js/sandblaster.js`: magazine, price, purchases and auto-buy removed. A
  grain is 20 g of the owner's cargo — sand first, then stone, never ore —
  taken out with its value and put back into whichever hopper catches it; a
  miss is spilled onto the floor rubble. A full hopper stops the nozzle.
  `setAutoFire` is the per-owner standing order (kept across New game); the
  rig's order holds below 20% reserve. The grain keeps the platform's
  horizontal velocity. `feed`, `grainsAboard`, `hopperFull` serve the panel.
- `js/ceiling.js`: `unloadCargo`, `loadCargo`, `spill` (the fragment deposit
  now goes through `spill`), `cargoHeight` in the state.
- `js/players.js`: `rival.cargoMinerals`, booked at harvest by the rock's
  abundance and cleared at sale; `unloadCargo`, `loadCargo` for a rival.
- Page: Buy button removed, the checkbox is Auto-fire, the Blast button
  shows the grains aboard, new status lines, cargo cells shown to a tenth.
  Shop text, help topic, README and the plan rewritten.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/*.test.js`: every file passes.
  `experiments/sandblaster.test.js` (13 tests) pins the feed order with ore
  untouched, the full-hopper stop, auto-fire with its reserve hold and its
  persistence, value-exact return to cargo, the spill on the floor, a
  steady cruise catching every grain, the rival hopper, and the ledger
  parked and under real drive. `experiments/interface.test.js` drives the
  panel, `F`, the checkbox and a rival's nozzle.
- A 300 s node run of the page loop (rig bot, auto-fire on, with and without
  a rival) closes the ledger to 1e-8 J whenever no grain is aloft. Not pinned
  by a test: a rival catching a rig grain.
- `node experiments/sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs`: ordinary-rock median
  profit 2.2 cr, market median 196.0 cr, no spill when parked — the 13.5 J
  draw needs no retune.

## Notes

- With the rig bot chasing the ball at base equipment, about a third of the
  auto-fired grains are spilled during hard acceleration, and the reserve
  runs down to the 20% hold in about four minutes.
- The total-energy readout is short by the flight energy of grains in the
  air and closes when they land, as before this change.

## Next step

Let bots use the nozzle: switch auto-fire on when the reserve and the engine
can carry it, and count the stream's draw in the rig's charge-trip decision.
A floor that kept its mineral mix would replace the abundance estimate in the
rival hopper and let the grader recover spilled sand as sand.

## Follow-up — high-power particle stream and bot nozzle duty

- The earlier 13.5 J / 10 Hz calibration above is historical. The nozzle now
  spends 135 J per grain at 20 Hz (2.7 kW held); grain dots replace the stale
  origin-to-current lines, and the muzzle is aligned with the collector glyph.
  A grain inherits both platform velocity components at fire time, then
  advances only under gravity. Extra kinetic energy from a climbing platform
  is booked as terrain work; a rival's entire grain is imported work.
- Bots engage an installed, fed nozzle only when their own engine's pad
  refill power covers the stream. Their order is separate from the player's
  persistent Auto-fire; unchecking pauses BOT until it is cycled. The rig
  pauses for service/manual travel and heat, and starts charging with one
  second of stream demand above the 20% floor.
- A full hopper receiving only part of a falling rock fragment exposed an
  integer-pool truncation: the residual debris rounded to zero cells and
  created energy. `debrisCells` is now floating point; fractional remainder,
  mineral value and energy all survive to a floor landing.
- The revised 30 s probe in `experiments/logs/0.3.6-sandblaster-yield.txt`
  reports ordinary-rock median -518.5 cr and market median -445.8 cr at the
  paid emergency import price. The former near-zero paid-energy target is
  intentionally superseded by the requested tenfold draw without widening
  the one-cell chip footprint; cheap pad energy and richer seams matter more.
- Validation: `node --test experiments/*.test.js` — 378/378. New regression
  checks cover inherited horizontal/vertical launch speed, dot-only drawing,
  the bot's own order, manual pause, engine/thermal gating, early charge trip,
  rival isolation and the fractional full-hopper catch. A 50 s held stream
  that fills the rig hopper and spills the rest closes the ledger within
  1e-6 J after every projectile and fragment settles.
