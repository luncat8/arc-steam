# 0.4.0 Wild Mine — P2 ball upgrades worklog

Covers the three P2 ball additions from `0.4.0-wild-mine-draft.md`:
spin motor with faster frictionless mid-air windup and Coulomb bounce friction,
piston-style jump actuator toggle, and bounce energy recycling.

## 1. Review of M3 baseline (`js/wild-ball.js`)

Reviewed `js/wild-ball.js`, `js/main_wild.js`, `js/main_input.js`, `index.html`,
and `archive/0.4.0-wild-mine-m3-worklog.md` before editing:

- `rollStep` pushed translational `vt` directly and locked `omega = vt / r` via
  `settleRoll`; `airStep` held `omega` frozen in flight, so the motor was dead
  in mid-air.
- `resolveContact` applied `vt *= 0.88` and left `omega` disconnected from
  `vt`, so spin never bit into a floor, wall, ceiling or deck bounce.
- Jumping used a one-shot `requestJump` button/key firing only from ground roll.
- Passive bounces at `restitution = sqrt(0.9)` took ~28 bounces / ~26 s to
  settle on a flat shelf (noted in the M3 worklog), with no active damper or
  battery recuperation.
- Minor M3 guard found during review: `setBallMotor()` in `js/main_input.js`
  ran from `clearInput()` in Classic mode and attached `.motor = 0` to classic
  balls; all four ball-control helpers are now gated on `wildIsActive()`.

## 2. What was implemented

### Spin motor & Coulomb contact friction (`js/wild-ball.js`)

- Rigid solid sphere (`I = 2/5 m r^2`, `omega > 0` clockwise matching
  `main_wild.js` rendering, oriented surface tangent `t = (ny, -nx)` so pure
  rolling is `v·t = omega * r` on floors, walls and ceilings alike).
- `stepSpinMotor` applies internal spin torque on both rock and air:
  - Opposing existing `omega` brakes spin into `world.heat` at zero battery
    cost before accelerating past zero.
  - Angular acceleration `(7/2) * (motorAcceleration / r)` at `80%` battery
    efficiency is flat up to `motorTopSpeed = 5 m/s` and tapers linearly by
    back-EMF to `motorFreeSpeed = 8 m/s`. In frictionless mid-air `I` carries
    the torque alone, so `omega` climbs `3.5x` faster than ground rolling and
    winds up to `8 m/s` rim speed without altering `vx`.
- `applyRockFriction` / `applyDeckFriction` resolve Coulomb impulse at contact
  point `x - r*n`:
  - Slip `s = (v - v_surface)·t - omega * r`, ideal stick impulse
    `J_stick = -(2/7) m s`, clamped to `[-mu * J_n, +mu * J_n]` with
    `surfaceFriction = 0.85`.
  - Conserves angular momentum `r * m * v_t + I * omega` about the contact
    point to float64 precision (`~2e-16`), lets gentle high-spin impacts skid
    and retain spin for the next bounce, and books exact slip work to
    `world.heat`.
  - `rollStep` resolves motor torque against Coulomb friction bounded by normal
    load `mu * m * g * ny * dt` (limiting traction on steep slopes and dropping
    inverted overhangs `ny <= 0` back to `airStep`), applies
    `rollingResistance = 0.35 m/s^2` only once rolling cleanly, and advances
    with trapezoidal velocity.

### Piston-style jump actuator toggle (`#ball-jump`, `ArrowUp`)

- Replaced one-shot `requestJump` with persistent on/off state (`setJump`,
  `toggleJump`, default `false`, preserved across launch and deck capture).
- When switched on, `applyJumpPulse` feeds up to `0.5 * m * jumpSpeed^2`
  (`jumpSpeed = 2.6 m/s`, `jumpEfficiency = 0.8`) into normal exit speed
  `v_n' = sqrt(v_n^2 + 2 * useful / m)` — matching `physics.js` reflector drive
  — lifting off from a roll while keeping `v_t` and `omega`, powering repeated
  rock bounces, contributing `m * deltaVn` to normal friction impulse, and
  falling back seamlessly to passive bounces when the battery empties.
- `#ball-jump` uses the same `.drive` button and `aria-pressed` state pattern
  as `#drive` (`Jump actuator on` / `Jump actuator off`, shortcut `↑`).

### Bounce energy recycling checkbox (`#ball-recycle`, `ArrowDown`)

- Added persistent `recycle` toggle (`setRecycle`, `toggleRecycle`, default
  `false`) rendered as an `.autopilot` checkbox row (`Recycle bounce energy`,
  shortcut `↓`) under `#ball-controls`.
- On rock hits (after D3's 10% fracture/heat slice), slow settling touches, and
  deck rebounds, active damping strips `recycleDamping = 88%` of post-fracture
  normal rebound energy (snapping sub-`bounceFloor` rebounds straight to roll on
  smooth faces while keeping tangential roll intact) and routes the damped
  energy through `recycleEnergy`: matching `physics.dissipate` + Brake flywheel
  (`recycleRecovery = 0.55`, `recycleEfficiency = 0.9`, net `49.5%` up to
  `batteryCapacity`, remainder to `world.heat`).
- `jump` and `recycle` are opposite duties of the bounce actuator, so enabling
  one disables the other.
- Updated `main_wild.js` (battery-arc tint, `JUMP` / `RECUP` HUD tag, status-bar
  state and airborne `ω`, `wildSyncBallControls`), `js/help.js` (`ball` topic),
  and `0.4.0-wild-mine-plan.md` (D1, D4, Section 4, M3 table row).

  (added dedicated unit tests for spin motor + mid-air windup + brake, Coulomb
  bounce friction across floor/wall/ceiling/deck + grip/skid, jump actuator

  coverage in `experiments/wild-mine-interface.test.js`).
