# 0.4.0 Wild Mine water — worklog

## Review of the fuel and fabrication slice

- The Wild HUD read `fuel.reason`, but the live readout had no such field. The
  cargo card and low-reserve strip now share the actual reason, including the
  new oil/condensate case.
- The fabricator controls existed in HTML but were missing from the page's DOM
  bindings. Wiring them exposed that a Classic rival's fab used the rig hopper
  and reserve. Fabrication now draws from the selected platform's own hopper;
  only the rig cycle touches the rig reserve and export ledger.

## Liquid cycle

- The cave has finite seeded seeps, including a reachable pool on the starting
  shelf. Liquid uses the cave grid without editing occupancy or its SDF. Only
  liquid overrides survive chunk eviction, so a drained cell cannot refill on
  return and water alone does not pin rock chunks.
- The built-in intake scans below the hull and stops at solid rock. It fills a
  12 kg tank at up to 2.6 kg/s independently of dry cargo and falls back to a
  slow hand pump when flat. Tank weight and deck-height potential are accounted
  for during collection, spray, draining and combustion. The drain valve dumps
  only spray water, leaving condensate and making room even at zero reserve.
- Seep condensate is an oil-only fuel at 24 kJ/kg. Oil uses it before dry cargo
  at the existing combined refill rate. The installed gun uses plain tank water;
  drops hit the cave SDF and return as clean runoff or are booked as drainage.
  Recovering runoff never creates condensate. Classic keeps its unlimited spray
  and brine solver. Cave-wide flow and wet rock chemistry remain separate work.
- The cave renderer, tank gauge, intake order and reserve card expose the cycle.
  The help topics explain how to collect, spray and hand-prime.
