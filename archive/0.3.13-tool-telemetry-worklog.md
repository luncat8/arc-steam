## Expected-Value-Per-Energy Telemetry (`js/tool-telemetry.js`, `js/water.js`, HUD)

Closed the telemetry item from `0.3.8-plan.md` and archived the updated spec to
`archive/0.3.8-plan.md`:

- **Core estimator (`js/tool-telemetry.js` — `SteamToolTelemetry`):**
  - `createSummary()` allocates one reusable caller-owned summary object;
    `measure(world, owner, out)` writes in place with zero per-frame allocations
    and works identically for `'rig'` or any rival profile.
  - **Ball strike (`measureBall`):** targets `SteamPrediction.state.endX` on rock
    contacts, inspects `trace.pathX` at peak `trace.pathY` while ascending under
    `ballistics2` (`trace.uncertain && focus.vy > 0`), or uses `focus.x`/`body.x`;
    computes expected ceiling fracture payout over `firstRow` and the immediate
    backing row (`0.35` weight) divided by `reserveCost(pulse) / 1000`.
  - **Sandblaster (`measureSandblaster`):** evaluates the exposed ceiling column
    above `sandblasterNozzleX`, accounting for geometric impact steps (`hitLooseCap`
    / `collapseStrengthLimit`), sweep speed collapse waste, dry-hopper detection,
    and `reserveCost(shotEnergy) / 1000`.
  - **Water gun + pump (`SteamWater.estimateYield` & `hullNeedsPump` in `js/water.js`):**
    traces the aimed nozzle trajectory non-mutatingly with preallocated scratch
    buffers (`estimateNozzle`, `estimateWet`, `splitScratch`), sums brine and
    undermined residue returns across the `jetSplashCells = 2` splash patch
    under the active chemistry/saturation/pump rules (`'always'` vs. `'solute'`),
    tracks gas-vent value loss (`gasLossPerKJ`), and bills jet plus active pump
    reserve draw through engine efficiency.
  - **Road grader (`measureGrader`):** integrates `floorPiles` / `floorValues`
    across the `1.5 m` swath against rolling-resistance power
    (`45 N · speed / η`, zero in Communism mode).
- **UI & Help (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `js/main.js`, `js/main_readouts.js`, `js/help.js`):**
  - Added compact `.tool-yield` lines (`#drive-yield`, `#sandblast-yield`,
    `#water-yield`, `#grader-yield`, and `#market-tool-yield` beside emergency
    fuel price) updated from one preallocated `toolMetrics` summary on the
    100 ms `readout()` cadence with green/amber comparison against `fuelPrice`
    (`25 cr/kJ` or free in `commune`).
  - Updated `js/help.js` (`drive`, `sandblaster`, `water`) and `README.md`, and
    recorded test-rock reset and `ballistics2` trace notes in
    `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.
