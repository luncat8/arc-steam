# 0.1.1 — Physics sandbox

## Decisions

- Platform supplies bounce energy; balls are passive.
- Ground contact dissipates ball energy. Moving the platform over a grounded ball collects it.
- Collector catches/holds and dissipates energy. Explicit release transfers a ball to the reflector and pays lifting energy.
- Low-tier drive pulses are limited; the initial 36 J calibration takes three pushes to reach the 10 m ceiling from storage.
- Updated the standalone plan with these rules; remaining unknowns are not silently treated as confirmed requirements.

## Implementation

- Added standalone Node/browser physics with a 240 Hz fixed step, analytic gravity, swept boundary/deck contacts, 90% passive vertical energy retention, and separate held/grounded/flying states.
- Added force-limited platform motion, manual braking, click-to-target travel, finite shared reserve, inefficient power delivery, impact/braking heat, and a total-energy diagnostic.
- Added responsive canvas chamber, DOM controls, telemetry, pause/speed/reset, pointer movement controls, focus-aware keyboard shortcuts, and tab/blur handling.
- Kept classic local scripts and direct-file startup; no runtime dependencies, build, or external assets.
- Added numerical tests, optional browser smoke script, and run/control documentation.

## Validation

- `node --test experiments/physics.test.js`: 9/9 pass. Covers analytic fall, passive retention, collector losses, ground recovery, three-push climb, empty reserve, acceleration/braking/wall accounting, fast collisions, and deterministic replay. Energy checks tolerate at most 1e-6 J discrepancy in these scenarios.
- `node --check js/main.js` and `git diff --check`: pass.
- Browser smoke test could not execute: Playwright browser download host is inaccessible; an npm-distributed Chromium binary was obtained but cannot launch because the sandbox lacks `libnspr4.so`. No browser/UI pass is claimed. The smoke script remains available for an environment with browser prerequisites.

## Boundaries and next step

Ceiling is deliberately inert. Heat is an energy sink counter, not yet a temperature/cooling model. No wear, debris, sale economy, research, or autonomous interception is implemented. Target travel is a prototype controller, not researched autonomous ball catching.

Next step: complete browser smoke/manual play validation, then implement seeded mineral ceiling damage and falling debris with conserved material mass.
