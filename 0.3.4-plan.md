# 0.3.4 — Perspective scaling of the ball

## Goal

A setting that draws each ball with the rock layer's own vertical perspective law
instead of a constant size, so a ball inside the rock reads at the same depth as
the cells around it. Physics, collision radius, damage radius and balance are
unchanged: only the drawn radius changes.

## Decisions

- The law is the rock's, not a second curve: `SteamCeiling.rockScale(y)` — 1× at
  the rock's near edge, 0.25× at `y = 15 m`, a monotone cubic in between, and 1×
  below the layer. The centre of the ball takes the scale, exactly as it already
  takes the warp, so the sphere stays round in screen space near the layer.
- The setting is a chamber view rule, like the rock shape and the ball
  arrangement: a `PERSPECTIVE` select in the system bar (`Depth scale` by
  default, `Constant size`), held in `js/main_draw.js` as `ballPerspective`,
  kept across New game and applied to every ball the page draws (rig and
  competitor).
- Off means 1× everywhere, so the classic constant-size ball is one option away.
- Collision, damage and every reader of `config.radius` stay untouched; the
  drawn ball is a rendering of a fixed sphere.
- The damage footprint is a property of the strike, not the picture, so the
  overlay that shows it must not shrink with the ball. `F4` toggles a
  splash overlay: for every ball, a ring at that ball's owner's own
  `damageRadiusNow() * cell` (already exposed as `SteamCeiling.damageRadiusNow`),
  drawn with the rock perspective's local scale applied to both axes. A ball in
  the rock therefore shows its real damage radius around a smaller body — the
  one thing a scaled-down ball could otherwise misreport.
- No new module, no new geometry buffer: one multiply per ball per frame.

## Suggested implementation

- `index.html`: `<select id="ball-perspective">` with `on` (`Depth scale`,
  selected) and `flat` (`Constant size`), plus a help mark on the new topic.
- `js/help.js`: topic `perspective` — what scales, what does not, and the `F4`
  overlay.
- `js/main.js`: add `ball-perspective` to the id list.
- `js/main_draw.js`: `ballPerspective` state, `ballRadiusScale(y)`,
  `drawSplashOverlay()`, and one ball-scale multiply in the ball pass.
- `js/main_input.js`: bind the select (change marks the scene dirty) and `F4`.
- `js/main_help.js`/`js/main_boot.js`: nothing new — help marks are bound by id
  from the topic table.

## Acceptance

- `Depth scale` draws a ball at `rockScale(ball.y)` inside the layer and at 1×
  below it; `Constant size` draws it at a constant radius.
- The setting survives New game and never changes collision, damage, prediction
  or the energy ledger.
- `F4` shows each ball's damage radius at the perspective's local scale, and
  hides it again.
- `node --test experiments/*.test.js` passes with the new interface assertions.
