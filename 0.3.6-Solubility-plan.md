# 0.3.6 Solubility & Hydro-Erosion Model

A unified water–mineral chemistry and sediment-transport model for the 3-element `(R Metal, G Crystal, B Gas)` geology system (`js/minerals.js`, `js/geology.js`, `js/rock-materials.js`), paired with the Hybrid 2D fluid architecture (`0.3.6-Water-LLM-draft.md`) and validated interactively in `prototypes/solubility-lab.html`.

---

## 1. Design Principles & Role of the Water Gun

The water gun is **not** a second sandblaster or brute-force breaker. It occupies a distinct physical and economic niche:

1. **Very low mechanical fracture force:** Shooting intact insoluble rock (Stone, Iron, dry Chalcopyrite, Quartz, Corundum, Silver) barely chips it. Heavy breakers (ball, rockets, sandblaster) stay mandatory for hard veins and gas pockets.
2. **Fast, selective chemical dissolution:** Soluble salts and hydrates dissolve rapidly on exposed rock surfaces. Once a surface cell's cumulative dissolution crosses its detachment threshold (with probabilistic full-cell completion so faces etch organically), the entire cell releases cleanly:
	- **Soluble phase** enters the water as **dissolved solute** (stays permanently dissolved until recovered by the under-platform suction pump and separated into cargo storage).
	- **Volatile gas phase** erupts upward as rising bubbles and is **permanently lost** — expensive gas cannot be harvested wet.
	- **Insoluble solid phase** detaches and falls straight down as **solid debris** (collectible on the platform or eroded across the floor).
3. **Visible solution chemistry:** Dissolved solutes color the water cleanly by chemical family so the player can read pool contents by sight before driving over to pump them:
	- **Halite brine (`+G`)** tints water **emerald / jade green**.
	- **Copper sulfate hydrate / Blue Vitriol (`+GB`)** tints water **cobalt / azure blue**.
	- Mixed brines blend smoothly into deep turquoise-teal.
4. **Separate mechanical hydro-erosion (bedload saltation):** Flowing water does *not* dissolve loose solid debris into long-term suspension. Instead, fast currents briefly lift and hop light grains downstream (`0.18–0.50 s` saltation hops) and drop them as soon as flow slows, naturally sorting minerals by specific gravity — most notably washing light Quartz gangue away from heavy Gold nuggets.
5. **Unlimited v1 tank & under-platform recovery:** The water gun floods freely on power; the platform simply pumps water up from beneath its hull, separating any dissolved solutes straight into the hopper.

## 2. Water's Place in the `(R, G, B)` System

**Decision: Water is an external fluid outside the dry mineral ledger, chemically acting as a polar `G+B` solvent.**

Keeping water outside the dry rock table avoids cargo-capacity conflicts with an unlimited v1 water tank and keeps the rock-mass conservation ledger exact. Chemically, treating water as a polar non-metal/volatile (`G+B`) carrier explains every mineral reaction in the `(R, G, B)` element cube without arbitrary rules:

| Element Role | Physical Meaning | Water Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| **`R` (Metal)** | Dense metallic bonds (`Fe, Cu, Ag, Au`) | High specific gravity (`ρ` rises strongly with `R`); pure metals are insoluble and resist hydro-erosion. |
| **`G` (Crystal / Non-metal)** | Rigid covalent networks (`SiO₂, Al₂O₃`) or ionic salt anions | Pure `G` crystals are hard and insoluble, but light (`ρ ≈ 2.6`) so loose grains wash easily. Paired with `R`, forms soluble **ionic salts** (`R·G`). |
| **`B` (Gas / Volatile)** | Trapped gas, halogens, water of crystallization | Moderate `B` hydrates metal salts (`R+B+G` → soluble vitriol). Excess `B` (`> 0.55`) cannot remain bound in wet solution and **escapes as lost gas bubbles**. |

## 3. Mapping Solubility onto the Real `(R, G, B)` Cube

Scanning the baseline 10-mineral table (`js/minerals.js`) across the 8 octants of `[0, 1]³` (`seed: 1..8`, `192 × 120` cells via `js/geology.js`) reveals that **four octants are largely empty pure `stone`**:

| Octant `(R, G, B)` | Baseline Dominant Minerals | Baseline Pure Stone | Chemical Meaning in the Solubility Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LLL` (low R, low G, low B) | Quartz, Iron, Sand | 7.0% | Common silicates, iron oxides, loose sand |
| `LLH` (low R, low G, high B) | Fluorite, Gas | 6.1% | Volatile gas pockets (`B` escapes wet) & leachable Fluorite-salt |
| `LHL` (low R, high G, low B) | Corundum, Quartz | 2.9% | Ultra-hard covalent crystals (immune to water) |
| `HLL` (high R, low G, low B) | Iron, Copper, Silver | 0.0% | Dense insoluble metals & dry Chalcopyrite sulfide |
| **`HLH` (high R, low G, high B)** | Edges of Copper & Gold only | **46.5%** | **Hydrated metal salt (`Blue Vitriol`, split from high-`B` Copper)** |
| **`HHL` (high R, high G, low B)** | Empty host stone | **69.8%** | **Ionic rock salt (`Halite`, `R·G` → `+G` green brine)** |
| **`LHH` (low R, high G, high B)** | Empty host stone | **62.1%** | Transition zone between Fluorite-salt, Quartz, and Fizzy salt |
| **`HHH` (high R, high G, high B)** | Empty host stone | **90.1%** | **Effervescent gas-salt (`Fizzy Halite`, `R₂G₃B` → green brine + lost gas)** |

Placing the new soluble phases in these naturally empty octants preserves every existing rare mineral's abundance almost unchanged.

## 5. Composition Law `f(R, G, B)` vs. Mineral Reaction Table

Two ways to compute a rock cell's dissolution channels are compared directly in `prototypes/solubility-lab.html`:

### Mode A: Mixture-Weighted Mineral Table (Recommended for Live Game)
Every rock cell already carries a normalized fractional mixture `mix[0..M-1]` from `js/geology.js`. Summing each mineral's reaction coefficients weighted by `mix[m]` automatically produces:
- **Exact pure-mineral reactions** (`Halite → 100% green brine`, `Blue Vitriol → 100% blue solution`, `Fizzy Halite → 65% green brine + 35% lost gas`, `Fluorite-Salt → 55% green brine + 45% solid crystal debris`).
- **Automatic mixed-cell leaching for free:** A transition cell with `50% Halite + 35% Chalcopyrite + 15% Stone` has `50%` soluble binder. Water dissolves the Halite into green brine, collapses the cell once its soluble binder is leached out, and drops the `35% Chalcopyrite + 15% Stone` intact as falling solid debris!
- **Clear player legibility:** Every named mineral in the codex/HUD has one memorable chemical rule and one market price.

### Mode B: Continuous Composition Law `f(R, G, B)`
Computes reaction shares directly from the cell's raw `(R, G, B)` element triple:

```text
ionic(R, G, B)   = smoothstep(0.22, 0.56, R * G)                      // +G green brine (HHL, HHH)
hydrate(R, G, B) = smoothstep(0.18, 0.48, R * B * (1 - 0.65 * G))     // +GB blue solution (HLH)
degas(R, G, B)   = smoothstep(0.56, 0.86, B) * (0.35 + 0.65 * (1 - R * (1 - G)))
solubility       = clamp(ionic + hydrate + 0.65 * degas, 0, 1)
solidResidue     = max(0, 1 - (ionicShare + hydrateShare + degasShare))
density(R, G, B) = 2.0 + 7.5 * R * R - 0.8 * B
```

Mode B proves that the mineral table is **chemically consistent with a single continuous 3-element physical law**, while Mode A keeps market pricing, cargo categories, and rare minerals (`Gold`, `Corundum`, `Gas`) sharp and predictable.

## 6. Surface Dissolution, Decomposition & Conservation Mechanics

### 6.1 Probabilistic Full-Cell Surface Dissolution
Only **exposed boundary cells** (solid cells adjacent to fluid or struck by ballistic jet droplets) experience dissolution:

1. **Chemical exposure rate:**
   ```text
   chemRate = baseDissolveRate * cellSolubility * (0.35 + 0.65 * min(1, contactFlow / 2.5))
   ```
   - Standing water touching a soluble wall slowly etches it (`0.35×` baseline).
   - Active jet impact or fast current accelerates dissolution up to `1.0×` (or `1.6×` on direct fresh-water jet splash).
   - **Cohesion threshold:** Cells with `cellSolubility < 0.22` (mostly insoluble stone/ore) cannot dissolve chemically; they can only be chipped slowly by the weak mechanical impact term (`mechForce ≈ 0.15×` sandblaster).
2. **Accumulation + stochastic full-cell detachment:**
   - Each step advances `rockDissolveProg[idx] += chemRate * dt`.
   - Once `rockDissolveProg[idx] >= 0.55`, each step rolls `rand() < (rockDissolveProg - 0.50) * 6 * dt` (with guaranteed detachment at `1.0`), producing natural jagged karst dissolution fronts rather than artificial synchronized rows.
3. **Exact phase splitting on detachment (zero mass/value drift):**
   Let cell mass be `M_cell` (default `1.0` unit) and cell base market value be `V_cell`:
   - **Halite brine (`f_halite`)**: mass `M_cell * f_halite` and value `V_halite` transfer directly into the adjacent water cell's `solHalite` & `solValue` buffers.
   - **Blue Vitriol (`f_vitriol`)**: mass `M_cell * f_vitriol` and value `V_vitriol` transfer directly into `solVitriol` & `solValue`.
   - **Vented Gas (`f_gas`)**: mass `M_cell * f_gas` and value `V_gas` (priced at high `gas` market value!) are booked immediately to `lostGasMass` / `lostGasValue` and spawn rising animated gas bubbles.
   - **Solid Residue (`f_solid = 1 - f_halite - f_vitriol - f_gas`)**: if `f_solid > 0.02`, spawns 1–2 falling solid debris grains carrying exact mass `M_cell * f_solid`, exact remaining value `V_solid`, and the dominant insoluble mineral's identity & specific gravity `ρ`.
   - **Exact Invariant:**
     ```text
     M_halite + M_vitriol + M_gas + M_solid === M_cell
     V_halite + V_vitriol + V_gas + V_solid === V_cell
     ```

### 6.2 Solute Transport & Under-Platform Separation
- Dissolved `solHalite`, `solVitriol`, and `solValue` advect conservatively with water mass across every fluid cell exchange (`cell ↔ cell`), splash/droplet emission (`cell → jet`), and landing (`jet → cell`).
- Whenever the platform's under-hull suction draws a fraction `f = pulledWater / cellWater` from a water cell:
  - Exact shares `f * solHalite`, `f * solVitriol`, and `f * solValue` move straight from the water cell into the platform's **Pumped Solute Hopper** (`pumpedHalite`, `pumpedVitriol`, `pumpedValue`).
  - Solid debris is **not** pulled by the wet solute pump (keeping dry/mechanical collection and placer panning meaningfully distinct).

## 7. Separate Process: Hydro-Erosion (Bedload Saltation) & Gold Panning

Hydro-erosion acts **only** on loose solid debris particles (never intact rock walls, except loose `Sand` overburden which crumbles under direct jet impact):

### 7.1 Shields-Style Shear Pickup & Short-Hop Saltation
Each loose debris particle stores `(x, y, vx, vy, mineral, mass, value, density, state, hopTimer)` where `state ∈ {FALLING, RESTING, ENTRAINED}`:

1. **Critical pickup speed** scales linearly with submerged excess specific gravity `(ρ - 1.0)` relative to water:
	```text
	v_crit(ρ) = (0.78 / erosionPower) * max(0.35, ρ - 1.0)
	```
	At default `erosionPower = 1.0`:
	- `Sand (ρ = 1.6)` → `v_crit ≈ 0.47 m/s` (washes easily in gentle trickle).
	- `Quartz (ρ = 2.6)` → `v_crit ≈ 1.25 m/s` (hops briskly in moderate sluice stream).
	- `Stone (ρ = 2.8)` → `v_crit ≈ 1.40 m/s` (short hops in brisk current).
	- `Chalcopyrite / Iron (ρ = 4.8–5.2)` → `v_crit ≈ 2.96–3.28 m/s` (barely budges except in strong surges).
	- `Gold-bearing quartz (ρ = 5.6)` → `v_crit ≈ 3.59 m/s` (stays mostly anchored on riffles while current pans out its light Quartz gangue).
	- `Silver (ρ = 10.5)` → `v_crit ≈ 7.41 m/s` (stays right where it drops).
	- `Pure Gold (ρ = 19.3)` → `v_crit ≈ 14.27 m/s` (rock-solid anchor — concentrates cleanly in upstream riffles!).
2. **Transient entrainment (NOT permanent dissolution):**
   - A `RESTING` grain submerged in flowing water (`depth > 0.06` and local speed `v_flow > v_crit(ρ)`) has a per-second pickup chance `p_pick = 3.8 * (v_flow / v_crit - 1)`.
   - When picked up, the grain becomes `ENTRAINED` with a short random hop lifetime **`hopTimer ∈ [0.18, 0.50] s`** (shorter for heavier grains), gets a small upward lift impulse (`vy = +(0.6..1.5) m/s`), and experiences hydrodynamic drag toward the local water velocity `(u_water, v_water)` under reduced submerged gravity `g_eff = g * (1 - 1 / ρ)`.
   - When `hopTimer <= 0` or local flow drops below `0.65 * v_crit`, entrainment ends and the grain returns to `FALLING → RESTING`.
   - Result: light grains visibly **skip, tumble, and migrate downstream in short bursts**, then settle into calm tailing pools — completely distinct from permanently dissolved solutes!

### 7.2 Differential Placer Separation (`Gold-Bearing Quartz → Pure Gold + Quartz Gangue`)
When a loose composite `gold` (`Au in SiO₂ gangue`) debris grain rests in active flowing water (`v_flow > 0.85 m/s`) or takes direct jet wash:
- Washing agitation accumulates liberation progress (`libRate ∝ v_flow`).
- Upon liberation, the single `gold` lump splits with **exact mass & value conservation**:
  - **Pure Gold nugget (`pure_gold`)**: `25%` of lump mass, **`92%` of lump value**, specific gravity **`ρ = 19.3`** → stays concentrated right on the upstream bed/riffles!
  - **Quartz gangue (`quartz`)**: `75%` of lump mass, **`8%` of lump value**, specific gravity **`ρ = 2.6`** → immediately becomes `ENTRAINED` and washes away downstream into the tailings basin!

Gameplay payoff: Wet sluicing a mixed Gold-quartz rubble pile sheds 75% of useless gangue weight while preserving 92% of the value in a gleaming high-grade concentrate.
