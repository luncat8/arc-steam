## Detailed findings

### F1 — Saturation cap (`60 g/wu`): implemented, three caveats

Implementation: `saturationFactor()` at `:282-290` computes
`1 - totalLoad / (vol * satCap)`, clamped to `[0,1]`; `exposeRockCell()` (`:1194`)
and `dissolveSubmergedGrain()` (`:1627`) multiply their rates by it. Fresh parcel →
`1.000`, parcel at `vol × satCap` → `0.000`. The intended gameplay loop works.

Caveats worth fixing before live port:

1. **Volume floor.** `:285` uses `Math.max(state.water[fIdx], 0.05)` instead of
   `max(eps, waterVol)`. Every thin film is treated as at least 0.05 water units,
   i.e. a 3 g capacity instead of `vol × 60`. A 0.04-unit puddle (created by
   `:1056`) gets a 25% larger cap; a 1e-5-unit film (`:1535`) gets ~5000×. The
   floor is exactly the "tiny puddle in a Halite depression" case the finding
   calls out. Use a true `eps` (e.g. `1e-6`) and clamp the deposited mass to the
   remaining capacity instead.
2. **Pumping does not lower saturation by itself.** `stepUnderPlatformPump()`
   takes `frac = pull / w` (`:1594`) and scales both `water` and every `loads[m]`
   by that same `frac` (`:1598-1603`), so `load/water` is invariant and `sat` is
   invariant. Only complete dry-out or **fresh-water dilution** restores cutting.
   The plan's "stagnant brine stalls until pumped away" is physically fine as a
   loop (pumping makes room, fresh jet refills), but the wording hides that the
   pump alone cannot re-activate a cell; add a test that pumps a saturated parcel
   and re-fires to prove the loop.
3. **Rate limiter, not a hard ceiling.** Nothing clamps `loads[m]` to the
   remaining capacity inside `releaseDissolvedSlice()` (`:1034-1066`); a single
   low-`satCap` step can overshoot `vol × satCap` by a factor of ~6 before `sat`
   clamps to 0. Also jet impact passes a hardcoded `dt = 0.045` (`:1327`) to
   `exposeRockCell`, so the collapse roll `(prog-0.50)*6*dt` (`:1211`) is 2.7×
   the per-step probability implied by the plan §6.2 and is per droplet hit, not
   per simulation step.

### F2 — Submerged soluble debris ×3: implemented

`dissolveSubmergedGrain()` (`:1621-1680`) dissolves only minerals with
`brine + vent > 0`, at `3.0 × sat × dt` (`:1629`), with the same stoichiometric
splits, the 45% Fluorite floor, tiny-remainder clean-up, and `dLeach[i]` tracking
so exhausted grains become insoluble residue. Probe: Halite `0.55 → 0.55 brine`,
Fluorite `1.00 → 0.55 brine + 0.45 residue`, Quartz `0.55 → 0 brine + 0.55
debris`. Insoluble grains are never dissolved and hydro-erosion stays purely
mechanical (test 7 asserts `waterSoluteMass === 0`).

Minor: the rate is `dMass × 0.55 × dissolveSpeed × dissolveRate × 3 × sat`, while
a wall cell dissolves at `chemSpeed × dissolveRate × intensity × sat`. Against a
jet-hit wall (`intensity 1.85`) the grain is effectively ~0.9×, not 3×; against
stagnant contact (`intensity 0.32`) it is ~5×. "×3 surface area" is a nominal
constant, not a measured 3× of the live wall rate — worth a calibration note in
the plan. Also, `detachRockCellMechanical()` (`:1143-1164`) prices a partly
leached **mixed** cell's debris by its dominant mineral only, so a Halite-dominant
mixed grain can dissolve its Quartz minority in water.

### F3 — Stoichiometry: one serious Mode B gap, one economy gap

**Fizzy Halite ✔**: `R₂G₂B₂` with `brine = 2/3`, `vent = 1/3` (`:161-170`); probe
measures `0.333 / 0.667` after a 3 s jet. The stoichiometric invariant in plan
§4.2 rule 1 holds in table mode.

**Fluorite in Mode A ✔**: 55% green brine, 45% concentrate, zero gas, standing
1.35 J skeleton (probe: `8.774` brine / `0.000` gas / 14 exhausted cells).

**Fluorite in Mode B ✘ (P0)**: `evaluateCellChemistry()`'s formula branch
(`:332-348`) maps `out.gas` straight into `CHEM_GAS[9]`, i.e. lost gas. At the
Fluorite center `(0.225, 0.325, 0.80)`, `evaluateCompositionLaw()` returns
`solubility 0.640`, `gas 0.640`, `brine 0.000` (`:235-262`). Firing a
`model: 'formula'` simulation at the Fluorite column produces **9.505 g of lost
gas worth 116.25 cr and 0 g of green brine** (probe F3). This directly violates
finding 3 ("zero gas lost, 45% concentrate residue"), plan §1 item 7 ("sharing
identical ... gas-loss ... mechanics") and the `B > 0.55` resolution in plan §2.
Test 8 only checks Mode B at HHL/HLH/HHH/LHL/HLL centers, so LLH/Fluorite is
uncovered.

**Gas rock stone residue ✘ (P0 for economy)**: `evaluateCellChemistry()` prices
every residue share at the **parent** mineral's quote (`CHEM_RES_VAL[m] = rMass *
def.price`, `:366`), and `dissolveSubmergedGrain()` uses `unitValue =
dValue/dMass` of the parent (`:1643`). A 1.0 g gas grain therefore leaves `0.15 g`
of "Stone" debris worth **22.50 cr** instead of `0.15 × 2.90 = 0.435 cr`, and
books only 127.50 cr of lost value instead of ~149.57 cr (probe F3). GLM's
corrected reaction table (`prototypes/water-solubility-GLM53/0.3.6-Solubility-GLM53-review.md:118`)
specifies "debris at stone quote, gas gone". The mass/value ledger stays exact
because everything is priced at 150/g, but the *market* is wrong: wet gas mining
keeps 15% of the gas quote as fake stone. Fix requires intentionally booking the
destroyed ~22.07 cr/g into `lostGasValue` (or a separate `valueDestroyed`
channel) so conservation still closes.

### F4 — `debrisLimit` skeleton collapse: mechanically wired, gameplay not delivered

Wired: `DEBRIS_LIMIT = 0.8` (`:28`), `skeletonStrength()` (`:299-315`) computes a
`strengthInto`-style `Σ mix[m] × residue[m] × strength[m]`, and `exposeRockCell()`
(`:1211`) gates probabilistic detachment on `remStrength < 0.8`, with exhaustion
instead of detachment for strong skeletons (`:1128-1141`). Pure Halite → `0.000 J`,
pure Fluorite → `1.350 J`, Quartz stands (probe F4, test 6).

Gaps:

1. **`remStrength` is static.** It is computed once from the original `rockMix`
   and never falls as leaching advances, and jet damage only reduces
   `rockMechHp` — it never feeds the collapse condition. Consequently any cell
   with a modest insoluble skeleton stands forever: 75% Halite + 25%
   Chalcopyrite = `1.200 J`, 60/40 (the karst showcase) = `1.920 J`. Both exceed
   `0.8`. Firing at the karst mixed seam for 6 s yields **0 dissolved cells, 18
   exhausted cells and 0 Copper debris grains** (probe F4), while the scene
   annotation promises "falling Copper clasts" and finding 4 promises that
   "75% Halite cementing 25% Chalcopyrite collapses right on cue". GLM's own lab
   uses `strengthInto(remaining mix) - damage` so the weak jet's accumulated
   damage eventually drops the undermined skeleton below `0.8`; that coupling is
   missing here.
2. **Not actually using the shared API.** The lab hardcodes `0.8` and its own
   `MINERALS[].strength` instead of reading `SteamRockMaterials.debrisLimit` /
   `strengthInto()` (`js/rock-materials.js:43,46`). Tests assert `0.8`, so a live
   economy change could silently diverge. For the live port, import those values
   and have the test compare against them rather than a literal.
3. Plan §6.2 itself is internally inconsistent: its own formula gives 1.2 J for
   the 75/25 example it claims collapses. Either the plan or the threshold needs
   a decision (e.g. scale `remStrength` by remaining mass fraction, or add jet
   `damage` into the collapse check).

### F5 — Live economy and Gold liberation

**Gold split ✔**: `GOLD_GOLD_VALUE_FRACTION = 0.9735` (`:29`, `:1713-1745`),
`pure_gold` quote `573.80`, plain Quartz gangue `5.21`; probe computes 97.35% /
2.65% exactly. Test 7 verifies the nugget's unit value and 25% mass.

**Reference quotes ✔ for a prototype, ✘ for "live"**: all prices are literals in
`MINERALS[]` (`:40-181`); nothing calls
`SteamRockMaterials.basePricesInto()` (`js/rock-materials.js:80`). They match GLM's
13-mineral calibration (target 12.5, mean cell ~11.99) but will not follow the
real market/demand seed. `js/minerals.js` still has 10 rows, so the 13-mineral
quote vector cannot even be computed by the live economy yet.

**Gas residue ✘** — see F3. This is the one place where "exact live-market value"
is actually wrong, not just static.

**Copper-family calibration ⚠**: `experiments/solubility-geology-probe.cjs`
mirrors `autoAdjust` and converges on `copper + vitriol = 7.83%` with calibrated
boxes and host weight `0.0883` (plan §4.2, log in
`experiments/logs/0.3.6-solubility-geology.txt`). But the lab's geology scene
does **not** use those boxes: `loadScene()` compiles `GEO_RANGES` from
`MINERALS[]` with `hostWeight 0.08` (`:895`), where Vitriol is already the
narrowed `r [0.46,0.76] / b [0.54,0.74]` yet Copper, Sand, Halite and Fizzy are
still the §4.1 boxes. Measuring those actual lab boxes over 8 seeds × 192×120
gives `copper + vitriol = 8.30%` (still a +6% buff), not the 9.64% of §4.1 nor
the calibrated 7.83%. Plan §4.2 line 120 says "the lab keeps the narrowed
prototype boxes ... comparable with §4.1", which is inaccurate (§4.1 Vitriol is
`0.42–0.78 / 0.54–0.78`). No test asserts the lab scene is calibrated or that
the live table matches the probe. For the live merge, land the calibrated boxes
and host weight from §4.2 verbatim and re-run `autoAdjust` against the real
13-row table.

### F6 — Per-mineral bookkeeping: done in the lab, absent in the game

Lab ✔: `loads`, `loadValues`, `loadTotal`, `loadsNext`, `jLoads`,
`hopperLoads` are all `Float64Array`s indexed by 14 minerals (`:450-525`);
`transfer()` (`:1413`), pump (`:1594`) and `measureMetrics()` (`:1927`) move and
report exact per-mineral shares; `dye` blending is load-weighted (`:2265`).
`pumpedFizzyMass` / `pumpedFluoriteMass` etc. are exposed (`:2004-2005`).

Live ✘: `js/minerals.js` has 10 rows with no `sol`, `rho`, `dye`, `ventF`,
`leachFloor` or `residueF` (probe F5: `10 / 0`); there is no `js/water.js`. The
lab also names the field `density` while the plan's drop-in schema uses `rho`
(`:48`, plan §4.2 rule 4) — a trivial rename to settle before porting, otherwise
the live `strength`/`rho`/`sol` symmetry the finding asks for is lost.

### F7 — Mode B calibration: tweak applied, documentation and law disagree

Applied: `hydrate = smoothstep(0.15, 0.42, r*b*(1-0.65*g))` (`:237`). Recomputing
the plan's published formula at the §4.1 Vitriol box center `(0.60, 0.21, 0.66)`
reproduces GLM's `0.676` with the old `0.18–0.48` window and `0.914` with the new
one; the lab returns `0.907`. So the intended fix works (0.68 → ~0.91), but:

- Plan §5 line 161 claims Blue Vitriol `(1.00)`; **0.91 is not 1.00**. GLM only
  promised "much closer agreement", so the plan overstates. Test 8 merely asserts
  `out.vitriol > 0.5`, so nothing locks the calibration in.
- The implemented law is **not** the plan's published `f(R,G,B)` (plan §5): it
  adds `fHal = ionic(1-0.35·degas)`, `fVit = hydrate(1-0.45·ionic)`,
  `fGas = degas(0.42+0.48(1-r))`, a global normalisation, and a leftover
  `reactTotal >= 0.22` gate that zeroes all reactivity (`:241-261`). At the Gas
  center the lab returns `0.802` solubility vs the plan's `~0.61`; at the
  Fluorite center `0.640` **all in the gas channel** vs `~0.52` intended as
  leachable brine. The old "arbitrary 22% reactivity cutoff" that finding 4
  wanted to remove survives here as a solubility gate.
- Mode B therefore also misses F3's Fluorite leach floor and gives Blue Vitriol
  an 11% gas-loss side channel (`0.798 brine / 0.110 gas` at its center) that
  Mode A never has.

Fix direction: replace the ad-hoc normalisation with the published formula plus
an explicit **bound-B** rule (hydrate-bound B → blue brine; crystal-bound B →
55% brine + 45% residue; free B → lost gas), then assert both modes' channel
splits (not just total solubility) at all eight octant centers.

### F8 — Deferred extras: correctly deferred

`solubility.2-plan.md` tracks evaporative salt crusts, settling basins, Beaker
Fill/Drain, dissolution strip chart and 0.5×–4× time warp as low priority. The
lab has Pause/Step but none of those features (probe F8). This matches the
finding's "worth considering later".

## Cross-cutting issues

1. **Test coverage does not match the claims.** 9/9 tests pass, but there is no
   test for: Mode B reaction channels at Fluorite/Gas/Vitriol centers, gas-rock
   residue value at the stone quote, mixed-cell collapse / Copper clast release,
   pump-then-fresh-water saturation recovery, or the lab geology scene's
   calibrated shares. Test 6 checks only static `skeletonStrength()` of pure
   cells, which is why the 60/40 and 75/25 gap survived.
2. **Plan drift.** §1 item 7 (identical gas-loss mechanics), §5 line 161
   (Vitriol 1.00), §4.2 line 120 (lab keeps §4.1 boxes) and §6.2's 75/25 collapse
   example are all contradicted by the shipped code. These are documentation
   defects, not just code defects; fix them together with the code so the plan
   stays usable as a standalone reimplementation artifact (AGENTS.md).
3. **Style nits (AGENTS.md no-allocation rule).** `stepFluidGrid()` declares
   `function transfer(...)` inside the per-frame function (`:1413`) — one closure
   allocation per frame; `measureMetrics()` allocates two `Float64Array(14)` per
   call (`:1929-1930`) and is called from the HUD every 4 frames. Both are small
   but easy to hoist into module scope / preallocated scratch.
4. **Conservation ledger scope.** `carve` (`:2083-2100`) deletes rock without
   touching `totalReactedMass`, and exhausted standing skeletons remain outside
   both `expectedMass` and `accountedMass`. This is internally consistent but the
   "exact conservation" claim in the plan applies only to *reacted* mass; worth
   stating explicitly before live integration.
