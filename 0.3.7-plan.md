# 0.3.7 — Balance and performance

## Goal

Tune the economy and performance of the new systems and keep save/load correct.

## Decisions

- Calibrate tier-III equipment and late progression costs against the realised
  income spread of the live six-mineral market once top-row growth, rockets and
  the sandblaster are in place.
- Balance rockets and sandblaster economy.
- Cap physical particle counts.
- Pool fragments and sandblaster particles.
- Batch ceiling damage updates.
- Tune collapse probability and volume.
- Add telemetry for expected value per energy unit.
- Verify save/load for new resources and settings.

## Suggested implementation

- Measure shift income across seeds with the live market, top-row replenishment,
  rockets and sandblaster active, then retune tier-III equipment and late
  research prices against that realised income distribution.
- Add pooling and batching in `js/ceiling.js` and effect modules.
- Add telemetry readouts.
- Add migration-safe save/load handling.
- Add performance tests.

- No unbounded particle growth.
- Economy meets the planned zero-profit targets and tier-III progression pacing.
- Save/load preserves new state.
