# 0.3.6 — Sandblaster aggregate

## Goal

Add a sandblaster that uses energy and abrasive storage, with particles that
return and can be collected again.

## Decisions

### Resources

- Uses energy and abrasive material storage.
- Material is spent from storage on firing.
- Material is not destroyed: particles fall back and can be collected.
- Loss comes only from uncollected material, not from artificial deletion.

### Physics and damage

- Fires a stream of small stones upward.
- Stones hit the ceiling.
- Each hit deals small cumulative damage.
- One hit is usually not enough to destroy even one pixel.
- Effective impact area is about 1 px, requiring several hits.
- After impact particles fall like other debris.

### Balance

- High energy cost.
- Near zero or slightly negative profit at base energy and cheap minerals.
- Becomes profitable with expensive minerals, better engines, energy efficiency
  and good material recovery.

## Suggested implementation

- Add energy and storage costs.
- Add particle stream and collision.
- Reuse debris collection for recovery.
- Add tests for cost, damage accumulation and recovery.

## Acceptance

- Material is conserved except for collection losses.
- Damage accumulates as specified.
- Energy cost keeps early use unprofitable.
