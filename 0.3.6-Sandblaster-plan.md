# 0.3.6 — Sandblaster

## Goal

Add a sandblaster that uses energy and the hopper's own debris as abrasive,
with grains that return and can be collected again.

## Decisions

### Resources

- Uses reserve energy and the owner's cargo. Nothing is bought: debris
  returns and its mass is preserved, so there is no ammunition, no magazine
  and no shop purchase.
- Only sand and stone are abrasive. A grain is 20 g of the hopper's sand, or
  of its stone once the sand is gone (sand is nearly worthless, so it goes
  first). Ores are never thrown; a hopper with neither leaves the nozzle dry.
  This is the challenge: the nozzle has to be fed by mining first, and a sale
  sells the abrasive with the rest of the cargo.
- A grain leaves the cargo with its mass, its mineral and its value at the
  live quote, and returns to cargo with the same mineral and the same value,
  so a round trip neither makes nor loses anything.
- Material is not destroyed: a grain that hits the rock falls back as debris.
  Any platform's collector with room catches it into that platform's own
  hopper; a grain that misses is spilled onto the floor rubble where it lands
  and can be graded up like any other rubble.
- A full hopper stops the nozzle: nothing it loosened could be caught and the
  grains could not come home. A full hopper also refuses a returning grain.
- Every hopper carries a per-mineral mass. The rig's is booked from the
  fragments it catches. A rival harvests blended floor rubble, which has no
  composition, so its harvest is booked at the rock's measured abundance.

### Control

- Hold the fire key or the Blast button to keep the stream going.
- A player's Auto-fire is a per-owner standing order that survives New game.
  It holds the rig's fire under 20% reserve; a hand throw is not limited.
- Bots issue a separate, temporary auto-fire duty when a nozzle is installed,
  abrasive is aboard, the hopper has room and that owner's engine can refill
  at least as fast as the stream draws. The rig starts a charge trip with one
  second of stream draw in hand and pauses its bot stream for trips, manual
  travel and heat near the drive derating band. Unchecking Auto-fire pauses a
  bot stream until BOT is cycled; turning BOT off never erases a player's order.

### Physics and damage

- The sandblaster is a nozzle in the collector. Firing throws 20 g grains
  from its lip at 135 J each, up to 20 per second (2.7 kW stream). A grain
  inherits the platform's horizontal and vertical speed at fire time, plus
  its relative muzzle speed; after that it moves independently under gravity.
  A steady cruise keeps it over the collector, but acceleration leaves it
  behind. Only live particle positions are drawn, never origin-to-grain lines.
- Grains are ballistic: no motor, no fuse, no duds. A grain decelerates
  under gravity, reaches the rock face and chips it through the shared
  `SteamCeiling.chip` rule with a one-cell radius and the energy it gives up.
- One hit is usually not enough to destroy even one pixel: the weakening
  rule takes at most half a cell's strength per blow, so several hits are
  needed and the effective impact area is about one cell.
- After impact the grain falls from the impact point with its travel and a
  small spread.
- Energy ledger: the draw is the grain's whole launch energy, priced exactly
  like the piston's pulse. The rig's hopper is inside the ledger, so a rig
  grain also takes its cargo height and its share of the platform's motion
  out with it and gives them back on a rig catch. Vertical motion prescribed
  by the terrain is booked as terrain work at launch. At impact the share the
  rock took is booked to fracture energy and the rest to heat; a spill books
  the grain's remaining energy to heat. A rival's nozzle, body and hopper are
  outside the rig's ledger: its throw books as imported work, and a grain it
  catches leaves the ledger whole as heat. The mine's total energy is
  conserved across firing, impact, catch and spill.
- Mining statistics (extracted, caught, lost cells) count mined rock only; a
  grain's trip changes cargo, not those counters.

### Balance

- The revised draw is **135 J per grain**, 10× the previous 13.5 J; doubling
  the rate to 20/s makes a held stream draw 2.7 kW (20× the old 135 W).
  The material cost remains only what is spilled. The old near-zero-profit
  *paid-energy* target was superseded by the requested high energy draw: with
  the same one-cell chip footprint and half-cell cap, retuning yield to
  cover 10× more paid energy would change the rock/damage model instead.
- The [yield probe](experiments/sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs) parks under fresh
  rock for 30 s, across six seeds and seven positions, prices all energy at
  the 25 cr/kJ emergency import rate, and subtracts spilled abrasive. It
  fires a mean 237 grains (some positions exhaust their reserve), catches a
  median 57.5 cr at ordinary prices and **loses a median 518.5 cr** there;
  the market-price median is -445.8 cr. Stationary firing spills no abrasive.
  Free pad energy and richer seams, not paid base-energy mining, can make it
  worthwhile. The measured output is in
  `experiments/logs/0.3.6-sandblaster-yield.txt`.

## Suggested implementation

- `js/sandblaster.js`: owner profiles (auto-fire, counters), a fixed
  owner-tagged grain pool carrying each grain's mineral and value, the feed
  (sand, then stone), fire (rate-limited stream, energy and cargo), the grain
  step (climb, impact, fall, catch, spill), draw.
- `js/ceiling.js`: `unloadCargo` / `loadCargo` for loose hopper material and
  `spill` onto the floor, shared with the landed-fragment deposit; fractional
  debris cells are kept in a floating-point pool after partial full-hopper
  catches, preserving their mass, value and energy.
- `js/players.js`: the rival hopper's per-mineral mass, booked at harvest
  and cleared at sale, with the same unload/load pair; bots run their own
  nozzle duty if their engine can sustain the draw, and the rig's recharge
  threshold anticipates the stream.
- `js/physics.js`: a shared collector-position accessor for the nozzle,
  the step and reset hooks.
- `js/equipment.js`: the Impact-gated sandblaster shop item.
- Page: the sandblaster panel (Blast with the grains aboard, auto-fire,
  status), the fire key, the nozzle and stream drawing, cargo readouts that
  show fractions of a cell, the help topic, the README.
- Tests for the feed order, the full-hopper stop, auto-fire, damage
  accumulation, the return to cargo and the spill, the rival hopper, the
  energy ledger, and pool bounds; the yield probe for the economy.

## Acceptance

- Material is conserved: every grain ends in a hopper or on the floor.
- Only sand and stone are thrown; ore mass in the hopper never changes.
- Damage accumulates through the shared weakening rule; one hit never
  destroys a cell by itself.
- The energy ledger stays balanced across firing, impact, catch and spill,
  parked and on the move.
- The high-power stream and the new paid-energy yield (negative at ordinary
  prices) are recorded by the probe; profitability requires cheap energy or
  richer rock. The old 13.5 J break-even baseline no longer applies.
