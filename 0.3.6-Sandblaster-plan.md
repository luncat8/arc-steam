# 0.3.6 — Sandblaster

## Goal

Add a sandblaster that uses energy and abrasive storage, with particles that
return and can be collected again.

## Decisions

### Resources

- Uses reserve energy and an abrasive magazine, both per owner.
- Each grain fired spends one charge of the owner's magazine and a fixed
  amount of reserve energy at the moment of firing.
- The abrasive is bought at a SELL wall like rocket rounds, debits the
  owner's own purse and ledger, and has its own per-owner standing
  auto-buy order. The magazine is a fixed capacity.
- Material is not destroyed: a grain that hits the rock falls back and can be
  collected again, returning one charge to the owner's magazine.
- Loss comes only from uncollected material: grains that reach the floor,
  leave the chamber or fall outside every collector are gone.

### Physics and damage

- The sandblaster is a nozzle in the collector. Firing throws a stream of
  small grains straight up from the nozzle at a fixed launch speed; the
  launch energy is the per-grain reserve draw, so the grain carries exactly
  what was spent.
- Grains are ballistic: no motor, no fuse, no duds. A grain decelerates
  under gravity, reaches the rock face and chips it through the shared
  `SteamCeiling.chip` rule with a one-cell radius and the grain's remaining
  kinetic energy.
- One hit is usually not enough to destroy even one pixel: the weakening
  rule takes at most half a cell's strength per blow, so several hits are
  needed and the effective impact area is about one cell.
- After impact the grain falls like other debris from the impact point with
  a small spread. Any platform's collector can catch a falling grain and
  the charge returns to the grain's owner.
- Energy ledger: the draw is the grain's whole launch energy — its lift and
  its speed, priced exactly like the piston's pulse — so the grain carries
  exactly what was spent. At impact the share the rock took is booked to
  fracture energy and the rest to heat; the fall and the catch or loss book
  the rest to heat. The mine's total energy is conserved across firing,
  impact and recovery. A rival's nozzle is machinery outside the rig's
  ledger, so its throw books as imported work the way its piston pulse does.

### Balance

- High energy cost per grain plus the abrasive purchase price.
- Near zero or slightly negative profit at base energy and cheap minerals.
- Becomes profitable with expensive minerals, better engines, energy
  efficiency and good material recovery.
- Calibrated with a yield probe (`experiments/sandblaster-yield-probe.cjs`,
  template: `experiments/rocket-yield-probe.cjs`): sustained firing at a
  fixed rock measures the caught value against the energy drawn and the
  net abrasive spent, across seeds, at ordinary-rock prices. The constants
  are tuned so the base case is around zero.

Measured (`experiments/logs/0.3.6-sandblaster-yield.txt`, 30 s held stream,
six seeds, seven positions, 300 grains per run at the 10/s rate): with the
calibrated draw of 13.5 J per grain and 3 cr per charge, the catch valued at
ordinary-rock prices gives a median profit of 2.2 cr — the base case is at
zero. The same catch at market prices is a median 196.5 cr, so profit comes
from rich seams; the abrasive spend is zero when the stream is held under the
seam (every grain is caught), and any grain lost to the floor or the chamber
edge costs its 3 cr charge, which is the material cost of poor recovery.

### Scope

- The sandblaster is a manual platform tool in this milestone: the player
  holds the fire key or the Blast button to work a seam. Bot operation is
  a follow-up, like the rig's rocket duty was before rockets.2.

## Suggested implementation

- `js/sandblaster.js`: owner profiles (charges, auto-buy, counters), a
  fixed owner-tagged grain pool, fire (rate-limited stream, energy and
  charge), the grain step (climb, impact, fall, catch, loss), draw.
- `js/physics.js`: a shared collector-position accessor for the nozzle,
  the step and reset hooks.
- `js/equipment.js`: the Impact-gated sandblaster shop item.
- Page: the sandblaster panel (Blast, Buy, auto-buy, status), the fire
  key, the nozzle and stream drawing, the help topic, the README.
- Tests for cost, damage accumulation, recovery and loss, the energy
  ledger, and pool bounds; the yield probe for the economy.

## Acceptance

- Material is conserved except for collection losses.
- Damage accumulates through the shared weakening rule; one hit never
  destroys a cell by itself.
- The energy ledger stays balanced across firing, impact and recovery.
- The base economy is around zero profit; the probe records the numbers.
