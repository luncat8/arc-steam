PISTON TILT

Tilt the reflector piston from −10° to +10° to steer the bounce: use this slider, the mouse wheel over the chamber, or drag in open air. A positive angle raises the right edge and sends a falling ball left; negative sends it right. The piston compresses in proportion to the impact speed, then pushes back out.

Hold A / D to drive or brake. W / S adjusts force. Space or Q toggles the bounce drive. Click rock to inspect a cell; click low near the ground to set a travel target. The collector docks into the left-wall pocket so the reflector can cover the wall.
