Hybrid (Jet + 2D Cells) Crisp aimed ballistic jet + Marching Squares sub-cell surface with horizontal wave momentum and smooth waterfall streams. Unlimited flooded volume at fixed memory; handles overhangs, ledges, and U-tube siphons natively with no particle cap. Particles (2D SPH / DDR) Unified jet, splash, slosh and pool; organic droplets & surface tension via density-field Marching Squares. Exact per-particle slurry; wide caves spread particles thin unless sleeping/pooling layer is added.
Grid+Particle (2D FLIP) Incompressible real waves, splashes and bow wake; flat calm pools with zero spongy bounce. Great cave boundary handling via MAC solid mask, but still capped by particle count (~2,400) under unlimited flooding. Exact per-particle slurry; MAC grid + particles are heavier to stream across 512-row Wild Mine caves.
