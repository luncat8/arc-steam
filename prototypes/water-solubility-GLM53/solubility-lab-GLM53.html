The model (short)
  • Water is a G+B compound (crystal non-metal + gas, the H2O of this world): polar, kin to gas B, hungry for metal R, unable to break the G-framework. Water never chips intact rock — the jet is a delivery system (0.05 J vs 1.3–7.8 J/cell strengths).
  • Solubility is a class in the 13-mineral table, weighed per mineral the way strengthInto() weighs strength: instant (Halite, Blue Vitriol), fast (Fizzy Halite, Gas rock), slow (Fluorite-salt), none (silicates, dry chalcopyrite, native metals, sand).
  • Three wet reactions. Full dissolution: halite and vitriol enter the water 100% (green +G brine, blue +GB brine; blends read teal). Decomposition: fizzy halite (R2G3B) vents its B share as lost gas (16.7%), the rest dissolves green, and the bed's inclusions — quartz, chalcopyrite or the host stone — remain as the residue skeleton; gas rock vents 85% and drops 15% as stone debris; fluorite-salt leaches only its salt matrix and stops at a 45% concentrate floor (the B never enters solution, so nothing valuable vents). Entrainment: see below.
  • Saturation stands: a parcel of water dissolves at most its capacity (default 60 g per water unit) — fresh flow cuts, brine stalls, downstream salt survives a loaded stream.
  • Erosion is mechanical and density-sorted: a flow lifts a resting grain only above v_crit(rho) = 0.78·max(0.35, rho−1)/erosionPower (x0.7 to cells/s), carries it for one 0.18–0.5 s hop — shorter for heavier grains — and drops it when the flow slows to 0.65·v_crit. Sand (rho 1.6) washes in a trickle; quartz (2.6) hops in a sluice; silver (10.5) barely moves; a liberated gold nugget (19.3) never leaves the trap.
  • Gold panning: a loose gold-bearing-quartz grain washed by flowing water liberates into 25% pure-gold nugget (92% of the value, rho 19.3, stays) + 75% quartz gangue (washes away).
  • The platform pumps the brine dry: in Pump mode hold LMB — the hose sucks water units and their exact per-mineral load into the hopper, booked in grams and value. Solids are never pumped: dry collection and panning stay their own game.
  • Vented gas is gone for good — the expensive lesson: gas quotes at the reference cap (150/cell), and wet degassing burns it into bubbles. The ledger books the loss in value, not just grams.
