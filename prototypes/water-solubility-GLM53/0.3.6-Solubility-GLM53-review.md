# 0.3.6 — Solubility plan: independent review & verified numbers

A second-opinion pass over `0.3.6-Solubility-plan.md`, checked against the real
system: `js/minerals.js`, `js/geology.js`, `js/geology-config.js`,
`js/rock-materials.js` (verbatim copies), measured the same way the plan
measures — 8 seeds × 192 × 120 cells, frequency 0.075, yCompression 3,
mode `balanced`, specificity 0.6, hostWeight 0.08.

**Verdict: the ideas fit the system, and the plan's abundance tables are real.**
Every share in §4.1 reproduces to ±0.01 pts. Three mass-balance/economy points
must be fixed before merge (§3): the fizzy-halite vent exceeds its own B
content, fluorite contradicts the plan's own B-escape rule, and the saturation
term that makes brine stall has been dropped from `chemRate`.

## 3. Issues found (fix before merge)

1. **Fizzy halite vents more gas than it holds.** `R₂G₃B` normalises to
   b = 16.7%, but §4.1 vents 35% of the mass. Rule: **the vented share is the
   analog's B share**, never more. `R₂G₃B` → 16.7% gas lost + 83.3% green
   brine; if the fizz should be louder, restate the analog (`R₂G₃B₂` → 28.6%).
2. **Fluorite breaks the plan's own B-rule.** The box is b 0.62–0.98, the rule
   says B > 0.55 cannot stay bound in wet solution — yet §4.1 leaches 55% of
   fluorite into green brine with no gas loss. Fix that keeps both: **leach
   floor** — water dissolves only the salt matrix (R·G part, ~55% of mass) as
   green brine; the B-rich crystal (~45%) never enters solution and stays as a
   solid *fluorite concentrate* residue (full value, no gas lost). The existing
   `debrisLimit` then decides whether the leached skeleton still stands
   (pure seams hold at 1.35 J; vein-grade cells at ≤55% fraction crumble to
   concentrate grains — both behaviours are correct and readable).
3. **Saturation term is missing.** §6.1 `chemRate` has no
   `× (1 − load/capacity)`; a standing puddle would then eat a mountain of
   halite. Restore it (default 60 g per water unit, slider-tunable): fresh
   flow cuts, brine stalls, downstream rock survives a saturated stream.
4. **Soluble debris is immortal in §7.** The saltation states carry no
   chemistry, so a crushed halite grain never dissolves. Keep erosion
   mechanical *while carried*, but a resting submerged soluble grain must
   still dissolve chemically at `rate × surface factor` (×3).
5. **The copper family grows +23%.** Baseline copper 7.83% → chalcopyrite
   4.43% + vitriol 5.21% = 9.64%: vitriol's B window reaches up to 0.78, past
   the old copper box's 0.69, harvesting new cells. Either accept it as a
   copper buff (copper is the most demand-swinging ore) or narrow vitriol
   (e.g. r .50–.74) and recalibrate with `autoAdjust`.
6. **Show the gas bill.** Gas quotes at the 150 cap; venting 85% of a gas
   pocket is the single most expensive mistake a wet miner can make. The
   ledger/HUD should book `lostGasValue`, not just grams.
7. **Liberation value split vs the market.** Gangue booked as plain quartz
   carries ~2.7% of the lump's value, not the plan's 8% — total conservation
   lands at ~95.5%. Accept that, or price the gangue share at 0.107× the
   gold-quartz quote for an exact 92/8.
8. **Mode B drifts at vitriol's centre** (solubility 0.68 vs Mode A's 1.00).
   Widen the hydrate smoothstep (0.18–0.48 → ~0.15–0.42) or accept the
   divergence; halite (1.00), fluorite (0.52 ≈ its 55% leach), gas (0.61) and
   dry copper (0.00) all agree.

## 6. The lab

`experiments/solubility-lab.html` (single file, no deps) updated to 0.3.6b to
demonstrate the reconciled model: 13 formation minerals + the liberated
pure-gold nugget, per-mineral brine loads with dye tinting (green/blue/teal),
the fizzy bed as a real mixture with quartz/chalcopyrite inclusions, the
fluorite leach floor (55%/45%, no gas loss), gas rock venting 85% + stone
debris, density-sorted saltation (`v_crit(ρ)`, 0.18–0.50 s hops), placer
liberation, saturation kept, and an under-platform **pump** whose hopper books
grams and value at the reference quotes. Three scenes: `Field` (vitriol
upper half / chalcopyrite lower half), `Columns` (one pure column per mineral,
beaker-immersion verification) and `Placer` (stepped sluice with a water feed).
`window.__lab` exposes the same probes plus `hopper()`, `brineDye()` and
`grainStats()`. Verified headless, seed 1337: beaker immersion of the columns
scene dissolves exactly the five soluble minerals and stops at saturation
(77,760 g = 1296 wu x 60 g), books 24.6 kg vented gas at 3,449 lost value and
4.05 kg stone residue; the fluorite column leaches to 0.454 against its 0.45
floor with zero B vented; the pump moves 120 wu into the hopper as exactly
7,200 g of brine worth 78.2; a 25 s jet on the fizzy outcrop leaches the salt,
vents the B share and crumbles the chalcopyrite inclusion into 15 copper
grains; and 60 s of placer wash liberates 30/30 gold-quartz lumps — nuggets
hold at the source (mean x 24.7) while the gangue washes downstream (mean x
33.4+). The mass ledger holds 0.0-0.05 g through every scenario.
