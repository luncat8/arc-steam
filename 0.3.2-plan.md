# 0.3.2 — Top rock growth

## Goal

The rock ceiling grows downward from the top: one new row is added on every
ball bounce, so the playable space is replenished from above.

## Decisions

- Each upward ball bounce off a platform deck or elastic ground queues one new
  rock row at the top of the chamber (`y = 15 m`).
- Queued rows slide in smoothly at a bounded rate (5 rows/s, shared with 0.3.3)
  instead of popping in on the bounce frame.
- Existing intact rock, its remaining strength, looseness, mineral mix, live
  prices and smoothed collision profile shift downward together without jumps.
- New top rows continue the seeded 3-channel Y-compressed element noise and
  material-table formation seamlessly in geological row coordinates (or pure
  mineral bands when test rock is active).
- Completely mined bottom rows compact without moving surviving cells in world
  space.
- Rock thickness is capped at 70% of chamber height (`10.5 m`, leaving at least
  30% / `4.5 m` open below): lower intact rock is never destroyed, and no new
  lines are added while 30% or less of chamber height remains below the lowest
  intact rock until lower rows are mined clear.
- Vertical perspective stays anchored to the upper chamber span with unit scale
  below `11.375 m`, keeping collision geometry, rendering and hover hit-testing
  aligned while preserving exact chamber energy accounting.

## Suggested implementation

- Add a top-row insertion queue (`queueGrowth`, `grow`) and fractional downward
  shift state in `js/ceiling.js` backed by preallocated row buffers.
- Gate queueing and downward stepping on `lowestIntactY() - minBottom`
  (`minBottom = 0.3 * top = 4.5 m`) without retiring intact lower cells.
- Trigger `queueGrowth(1)` on platform and elastic-ground bounces in
  `js/physics.js`.
- Generate incoming rows through a shared row-formation helper using geological
  row depth and revalue at current market quotes.
- Book incoming strata and prescribed subsidence in `exportedEnergy` so
  `totalEnergy` remains invariant.
- Add tests for bounce-triggered queueing, smooth fractional entry, mineral
  continuity, non-destructive 70% height cap, coordinate/render alignment and
  energy conservation.

## Acceptance

- One bounce queues and adds one top row while more than 30% chamber height
  remains below the rock.
- Intact bottom rock is never destroyed at the 70% height cap, and new lines
  stop being added until lower rock is mined above `4.5 m`.
- No jump in ceiling heightmap, rendered rows, tooltip selection or ball state
  on the bounce frame or across row boundaries.
- Physics, perspective rendering and chamber energy accounting remain aligned.
