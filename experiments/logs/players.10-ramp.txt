run after: the unfold reach follows the closing speed, the plate holds until the
other hull's near edge has passed the ramp's tip, and a hull fast enough to cross
the deck inside one plate swing brings both ends down at once

approach - the plate angle at the moment a wheel leaves the floor onto the plate
run-off  - the plate angle at the moment a wheel leaves the deck onto the plate
"over N" - the worst of N wheel crossings in that run; 45° is the full ramp
