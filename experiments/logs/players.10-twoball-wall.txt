wallmax is the longest stint pinned against a selling wall. A selling trip is
wall time by design, so it is read next to `any` and `T`: a long stint with a
ball in play and a chamber over its thermal cutoff is a machine standing still
while the mine is playable. That is what x0=15 under `chase` is — 36.6 s at
146 °C, the release of a held ball gated on a pulse the thermal derate has taken
away. A 150 s run of the same start holds the wall for 67.5 s.
