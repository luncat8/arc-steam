== reading ==

The garage grader is the opening. Calling it at 0 s is worth +946 cr over
480 s and is already +769 cr by 240 s, because it halves the floor it sweeps
(free value the rig never mines itself) and leaves the rig's own trips intact.

The road grader is not the second purchase. A paid blade nets +265 cr against
doing nothing and *less* than the free call alone (1273 vs 1492) because it
buys at the first 300 cr — around 60 s — which is exactly when the purse is
about to compound into pulse and force:

  blade (paid)        811 cr
  blade (granted)    1704 cr

The same machine, with its price removed, out-earns every other opening: its
blade earns 369–395 cr of its own shavings (50% share) and it deepens the
floor for the garage grader. So the 300 cr is not the problem — the timing is.
A blade bought after the pulse/force chain pays back; one bought first costs
the first two research nodes and the harness still ends poorer at 480 s.

Open question: the blade's own proceeds should be reinvested by a policy (an
"opening" autopilot duty) rather than by the player, and the price should be
tied to the floor depth it can actually work. The probe is the place to test
both: `experiments/grader-worth.cjs` takes a strategy row per policy.
