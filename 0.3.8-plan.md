# 0.3.8 — Balance and performance

## Goal

Keep tool power, engine storage, pad reload, cooling, market payouts and frame
cost aligned across the full progression from a bare starter rig to a fully
upgraded multi-tool machine, while preserving exact ledger closure and clean
state handling.

## Tool inventory and resource coupling

Every powered reserve consumer routes through `SteamPhysics.spend(world, useful)`
so reserve cost is `useful / engine.efficiency` and motor loss `cost - useful`
enters machine heat directly. Self-contained or passive gear constrains other
axes instead of the reserve:

| Tool | Unlock / cost / mass | Useful work & reserve draw | Heat & thermal rule | Primary limiters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Movement | Stock | `dKE / η`; `480 → 1920 N`, `12 → 24 m/s` (scaled by engine) | Motor loss + unrecovered braking; derates `85 → 135 °C`, cuts off at `135 °C` | Thrust, braking heat, pack size; empty pack falls back to `60 N / 0.8 m/s` hand crank |
| Reflector piston (+ pad, coil, ram, strike balls) | Stock → `42 → 84 J` pulse; balls `+0/+3/+5 kg` | `pulse / η` per powered bounce | Motor loss + deck/rock impact loss + fallen debris PE; derates `85 → 135 °C`, cuts off at `135 °C` | Pulse size at start, bounce retention with spiked ball, hull heat on dirty engines |
| Brake + flywheel | `storage` + `flywheel` (`560 cr`, `+14 kg`) | Recovers `55% × 90% = 49.5%` of brake KE into reserve | Cuts braking heat roughly in half; brake always works at any temperature or charge | Dry mass on weak drives; wall impacts stay unrecovered |
| Ramp | Stock (`0 cr`, `0 kg`) | None | None | Chamber-wide ride-over vs bumper contact choice |
| Commercial grader | Root service (`0 cr`, `0 kg`) | None (external vehicle) | None | Pays 50% of swept rubble; slow shuttle cadence |
| Road grader | Root (`300 cr`, `+10 kg`, rig only) | `45 N · |v| / η` while shaving rubble | Full rolling work becomes heat | Mass and continuous drag heat on starter rigs; pays 50% of shaved rubble |
| Ballistic computer I / II | `520` / `700 cr` (`0 kg`) | None (cuts movement waste via timed intercepts) | Lowers braking heat in smart mode | Research cost and prerequisite depth |
| Rocket rack | `impact` + `480 cr` (`+8 kg`), `14 cr`/round | `0 J` reserve (self-propelled, 6 rounds, 0.5 s reload) | `0 W` machine heat | Purse, 6-round rack capacity, wall reload trips (3/visit) |
| Sandblaster | `impact` + `520 cr` (`+7 kg`) | `135 J` useful/grain × `20/s` = `2.70 kW` useful (`3.38 kW` stock electric) | Motor loss at launch + unfractured impact/catch loss; pauses at `135 °C` cutoff | Starter reserve (`~5–7 s`), cooling, sand/stone hopper feed, full-hopper stop |
| Water gun + under-hull pump | `hydrology` + `900 cr` (`+9 kg`) | Jet `36 J` useful/drop × `46/s` (`1.66 kW`) + pump `120 J` useful/unit × `2.6 u/s` (`0.31 kW`) = `1.97 kW` useful (`2.46 kW` stock electric; ~`82 / 18%` split) | Full reserve draw enters heat; both jet and pump pause at `135 °C` cutoff and cap at available reserve | Starter reserve (`~7–8 s`), cooling, mineral solubility (gas loss), brine saturation, full-hopper stop |

## Engine, storage and cooling progression

Source packs hold `4 × min(½ m v_max², F · span) / η`, growing naturally as
traction research and drive motors raise `F` and `v_max`. Convective cooling is
`3.6 W/K` on a bare hull, `5.0 W/K` with **Heat transfer** (`+1.4 W/K`), and
`+140 W/(m²·K)` across radiator fins: **Vent grille** `0.15 m²` (`+21 W/K`,
`4 kg`), **Radiator array** `0.35 m²` (`+49 W/K`, `8 kg`), and **Heavy radiator
bank** `0.70 m²` (`+98 W/K`, `16 kg`), plus Stefan–Boltzmann radiation.

Binding limits shift deliberately by stage and engine archetype:

- **Stage 1 — Starter rig (tier-1 drive, bare `3.6 W/K` hull):** runs a single
  core duty or economical smart movement + piston continuously on stock electric
  without crossing `85 °C`. Aggressive chase driving, adding the road grader, or
  running a 20%-efficient coal boiler crosses into thermal derating until **Heat
  transfer** or a **Vent grille** is fitted. Heavy radiators penalise tier-1
  acceleration. Fitting a sandblaster or water gun onto an un-upgraded engine
  yields only a brief burst (`0.05–0.2 s` reserve limit on hand, `1.6–3.6 s`
  thermal cutoff on coal, `5–8 s` reserve drain on oil and electric, `7–10 s`
  bare-hull thermal cutoff on nuclear); BOT refuses streams an engine's pad
  power cannot sustain (`hand`, tier-1 `coal`).
- **Stage 2 — Midgame rig (tier-2 drive + Vent grille / Radiator array):**
  **Vent grille** (`26 W/K`) carries continuous movement + piston + road grader
  at 100% output across electric, oil and nuclear and tames coal dry mining.
  **Radiator array** (`54 W/K`) sustains one continuous high-draw nozzle
  (sandblaster or water gun + pump) alongside movement, piston and grader:
  electric runs near 100% thermal uptime and binds mainly on reserve trips, oil
  minimises pad downtime (`3 s` reload) with moderate cooling pauses, nuclear
  stretches pack endurance (`187 kJ`) with moderate cooling pauses, and coal
  remains sharply heat-limited (`5×` reserve draw per useful joule). Running
  both nozzles together outstrips midgame cooling and reserves.
- **Stage 3 — Fully upgraded rig (tier-3 drive + flywheel + Heavy radiator bank,
  `103 W/K`):** sustains concurrent multi-tool operation (movement, rammed
  piston, spiked ball, road grader, rockets, sandblaster, water gun and brine
  pump) continuously with zero thermal cutoff on electric (coolest, `~100%`
  drive output), oil (fastest `3 s` refill, mild derate under unthrottled dual
  nozzles), and nuclear (largest useful reserve at `183 kJ` / `333 kJ` raw,
  lowest pad frequency, mild derate under unthrottled dual nozzles). Coal keeps
  its dirty-boiler identity: comfortable for full dry play or burst wet/sand
  work, still thermal-limited under simultaneous continuous dual nozzles. Hand
  stays a minimalist light pack bounded by thrust and storage.

## Economy, performance and state decisions

- Keep paid emergency fuel (`25 cr/kJ`) unprofitable for bulk sandblasting and
  water leaching on ordinary rock; free centre-pad reloads and targeted rich or
  soluble seams provide positive shift return.
- Calibrate tier-III equipment and late research prices against realised shift
  income across seeds with live ten-mineral market cycles, top-row replenishment,
  rockets, sandblaster and water gun all active.
- Keep physical particle pools bounded (fragments, rocket shards, sandblaster
  grains, water droplets and wet residue grains) with zero per-step allocations.
- Skip wet-chamber grid and solid-mask work when the chamber is dry, batch
  ceiling damage updates, and bound active wet-parcel scanning so multi-tool
  play stays within frame budget.
- Consider an optional solute-gated pump mode or idle-suction taper if players
  want to leave barren rinse water unpumped without toggling `Pump` manually.
- Add expected-value-per-energy telemetry for active mining tools.
- Verify migration-safe save/load coverage for all resources, upgrades and
  standing tool orders.

## Acceptance

- Starter electric runs core single-tool / smart play continuously below `85 °C`;
  weak packs exhaust or thermally gate sand and water streams within seconds;
  upgraded tier-3 rigs with the heavy radiator bank sustain full multi-tool
  operation without thermal cutoff.
- Every reserve-powered tool obeys engine efficiency, thermal cutoff and exact
  `SteamPhysics.totalEnergy` closure (`< 1e-5 J` drift across full shifts).
- Particle counts stay bounded and dry/wet simulation steps stay within frame
  budget.
- Late-tier research and shop costs match measured multi-shift income across
  seeds, and save/load preserves all resources and standing orders.
