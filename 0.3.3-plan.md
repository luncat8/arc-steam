# 0.3.3 — Infinite upper boundary

## Goal

Keep enough rock headroom above the ball and upper collapse front for the full
ball damage radius by feeding new rows smoothly from the top before the upper
chamber boundary is reached.

## Decisions

- Track the chamber's upper collapse boundary (exposed free faces plus
  contiguous collapse-ready rock above them) alongside the active balls'
  installed damage radii (`damageRadiusNow(owner) * cell`).
- Also look ahead along upward ball flights so deep shafts start replenishing
  before an approaching ball strikes near the roof.
- Ensure at least one full damage radius of rock headroom fits below `y = 15 m`
  whenever lower clearance above `minBottom = 4.5 m` (30% chamber height)
  allows growth.
- Share the single `queuedShift` / `stepGrowth` insertion pipeline with
  bounce-driven growth (0.3.2), topping up only the deficit above already
  queued rows so the two sources never double-count or conflict.
- Cap combined row addition strictly at 5 rows per second with smooth
  fractional motion, zero visual/collision jumps, exact chamber energy
  accounting and non-destructive clamping at the 70% height limit.

## Suggested implementation

- Add `upperBoundaryY()` and `watchBoundary(world)` in `js/ceiling.js`, invoked
  each simulation step before `stepGrowth(dt)` and inside `grow()`.
- Compute required shift from `upperBoundaryY + maxDamageRadius * cell - top`
  and upward-flying ball lookahead, then top up `queuedShift` only by any
  positive difference `neededShift - queuedShift`.
- Respect the shared 5 rows/s rate cap and the 30% remaining-height floor
  without destroying intact lower rock.
- Add unit tests covering headroom restoration for stock and upgraded strike
  balls, upward ball lookahead, strict 5 rows/s rate limiting, non-conflicting
  combination with bounce growth, 30% clearance gating and energy conservation.

## Acceptance

- New rows begin sliding in smoothly before a ball's damage radius clips the
  top boundary (`y = 15 m`).
- Combined bounce and boundary growth never exceeds 5 rows per second and does
  not double-add rows.
- No visual, collision or energy-ledger jump occurs, and intact lower rock is
  never destroyed when 30% chamber height remains.
