# 0.3.5 — Rockets and automatic purchase

## Goal

Add rockets as a purchasable consumable with automatic shop buying, and make
their explosions damage the ceiling with small fragments.

## Decisions

### Shop

- Rockets are a new resource.
- Checkbox enables automatic purchase.
- On each shop visit, if funds allow, buy up to 3 rockets.
- Never go negative.
- Show price, count and auto-buy state in the UI.

### Behaviour

- A rocket flies upward.
- Explosion height varies by about ±20%.
- With 5% probability the rocket fails to explode in air, falls back and
  explodes on ground contact.
- The explosion resembles a firework.
- Fragments scatter from the explosion point.
- Fragments interact with the ceiling and then fall as normal debris.
- Damage radius per fragment is about 1–4 px.

### Balance

- Rocket price yields roughly zero expected profit on cheap minerals.
- Profit appears with expensive minerals, good explosion placement, engine
  upgrades and efficient debris collection.

## Suggested implementation

- Add rocket resource, purchase logic and auto-buy checkbox.
- Add rocket entity and update loop.
- Reuse debris pooling for fragments.
- Add ceiling damage from fragments.
- Add tests for purchase cap, probability branches and damage.

## Acceptance

- Auto-buy respects funds and cap.
- Rocket behaviour matches the probability rules.
- Fragments damage and fall correctly.
